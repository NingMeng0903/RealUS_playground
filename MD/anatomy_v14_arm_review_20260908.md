# V14 connected left-arm independent review — 2026-09-08

This is an evidence-only review of `connected_left_arm_caps_v14`. It audits
the completed candidate export against raw142 on the same Genesis geometry
cells. It does not fit or tune the map, modify the renderer, or treat an
operationally complete artifact as an anatomical pass.

The machine-readable result is
[`v14_arm_audit_20260908_003/report.json`](../outputs/anatomy_retarget/v14_arm_audit_20260908_003/report.json),
generated by
[`audit_consistent_arm_v14.py`](../src/projects/genesis_ue_sync/anatomy_retarget/cli/audit_consistent_arm_v14.py).
The audit completed for subject `213328` in `tpose`, `pose_213328`, and
`heldout_sitting`. All three cells contain the same frozen topology and both
`raw142` and `candidate` geometry.

## Verdict

The candidate remains rejected for anatomical use. The connected map preserves
the selected elbow cap shapes almost rigidly in T-pose, but its station shift
does not preserve the relationship to the SMPL-X skin or to the complete
opposing bone surfaces. The failure is visible in the own capture pose and is
larger in heldout sitting, particularly in the wrist and hand branch.

The audit is operationally complete (`status=complete`), while
`anatomical_passed=false` and `publishable=false` remain explicit. The fit
artifact itself records `optimizer_success=false`,
`independent_validation_complete=false`, and `anatomical_passed=false`.

## Independent complete-mesh triangle checks

The new audit calls `surface_validation_v14.audit_bone_pair` on the complete
left Humerus/Radius, Humerus/Ulna, and Radius/Ulna meshes for both raw142 and
candidate.
Both meshes in every pair are watertight, consistently wound, and have valid
signed volume. VTK OBB collision catches triangle crossings independently of
the calibration sample points; bidirectional vertex and face-centroid signed
depths use a 0.5 mm tolerance.

| pose | geometry | Humerus–Radius | Humerus–Ulna | Radius–Ulna | result |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| T | raw142 | 52 | 147 | 83 | penetration exceeds tolerance |
| T | candidate | **53** | **143** | **83** | penetration exceeds tolerance |
| own `213328` | raw142 | 46 | 145 | 83 | penetration exceeds tolerance |
| own `213328` | candidate | **50** | **144** | **83** | penetration exceeds tolerance |
| heldout sitting | raw142 | 52 | 136 | 83 | penetration exceeds tolerance |
| heldout sitting | candidate | **54** | **140** | **83** | penetration exceeds tolerance |

The raw crossings show that the authored elbow geometry already has a local
baseline problem; preserving raw topology cannot count that problem as solved.
The Radius--Ulna pair has 83 triangle contacts in both raw142 and candidate in
all three cells, exposing the intrinsic forearm-pair overlap that a shared
rigid forearm field cannot remove. The candidate keeps the Humerus crossings
and changes their counts. The pair test is a
conservative contact/intersection diagnostic and cannot by itself establish
physiological joint contact, but it prevents a point-sample result from being
mistaken for a complete non-intersection proof.

## T-pose cap-shape preservation

For each frozen elbow validation cap, the candidate is aligned to the
subject-materialized raw142 geometry by a single rigid Procrustes transform.
Pairwise distances are then compared before and after the transform. The gates
are 0.1 mm RMS, 0.1 mm maximum residual, and 0.1 mm pairwise absolute p95.
These are **incremental** gates: they measure what the candidate map changes
after `materialize_subject(beta)` has already produced the subject geometry.

| cap | Procrustes RMS | max residual | pairwise p95 | shape gate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| left humerus | 0.000811 mm | 0.007535 mm | 0.000022 mm | pass |
| left radius | 0.000013 mm | 0.000064 mm | 0.000028 mm | pass |
| left ulna | 0.000009 mm | 0.000016 mm | 0.000017 mm | pass |
| right humerus | 0.000001 mm | 0.000003 mm | 0.000002 mm | pass |
| right radius | 0.000001 mm | 0.000003 mm | 0.000002 mm | pass |
| right ulna | 0.000001 mm | 0.000002 mm | 0.000002 mm | pass |

This is the useful part of the V14 construction: the cap itself is not being
scaled or thinned. The fitted left cap translation is approximately 9.07 mm
for the humerus domain and 14.63 mm for the radius/ulna domains. Those rigid
station changes explain why shape preservation passes while skin and opposing
bone relationships fail.

## Frozen-template shape limitation

The audit also compares both T-pose variants with the frozen
`operator.template_asset.vertices_rest`, without applying a gate. This is
needed because subject materialization can change cap shape before the
candidate map runs. The maximum pairwise-distance deltas for the audited
213328 validation caps are:

| cap | materialized raw142 vs frozen template | candidate vs frozen template | gate |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| left humerus | **0.418408 mm** | **0.418412 mm** | not applied |
| left radius | **0.443122 mm** | **0.443186 mm** | not applied |
| left ulna | **0.367494 mm** | **0.367492 mm** | not applied |

These values are above the user's 0.1 mm cap target before the candidate
transport is considered. Consequently, the passing table above does not prove
the total authored-142 cap error is ≤0.1 mm; it proves only the incremental
candidate-versus-materialized-raw142 preservation. The machine-readable
`tpose_cap_shape_vs_frozen_template` section records the full ungated
Procrustes and pairwise metrics and sets
`total_authored_142_gate_demonstrated=false`. An upstream operator preflight
also measured 213712 as exact zero for these three caps and a synthetic
PC1+1.5 body as 2.375436/2.384439/2.045453 mm (humerus/radius/ulna); those
figures are context for the materialization effect, not additional cells in
this audit.

## Root cause and handoff

The bounded fit moved the elbow and wrist stations with local axial fields,
then transported the wrist-descendant hand through one terminal response. That
is sufficient to preserve a small cap point set under a rigid comparison, but
it does not solve the simultaneous constraints of the SMPL-X skin boundary,
the humerus–radius/ulna articular surfaces, and all downstream hand branches.
The optimization's sampled population also under-represents those branches.
The result is a lower T-pose humerus maximum accompanied by more outside hand
vertices, followed by a strong own-pose/sitting regression.

The next fit should retain the cap-shape check and run the complete-mesh pair
checks and full connected-arm skin check on a frozen validation split before a
candidate is considered for any further stage. The new CLI accepts a direct
geometry NPZ or a directory, with `--operator`, `--calibration`, `--subjects`,
and `--poses` overrides, so the same audit can be applied to the next fit
without changing the renderer or the runtime.
