# Human-data confidence audit

Read 68 scans; processed 12588 images with fixed random-walk parameters. Per-frame CSV keeps quality, alignment coverage and configuration versions. Paired preview panels show image and confidence.

These data identify acoustic/torque disagreement; they do not establish mechanical separation truth or the causal efficacy of the new controller. Raw and already-aligned files are handled separately. No controller was connected, and no source data were changed.
