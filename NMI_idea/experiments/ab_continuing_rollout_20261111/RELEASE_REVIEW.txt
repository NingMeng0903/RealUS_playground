Sufficient attribution for this run
---------------------------------
Compare B_rollout_v4 with A_task_v4 on the same subjects and mirrored fixtures,
with the same fitted belief, public acceptance, option gate, physical checks,
legal recovery and checkpoint continuation. The B tail must use the same public
A_task_v4 controller. This isolates the implemented rollout override as a whole.
Keep A_info and the three complete-pair dwells as external strong comparators.
It does not independently isolate geometry, memory, anticipation or dwell modules.

Actual B diagnostics must distinguish switch, screened fallback, validation
fallback and upper-one pruning. If there is no switch, identical A/B trajectories
are expected under identical public prefixes; planner computation alone is not
evidence of benefit. At a switch, preserve the pre-action public history, base
and chosen actions, independent validation counts and subsequent actual outcomes.
A switch-conditioned success rate is descriptive, not a randomized subgroup test.

The renderer currently shares randomness for identical subject/action prefixes.
After policies diverge it need not supply the same packet. Subject-paired outcome
comparisons remain valid with equal marginal observation laws; do not describe
divergent continuations as identical-observation counterfactuals. The two mirrored
layouts share an object and are not independent subjects.

Unresolved/administratively stopped or computation-missing cases are not known
permanent failures. The pilot driver reports success bounds and paired descriptive
counts. No held-out significance conclusion is available from this development run.
High A success leaves little absolute room for improvement. Failure to improve
cannot be converted into a positive result or justified by weakening A.

Release follow-up — corrected source reviewed
---------------------------------------------
The blocking draft mismatches above have been closed in the inspected source.
Actual execution now checkpoints before the next fresh capture and preserves
pending retry, prescribed pair position, full actual state, evidence, public
decisions and private renderer mode. Both terminal rejections return to planning.
Native no-witness recovery is common to all arms and does not consume a frame;
executed invalid motion retains its actual exit. A public acceptance absorbs
regardless of the action label or the evaluator's private correctness label.

Rollout uses fitted response/observation parameters, one S/w draw per trajectory,
before-capture visibility transitions and conditional clutter. Only the simulator
and reward evaluator inspect the latent; the tail reads public packets, posterior
and proposed exits. The tail uses the same A_task scoring/tie rule and can recover
the full public action library at a later state. Selection and validation are
hash-separated; the latter consists of 32 new paired trajectories. The strict
S/U screen and discordant validation use unresolved A as a possible success.
The upper-one return now precedes other candidate simulations. Diagnostic
Hoeffding intervals are not used as an additional decision gate.

No further scientific-correctness blocker was found in this short source review.
Release the already specified grouped development pilot; do not add experiments
or thresholds. Root reports 30 passed / 2 skipped regressions and one successful
native B smoke. Those are engineering evidence, not group efficacy results.
The reported first B decision took about 65 seconds with 844 simulated captures;

First-checkpoint mechanism audit — before final-success inference
----------------------------------------------------------------
All 16 B initial RESULT.json records were available at inspection. Their logs
contain 37 rollout decision records: 21 screen_lower_not_above_baseline_upper,
16 baseline_upper_one_pruned, and zero actual rollout switches. These are logical
decision records, not 37 independent simulations: zero-history decisions may be
precomputed once and reused. For all 16 common A_task/B initial records, every
compared common-prefix actual action and public observation matched exactly.

In fixture 0, case 1, steps 3, 5 and 7 each have baseline S=0, F=0, U=16.
Each simulates 16*8=128 future packets, then skips the other 11 candidates.
The chosen actions are respectively side_right(.60), side_left(.60),
side_left(.60), identical to the base A choices at those public states.

This is a consequence of the frozen rule, not an unclosed implementation bug.
With S=F=0 and U=16, A's empirical completion envelope is [0,1]. No candidate
lower value can strictly exceed 1, so returning A is compulsory. This does not
mean A is predicted to succeed surely, nor that the model is confident in A.
It means the short rollout did not rule out success after its unresolved end.
The envelope is computed from finite model samples, not a statistical confidence
bound on the real mission probability.

I selected this conservative comparison to avoid counting tail truncation as
task failure. Its cost is now observable: it suppresses speed-only gains and
can suppress useful exploration precisely where A remains unresolved. This
implemented conservative finite-tail controller must not be presented as having
solved the user's full optimal autonomous probing/stopping problem. Zero switching
cannot show that hypothetical better probes are ineffective, since the upper-one
branch deliberately does not evaluate them.

In the unchanged no-deadline, zero-time-cost, reversible task, a longer route
primarily changes elapsed time. If the public route/dIRD proxy still supplies
the later exits, the future-executability term does not establish an irreversible
loss of completion opportunities. Actual selected native checking remains real;
the hypothetical proxy is not a continuous-motion certificate or a calibrated
whole-mission probability. The .90 score still concerns the local two-attempt
option, not the entire continuing task.

If the final group also contains zero switches, B's visited-state behavior is
the base A behavior plus extra planning work. More actual waiting/observations
then provide both policies the same recovery opportunities and cannot by
themselves create a B-specific benefit. This would be a negative result for
this implementation in this world, not proof that all task-aware probing methods
are useless. Strong-A ceiling, reversible environment and conservative screening
cannot be separately causally attributed from this one group. No new experiment,
rule, threshold, final-success rate or significance claim is introduced here.

Evaluator-only fixed-nominal-goal check for the same difficult object
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Independently recomputed from HARD_CASE_NOMINAL_GEOMETRY.json; this truth is not
available to the controller. All 12 diagnostic nominal poses fail the true
geometry gate for fixture 0, case 1. The nominal left terminal has true center
error 4.49703806161952 mm, only 0.00296193838048 mm inside the 4.5 mm gate.
At terminal dwell 1 s, center noise SD is 0.5*sqrt(.15)=0.19364916731037 mm.
Its conditional public center pass probability is
  Phi((4.5-4.49703806161952)/0.19364916731037)
    - Phi((-4.5-4.49703806161952)/0.19364916731037)
  = 0.50610173746667.
Quality/axis probabilities are effectively one here. Even with always-present
visibility and ignoring early false stops, per-frame correct completion is at
most p=.8*0.50610173746667=0.404881389973336. Independent two-frame completion
is therefore at most 1-(1-p)^2=0.645833839999932, below the common .90 gate.
The nominal right terminal's quality tail alone yields a two-frame upper bound
of approximately 3.84761702923205e-32, despite passing true geometry.

This fixed-pose calculation exposes a local-option versus mission distinction:
perfect knowledge cannot turn this particular two-frame option into a true .90
completion option. More diagnostic evidence alone cannot increase its known
physical observation success probability. An accurate common local gate can
therefore leave no nominal execution option to release, although a continuing
mission is a different probability question. This is not a claim that every
actual exit or every continuous motion is infeasible. The left goal is extremely
close to the threshold, so actual tracking/exit variations matter. Do not use
this private audit to select actions, change the shared gate or exclude the case.
