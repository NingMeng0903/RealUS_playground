# Continuation-specific B versus the shared replanning MPC

The comparison reuses the main sampled motion bank, synthetic joint acoustic kernel, 256-atom belief, confidence threshold of .87, and baseline controller from `ab_full_b_rollout_20261113` at commit `9eb12307d77fdd503006c42e640bce3fcc985126`. A continuation has a checked prefix followed by that exact MPC to termination. Both policies acquire a common time-zero anchor. The two decision conditions are immediately after that anchor and after two shared MPC commands. Episodes that stop or complete before a scheduled decision remain in their assigned denominator. Each continuation uses the same 65 mm TCP, 90° rotation, 250 mm rail and six-acquisition hard caps.

## Opportunity before prediction

Enumerate every checked single acquisition from the actual public state that has a full geometric witness for remaining task sites under the joint caps, assuming ideal acquisitions on the witness. Direct MPC is always included. The witness is mechanical and does not certify identity or MPC success. The two tasks are the original daughter sequence and single-trunk sequence. The same independent latent seeds are used across task and decision conditions. Within each case, retain the public prefix exactly, and independently redraw post-prefix clock/channel innovations for 24 paired repeats. All candidates see the same innovations at a given physical clock and return channel. The 96-case oracle namespace is disjoint from the training, development and final namespaces. The small pilot checks execution parity and runtime only.

Report three distinct quantities. Same-tape hindsight is a diagnostic ceiling within the tested library at common hard caps. A latent-informed selector uses 12 ranking tapes to choose each case's continuation and 12 separate tapes to measure its completed outcomes, then swaps the tape halves and pools the paired evaluation tapes. Report it with all legal actions and with a separate cost screen on ranking-tape means, with the three motion margins of ±2 mm TCP, ±2° rotation and ±0.10 s sampled motion duration. Neither diagnostic is available to the deployed controller. Report fixed-route and incumbent-fallback outcomes to identify which options drive the contrast. Bootstrap independent latent cases. A fixed-route report on its availability subset is descriptive and never enters a confirmatory denominator.

Before fitting, if the cost-screened latent-informed diagnostic has less than 3 percentage points completion gain or an interval reaching zero in either task, run one independent expansion to up to two checked acquisitions before returning to MPC. In the expansion, enumerate second acquisitions in the unresolved branch. After the first observation the continuation executes its checked second command if public task progress has not advanced, and hands off to MPC immediately if the claim advanced progress. The selected option never has access to the claim's true identity. This expands the feasible prefixes under the same caps, acoustic model and endpoint. Observe the same cost checks for the expanded library. Stop expanding the library after this comparison. The independent confirmation still tests a frozen public-only predictor, including all assigned cases, even if the diagnostic indicates a likely negative result.

In addition, run a cohort-budget diagnostic on both libraries. Within each ranking half, choose one continuation per latent case to maximize aggregate correct completion subject to the same three margins on the cohort mean predicted differences. Then measure the chosen set on the other tapes without any re-selection. This separates per-state cost screening from offsetting cost changes across states. It is label-assisted and uses the known diagnostic cohort. The independent tape evaluations and measured cost intervals still determine the interpretation. The cohort-average constraints are not prospective permission to use outcomes or the other arm's realized test costs.

## Direct prediction and prospective test

For public state \(h\) and legal continuation \(c\), the target is \(\Delta_c(h)=\mathbb E[Y_c-Y_{\mathrm{MPC}}\mid h]\), where each \(Y\) means two correct fresh identity claims and completion of the required scan. Training labels are paired differences from complete executions with repeated independent suffix noise. Keep each latent case, its tasks, decision states, candidates and repeats together when assessing transfer. Inputs compress the public posterior to moments, joint mass of prior-claim correctness, and per-candidate marginal predictive descriptors, together with observed packet features, task progress, physical state, remaining caps and checked geometry. Second-command likelihood descriptors refer to the initial belief at its prospective arrival clock. They do not condition on the first non-claim. Outcome labels, latent case seeds and unobserved noise enter only the evaluator and offline supervised labels.

The expanded library includes the single commands and both budgets are unchanged. Fit one case-weighted ridge predictor of paired completion, wrong identity and sampled motion differences on 192 training cases with 12 repeats, using a fixed ridge penalty of 24 after standardization and per-state candidate weighting. Assess transfer on 96 development cases with eight repeats. The per-state selector maximizes the raw predicted completion difference among continuations predicted within the inherited two-sided margins. The cohort selector instead uses fixed signed resource prices for the three motion metrics. Fit these prices by the linear-programming dual of maximizing predicted cohort success at half of the inherited margins, using only the development public predictions. The half margins reserve slack for prediction error. Direct MPC is exactly zero and wins ties in either selection rule. A no-cost-price selector is a hard-cap diagnostic. There is no success or uncertainty admission threshold. Use a task/depth fixed continuation from training as a comparator for public conditioning. Measure held-out paired prediction error and realized decision value against that comparator.

The primary prospective comparison is the cohort-priced B versus MPC in each prespecified task/checkpoint cell. The local screen, fixed route, and hard-cap-only ranker are diagnostics. Freeze prices, feature encoding, library, namespaces, files and case count before confirmation. Run one prospectively drawn tape for each of 512 new independent latent cases, with no test selection or parameter updates. Record new packets, belief-state hashes, chosen prefixes, exact MPC commands, stopping, route, elapsed acquisition time, claims, evaluator identities, and budget consumption in each arm. Record prediction and feature-generation CPU separately from acoustic time.

Report all-case paired completion, any wrong identity, wrong identity subtype, rail use, captures and actual sampled motion costs. Statistical units are independent case seeds. Use the prior point threshold of +5 percentage points with paired 95% lower limit above zero, exact one-sided 97.5% upper bound for harmful wrong-identity discordance at most .02, and the simultaneous three-motion-cost equivalence limits. Report cost nonincrease separately. Include measured compute in a separate time ledger because the acoustic simulator does not advance the object during processor time. A shared fixed anatomical chart, centroid observations and sampled kinematics bound the claim.
