Result-unit and evidence-scope notes

response_identification/
Normalized response/control identifiability mechanism. Its success rates do
not use the geometric ultrasound or native robot acquisition acceptance gate.

response_learning_geometry/ original benchmark
One numerical response/action unit was 1mm. The original task used a .50mm
centering threshold; all methods passed. The first typed model was manual,
not an e3nn-library implementation. Original FD values contain a diagnostic
array-broadcast bug and are superseded by the corrected addendum.

response_learning_geometry/geometry_addendum/
positive_runs and negative_runs use the original 1-unit=1mm prediction scale.
task_scale_rows/task_scale_id_rows reuse normalized models at 1 unit=10mm.
Their keys containing normalized are normalized lengths: multiply by10 to
obtain mm, including mean regret and actual/oracle centering error. For
example e3nn .000128125 normalized is .00128125mm, not .000128125mm.
The success threshold in those task-scale rows is2mm. Those are separate
post-initial-benchmark test seeds, not a retrospective rewrite of the .50mm
result. Artifact names marked OOD describe the probe-R sampling change; see
FINAL_REPORT for the isotropic-input limit of that generalization claim.

geometry_addendum/context_ablation/
Fields ending _mm are physically converted using10mm per unit. Raw error
arrays remain normalized; analyze_evidence.py multiplies by10 before computing
component RMSE. The512 contexts are independent sampling units; their32
candidates and three task readouts are correlated, not16384/49152 independent
objects. Bootstrap intervals condition on the frozen trained model.

harmonic_mechanism/
Positions are metre-based production mesh coordinates, angles are degrees in
summary fields, and material theta/h/d are mixed-unit scalar labels. An equal
float32 API label is not infinite numerical accuracy or true patient
correspondence. The normal counterexample uses sampled local planes inside
native cells, not measured ultrasound-probe normals.

inference/proposal_dev_28d/
mean_capped_NLL is a per-frame marginal heldout predictive score under the
declared geometric sensor, not a joint5-frame density or a clinical image
quality score. The score cap is evaluation-only, not a likelihood floor.
evidence_estimate in the proposal diagnostics is log Z, a Monte Carlo log
normalizer estimate. ESS after resampling is not posterior coverage. The
2conditions/prefixes share one independent DEV world and must not be counted
as four subjects. Forked public-stop checks are diagnostic, not a complete
online first-image-acquisition experiment with a native controller.

response_identification/b_decision_mechanism/
L=1, observation c=.1 and execution Delta are normalized toy costs. They are
not seconds or directly measured robot joint effort. Cheap threshold and
Bellman agree by design and independent enumeration; no novel algorithmic
superiority follows.
