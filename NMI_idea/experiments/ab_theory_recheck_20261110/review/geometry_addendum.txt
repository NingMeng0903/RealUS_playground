Independent method and numerical results
geometry_addendum_check.py selects the first five already frozen native cells
per leg, without filtering, and checks the canonical/0deg/90deg embeddings.
Ten cells yield20 cell-pose pairs; the remaining all256×7 summaries are only
recounted from the saved raw arrays, not described as a new geometry rerun.

For independent coordinates b=(b1,b2,b3), b0=1-sum(b), define
S=sum_i bi sin(theta_i), C=sum_i bi cos(theta_i). The local circular derivative
is dtheta/dbj=[C(sin(theta_j)-sin(theta_0))
             -S(cos(theta_j)-cos(theta_0))]/(S²+C²).
Rows for h,d are vertex differences from vertex0. This is exactly the code's
Kb, in the API's (theta,h,d) order. Central differences of direct double
atan2/barycentric label interpolation agree: max absolute error1.64e-11.
Local angular differences are wrapped; this does not make a scalar modulo
theta globally differentiable at its seam.

Let X0/Xp contain the three physical cell edge columns. Then F=Xp X0^-1 and
J=Xp Kb^-1 are correct where both inverses exist. Finite-difference J relative
error is4.08e-10. For a Cartesian quadratic test energy, the independently
differenced label gradient agrees with J^T e to relative1.79e-8.

The physical metric is J^T J. The code's physical natural-gradient direction
-J (J^T J)^-1 J^T e equals -e in this full-rank three-dimensional local chart.
Maximum Cartesian difference and difference after diag(.1,10,2) relabeling
are2.45e-15. This is a verified pullback identity, not a new optimization or
learning theorem; the strong Cartesian baseline is necessarily equal.

Independently constructed two tangent vectors give
cross(Fv,Fu)/det(F)=F^-T cross(v,u) to3.34e-16. The ordinary tetrahedral
barycentric position baseline agrees to2.23e-16m. Public query_atlas_coordinates
locates transported points without receiving the originating cell; its outputs
match across poses at saved float32 precision. Their maximum difference from
direct double label interpolation is2.20e-7. Therefore “all label errors zero”
means stored/public numerical equality, not infinite accuracy or patient truth.

Attribution limits that must remain in synthesis
1. The label-preservation result is expected from a fixed material mesh,
   unchanged scalar fields and shared barycentric correspondence. It checks
   the production query interface; ordinary tet correspondence also passes.
   It is not evidence that harmonic coordinates uniquely recover anatomy.
2. These assets have graph-harmonic d and transported h/theta labels. Pose
   transport does not re-solve harmonicity in the new metric. Complete surface
   contact_multichart is a different d=0 construction, not this deep volume.
3. The normal/tangent pair is a random local plane, not an observed skin or
   ultrasound plane. Fn is a deliberately incorrect type transformation.
   Its angular error is a mechanism counterexample, not an estimated patient,
   probe or controller error. Frozen-tangent angles use oriented random
   vectors; an unoriented vessel-axis metric would require sign invariance.
4. Frozen world positions ignore known leg motion. Their large displacement
   is a useful sanity contrast, not the error of a capable registered baseline.
5. The raw-coordinate-gradient contrast deliberately changes label scales;
   it establishes why a physical metric is needed. Cartesian and ordinary
   properly scaled local-coordinate methods remain strong equal controls.
6. F is exact inside the declared piecewise-affine tet interpolation of cached
   LBS vertices. It is not a measured human deformation gradient. Seven finite
   poses do not prove arbitrary shape/pose injectivity, tissue mechanics or
   a global continuous motion guarantee. Prior asset validation retains its
   own scope; this small formula audit does not replace it.

No blocking formula or hash error was found. The correct failure-to-success
statement is “incorrect coordinate/type handling fails; the verified physical
transport/metric interface works on these assets.” It must not be rewritten as
“ordinary correspondence fails; harmonic/e3nn uniquely succeeds.”
