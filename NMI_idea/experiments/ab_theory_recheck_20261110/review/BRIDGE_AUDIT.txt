The input/label separation is correct within this declared experiment: the
network gets s,u,n,t,R and its frozen parameters; generated q_true is used only
to score. However n/t are known material-coordinate directions, not inferred

a hidden blood vessel. No probe-origin/contact/controller loop is exercised.

Why correct rows are identical across legs and poses
make_actions() generates ONE set of256 scalar contexts and32 local actions,
outside the leg/pose loops. Those same draws are reused for both legs and all
seven poses. More strongly, constructing Rx=t and Rz=-n makes
R^T t=e1 and R^T n=-e3 for every correctly transported cell. Thus all correct
rows reduce to the same256 synthetic probe-local response problems, differing
only in their world embedding and small floating-point rounding.

This is a legitimate paired coordinate/transport mechanism test. It is NOT
512 independent subjects,3584 independent learning tests, or new evidence
that compliance coefficients transfer under nonrigid tissue deformation.
Scalar coefficients are held fixed by the synthetic law; no stretch-dependent
material coefficient is inferred or transported. e3nn's common SO(3) output
law does not provide a general nonrigid response theorem.

Ordinary-tet baseline and attribution
The implemented ordinary-tet check compares positions, not a separately
executed full response baseline. With the SAME material directions/labels
and vertices, ordinary tetrahedral F gives exactly the same transported
n,t,R and therefore identical frozen-network input/prediction. The spot check
confirms this shared F construction. Nothing here isolates a unique benefit
of solving harmonic d versus another valid common material correspondence.
The domain remains the audited h=.14–.94 annular shaft, not full-leg tissue.
