Final bounded independent module audit — 2026-11-11

Decision
The reviewed proposal density/importance/MH interfaces and corrected trained
response derivatives pass the bounded checks described below. Publish them
as separate numerical mechanisms with their negative results. They do not
release the locked 720913 cohort, establish learned-response necessity,
validate full B, or demonstrate physical/ultrasound acquisition. No training,
native trajectory, or locked-cohort instantiation was performed in this audit.
Existing reports and source files were not modified.

The source calls logmeanexp(logw) “evidence_estimate”; this is a log-evidence

distinct mutated geometries and MH acceptance do not imply independent
posterior draws. A prior mixture preserves mathematical support, not a useful
finite-budget chance of discovering all relevant modes.

2. Corrected response-learning experiment
Source inspected:
response_learning_geometry/geometry_addendum/run_corrected.py
SHA8847cf71d435dec087e8039ffaac23e062d549b57d9e3c563c34d805b15effc1
This actually imports vendor e3nn0.6.0 and calls ElementwiseTensorProduct
1o x1o->0e and0e x1o->1o. The first manual-basis benchmark remains a different,
superseded implementation, not retroactively called e3nn.

The corrected MLPs receive dyadic tt^T; the typed model's apparent signed-t
input enters only sign-invariant contractions. Thus the relevant unoriented
information is shared. Both the canonical MLP and typed model first express
geometry in the probe frame and rotate output back. Consequently canonical
normalization already guarantees common-world SO(3) consistency, up to
numerics; the result cannot attribute that consistency uniquely to e3nn.

The typed output basis is u, n(n·u), t(t·u), the SAME declared basis as the
synthetic plant. Its advantage is a strong correct structural prior plus
learned coefficients. Actual e3nn contractions implement that prior, but this
is not evidence that generic e3nn is uniquely necessary or that the response
law is independently established for human tissue. Public compliance/depth/
history descriptors, normal and vessel axis are supplied synthetic inputs.
They are not inferred here from the real ultrasound or production atlas.

3. Claims suitable for the final synthesis
Allowed: correct normalized public-conditioned importance sampling; explicit
finite-sample degeneracy and failed terminal outcomes; actual vendor-e3nn
contractions and derivative contracts; synthetic response fitting under a
matched structural basis; smaller chosen-action regret in that declared law;
correct null-control behavior; common-coordinate consistency already matched
by a canonical ordinary network; separately verified production geometric
transport/metric interfaces from the prior geometry audit.

Not supported: learning/e3nn necessity, a general failure-to-success result
against strong baselines, new POMDP/VOI theory, recovered continuous-patient
posterior coverage, complete A/B superiority, integration of these separate
modules into the full anatomy/ultrasound/control chain, or physical ultrasound
evidence. B remains a research direction to test after a valid belief and
response interface, not a result established by these module checks.
