Public-history informed proposal DEV record (corrected 28D)
============================================================

Scope and provenance
--------------------
This is a DEV-only retrospective comparison.  It used
``sample_prior(720901, 128)`` and the first ``sample_worlds(720902, 1)``
object, with the exact shared-history keys ``(0,'iid','dev')`` and
``(0,'shifted','dev')``.  Prefixes 5 and 8 were evaluated.  No 720913 data
were opened.  Predictive and terminal fields below are diagnostic forks and
never enter q, the importance weights, or the MH target.

The root task text originally called this vector 30D, but
2+10+10+2+2+2=28.  The current implementation is therefore the physical 28D
vector, with no auxiliary coordinates.  The pre-correction 30D implementation
and its DEV record are preserved unchanged under
``before_dimension_typo_fix/`` for audit transparency; they are not current
results.

Current informed proposal
-------------------------
epsilon=.1, 16 discrete modes, 8 public-only association patterns
(seed 720907), N=128, full public-history likelihood, normalized 28D q, and
one importance update per prefix.  The optional ``+MH8`` rows apply a
standard beta=1 full-history resample--move: resample only when ordinary ESS
is below .60N, then 8 symmetric blocked MH sweeps with scales
``[1,.5,1.8]``.  The target is normalized prior plus complete public-history
likelihood; q, old importance weights, GT, and task reward are not in the
acceptance ratio.

Every pre-MH informed run has raw ESS=1 and max weight=1.  This is genuine
importance-weight degeneracy, not a success claim.  Because ESS=1, all four
MH rows resampled; their post-MH ESS is 128 by construction.  The resulting
unique-geometry counts show movement, but do not establish posterior
coverage or independent samples.

The wall times are from one CPU rerun on the shared DEV environment and are
not a performance claim; the ``+MH8`` value includes proposal construction,
importance evaluation, and rejuvenation.  ``finite LL`` counts finite public
likelihood evaluations, while ``finite logw`` additionally applies the
normalized prior support (the shifted prefix-8 distinction is intentional).

For context, the fixed128 baseline predictive NLLs on the same public
histories were 69.3811, 84.5028, 337.2284, and 349.9118 for iid/5, iid/8,
shifted/5, and shifted/8 respectively; all public terminal flags were false.
The informed proposal plus MH improved the diagnostic predictive score in all
four rows relative to the corresponding informed-only row, and also removed
the zero-predictive-support diagnostic seen in shifted prefix 5 after MH.  It
did not achieve a public terminal stop, and it is not a validated A/B task
improvement.  In particular, the shifted rows remain far from a claim of
reliable support recovery.

Recorded contracts and limitations
-----------------------------------
The implementation records finite likelihood/log-weight counts, raw ESS,
maximum weight, evidence estimate, component/pattern hashes, resampling,
MH acceptance, post-MH finite-target count, and unique geometries.  A fully
zero-likelihood update is returned as ``posterior_valid=false`` with explicit
``zero_likelihood_support``; no likelihood floor, dropped block, old-weight
multiplication, or fake posterior is created.  MH movement after a collapsed
ESS=1 resample is diagnostic target-preserving rejuvenation, not evidence of
coverage.  The separate frozen SMC engine and its beta=0 regression remain
outside this proposal task.

Reproduction artifact
---------------------
``run_proposal_dev.py`` is the source-pinned DEV driver.  It writes
``proposal_dev_28d/RESULTS.json`` and ``proposal_dev_28d/RAW.npz`` with the
public history, audit-only scoring forks, normalized log-p/log-q/log-LL
arrays, weights, diagnostics, selected poses, seeds, command, and source
hashes.  The predictive values and terminal flags are the reproducibility
quantities; wall-clock values can vary between reruns and are not an
acceptance metric.  The existing table above remains the recorded report and
is not overwritten by the driver.
