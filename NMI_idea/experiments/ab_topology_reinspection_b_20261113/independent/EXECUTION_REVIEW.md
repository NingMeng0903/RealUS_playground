# Independent implementation and execution review

## Shared physical and observation interface

The benchmark uses the 30 previously checked nodes at five material stations and the 264 inherited directed or hold edges between them. I replayed all 20,064 joint/FK samples and reconstructed TCP length, cumulative rotation, rail travel and maximum joint-rate fractions. The maximum discrepancies are zero for TCP and rail, 6.4e−14° for rotation and 3.4e−16 for the rate fraction. All recorded edge clearance and limit checks pass their archived thresholds. The inherited tests cover sampled robot self-collision and kinematics. This study does not exercise contact forces, body collision, real B-mode frames or native control.

The common prescan executes five real synthetic captures at stations 7→3. It uses receding two-view optimization within that fixed material-station order. Twelve public model scenarios share their latent hypothesis between the two hypothetical views. At each step it executes the first view, observes the actual packet and reoptimizes. It pays 1.5 s of sampled motion and a common 0.1 s allotment per capture. All 324 complete checked paths in the prescribed order fit the declared 142 mm TCP, 114° rotation and 401 mm rail prefix envelope. The separate suffix limits are 65 mm, 90°, 250 mm and eight additional acquisitions. Reported end-to-end costs sum the paid prefix and suffix. There is no shared evidence credit from the prefix toward the fresh pair.

An independent 256-case draw for each nominal, stress and held-shape setting confirmed finite normalized sensor distributions, side convention at the two target sites, the sampled fork within its declared interval, and fixed motion costs across cases. Sensor packets are quantized multisets of up to three centroid detections. Equal bins preserve their multiplicity and carry no stable source ordering. Coincident independent centroid returns remain an approximation to segmentation of overlapping structures. Negative-world anatomical families are single, parallel and depth-separated projection crossing, with a persistent artificial return and time-varying outages across all families. Held shape changes the daughter and ghost shapes together with increased nuisance variation.

## Public decisions and fresh endpoint

All main comparison arms use the same motion graph, legal limits, exact same synthetic sensor packets at the same physical site and arrival clock, and full posterior over the same prior particles. The nearest-return arm is a deliberate suffix observation ablation. It retains the same full prescan posterior and sees a deterministic projection of each new identical underlying packet. An acquisition may only advance through a checked edge, and every policy runs until an explicit declaration or abstention. The two-step MPC searches all legal fixed first/second actions on public model samples and replans after real packets. Longer belief rollout ranks first moves using the public tail approximation and runs a greedy belief-updating tail to terminal stop on sampled persistent prior cases. This is a finite rollout baseline with ten first moves and twelve scenarios, without a proof of optimality.

A positive success requires the correct fork interval and side, fork location within 5 mm, and two newly executed consecutive images at the ordered required stations. Each selected slot must be the same connected daughter by evaluator-only component truth. The last available ordered pair can be retained through later acquisitions. A correctly classified negative is a declaration of no qualifying daughter. The endpoint does not distinguish the three negative graph subfamilies in the terminal declaration, although their truth families are retained for stratified readout. `wrong` includes false absence on positive cases. `affirmative_error` identifies a wrong claimed daughter, wrong pair or inadequate localization. Real packets, duplicate-bin tie permutations and the evaluator's accepted slots reproduce independently.

The final terminal verifier evaluates every distinct slot pair, then maximizes the joint identity and localization mass in a fork-coordinate window 10 mm wide. It uses a running sum over sorted public prior particles. Independent enumeration of possible interval starts checked 300 random posterior states and all 1,200 positive-label decisions. Both the scores and the masses obtained by the chosen centers agree to 1e−12. The earlier development logs used a posterior mean point estimate and retain their own archived audit.

The planner action functions read only belief, accepted public evidence, checked motion data, action budgets and model forecasts. Learned policies also receive the same full prior-particle posterior plus public position, resource, packet and witness features. The recurrent policy uses a GRU warmed on common prescan history and continues its state across suffix decisions. This is a test of adding learned memory to explicit Bayesian state, while a separate XOR fixture demonstrates recurrence through a delayed information requirement.

## Replay results and interpretation

- `BANK_REPLAY.json` verifies the bank and independent cost ledger. `WORLD_PREFLIGHT.json` and `OBSERVATION_PREFLIGHT.json` record the independent geometry and exact finite-packet preflights.
- `SMOKE_PRESCAN2_AUDIT.json` reconstructs the two-step prescan smoke across 12 cases and five arms before the terminal-window revision. The associated nominal development replay in `DEVELOPMENT_AUDIT.json` covers 96 cases, six arms and 1,964 suffix packets. It reproduces all summary counts, paired completion intervals, simultaneous cost intervals and harmful-discordance bounds. All cases stay in the denominators.
- `FINAL_DEVELOPMENT_AUDIT.json` uses the revised terminal window on 96 cases, eleven arms and 3,872 suffix packets. The common prescan produces 37 distinct view sequences. Its posterior-only MAP readouts score 78/96 binary presence, 67/96 exact five-class task label, 35/48 conditional positive fork/side label and 71/96 four-family topology. These readouts use evaluator truth only for scoring and do not qualify as fresh completion. After the prescan, MPC acquired the required consecutive sites in 37/48 positive cases, both correct daughter returns were present in 29/48, and 22/48 completed. Eleven cases did not visit the required consecutive sites, eight lacked the two daughter returns despite visiting, three abstained with the returns available, two declared the wrong interval or side, one chose a wrong slot and one missed the localization tolerance. On the same nearest-control paths, five positive cases contained both daughter returns in the full packets while the compressed packets retained none.
- `DEVELOPMENT_REACHABILITY.json` independently enumerates nondominated joint-cap states for the final development cohort. Every positive case has a mechanically legal ordered route. On each case's fixed tape, there is at least one path containing both daughter returns. This evaluator has future tape and identity access. It separates feasibility from actual policy results and supplies no online performance estimate.
- `memory_sanity/SUMMARY.json` records three trained recurrent PPO seeds reaching 128/128 and three matched feed-forward seeds reaching 64/128 in a balanced independent XOR test. One bit is available only in the first prefix frame, the other only in the first suffix observation, and the decision frame is identical for all four hidden patterns.

Actual sampled TCP, rotation, rail and clock have separate two-sided equivalence and upper-cost margin tests. Solver-call wall time and complete neural batched evaluation wall time are distinct measurement scopes. Planning can take longer than the common allotment on a given machine; the synthetic clock does not use measured compute delay. Completion gains should be reported with the affirmative harm and tradeoff metrics, including both the positive-target and negative subsets.
