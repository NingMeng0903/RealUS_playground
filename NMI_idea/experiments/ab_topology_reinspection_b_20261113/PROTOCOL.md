# Active reinspection of uncertain vessel connections

This is a simulation screen of local branch finding and fresh inspection. The task starts with a sampled hidden structure and a shared ordered coverage scan. The controller knows the material chart and checked probe moves. It observes unordered image-plane centroid candidates with miss, clutter and persistent artifact processes.

## Environment and endpoint

The four scene families are a connected side offshoot, a single trunk, disconnected parallel vessels and a projection crossing of disconnected vessels. Positive and negative scenes each occupy half the cohort. A positive branch originates at a continuously sampled location in either of two local intervals and extends to either material-lateral side. We score the discovered connected side offshoot. The simulator exposes no truth node names, component identities or case-dependent legal moves. The shape stress uses a new curved side path and persistent artifact as well as larger nuisance variability.

The nominal atlas and old-ID checked motion bank are shared across all cases. The prior consists of 256 disjoint scene draws and sums all associations for up to three binned returns on the 5×3 image grid. Continuous truth is drawn separately. An exact nested nearest control receives a deterministic compression of the same packets after the common scan and marginalizes its corresponding likelihood.

The common coverage policy executes five captures at stations 7 through 3, selecting views with a two-capture receding lookahead over checked paths. It updates the posterior after each actual packet. It gives the same observations, robot configuration, clock and posterior to all policies. Its route envelope is 142 mm TCP, 114° turn and 401 mm rail. This covers all 324 checked ordered routes. After the shared scan each policy can execute up to eight more checked acquisitions under 65 mm TCP, 90° turn and 250 mm rail limits. Total costs include the common scan. Each move uses the archived 1.50 s sampled motion and a common 0.10 s simulated decision allowance. Wall compute is recorded separately. This environment uses centroid likelihoods and fixed-surface checked sampled robot paths.

A positive completion requires the correct side and fork interval, a fork estimate within 5 mm of truth, and two successive new acquisitions at the correct adjacent distal stations. Both chosen frame slots must be the same real daughter. Prescan frames update the belief but cannot satisfy this requirement. A negative completion requires declaring no qualifying daughter. Abstentions and all assigned cases remain in the denominators. We separately count positive completion, no-target specificity, erroneous affirmative declarations and all wrong declarations.

The common commit scorer marginalizes the joint event of fork class, localized fork and both frame-slot identities. Its fork estimate is the center of a width-10 mm interval of maximum joint posterior mass. Public stop utility is success probability minus 1.5 times error probability. Training uses realized terminal utility and the same cost prices, with potential differences that cancel in complete undiscounted episodes.

## Controllers and training

- Full MPC evaluates checked one- and two-capture fixed motion sequences with 12 common prior and observation samples and a shared feasible-route terminal potential. It executes the selected first acquisition, incorporates the actual packet and replans through task termination.
- Full continuation rollout samples 12 public-model worlds per root move and simulates a potential-guided receding policy through the remaining budget and stop. It considers ten root moves ranked by the shared one-step potential. Each simulated trajectory retains the same latent scene draw throughout. The online policy chooses one acquisition and replans on its actual observation.
- The secondary route-planning B enumerates checked motion paths to both required ordered fresh pairs and filters each using its complete vector of resource costs. It ranks feasible route proposals by expected probability of both true offshoot detections, considering the three top proposals for each pair and first move. It uses 16 simulated joint scenes to score each shortlisted complete route with the public terminal scorer and actual route cost, and compares the result with all one-step moves and stopping. It executes only the first acquisition and replans. The detection proxy is used for ranking. It does not certify a route's outcome or bound the full policy value.
- The one-step and Fisher controls share the commit and route-potential scoring. Fisher adds conditional geometric information about fork position under a branch label. The prescan-only control evaluates the negative/abstention decision at the exit.
- The two actor architectures share public posterior, packet, geometry, state and resource features. The recurrent architecture carries its state from the initial scan through every new observation. Pure behavior cloning uses the same 1,536 checked MPC teacher episodes for both architectures, one disturbance tape each, 32 supervised epochs. Initialized PPO uses 240,000 interactive decisions per seed and new suffix disturbance draws on repeats of the training scene pool. The CQL reference fits a fixed greedy/random replay with 240,000 retained decisions. Its replay differs from the demonstrations and it uses no recurrent state. This is a bounded comparison of the tested learners.

## Development and held-out comparisons

Development smoke and early uninitialized 64,000-interaction runs used earlier code. Those files retain their settings as development records. The fixed evaluation uses the two-step initial scan and shared interval decision rule. The seed-7 trained checkpoints are the primary learned instances by fixed order. Seeds 29 and 61 measure training sensitivity. Checkpoints are selected using development utility and frozen before the primary results. Source and checkpoint hashes are saved with the configurations.

The primary cohort contains 256 new scenes, including 128 Y scenes. The additional 256-scene cohort uses the held-out curved daughter and artifact family and stronger nuisance variation. The evaluation pairs each policy on the same anatomy and noise keyed by physical acquisition time and material station. Both cohorts report positive and negative counts and actual motions as well as pooled results. The principal contrasts are learned recurrent B and complete continuation rollout against full all-return MPC. Route-planning B is a secondary alternative added after the development audit and before opening either cohort. Nearest, Fisher, MLP PPO and pure cloning are diagnostic controls. Separate seed results are training sensitivity on the same cases.
