Acoustic likelihood experiment
2026-11-12

The 28-station first sweep is identical between methods within each case and
acoustic condition. In recheck, all arms acquire two frames at material sites
4 and 3. Centered views 3, 7 and 8 can be used when the existing baked edge
checker admits entry and a continuation to the next central view. The stage
clock is fixed before observation. It includes a conservative translation and
rotation allowance based on the baked geometry and nominal modeled speeds.
This does not certify continuous joint tracking or contact control.

Train from 256 new synthetic subjects and common exposures across views.
The only fitted observation component is false-structure probability and
location. Simulator labels select false centroids in training. The pooled
and view-conditioned likelihoods have identical mixture structure, with
components tied to each projected branch plus a uniform background. The
view-conditioned fit shrinks towards the pooled parameters. No labels or
private case variables are read by the filters and two-step planners.

Primary closed-loop comparison is view-conditioned versus pooled fitting for
two correct branch identities and strict completion (both correct and the
same 3 mm map error threshold). Report confident wrong identity and abstention
separately. Shared-frame predictive NLL compares learned replay to nominal
on the nominal planner's exact recheck frames, with prediction scored before
assimilation. Replay uses the same fitted model as the learned planner and
separates observation interpretation from the effect of new views. Confidence
can use historical witnesses; this is identity rechecking, not an independent
test of reacquiring a currently lost target.

128 held-out cases are paired in each acoustic condition. The original
simulator is evaluated without changes. The structural stress only changes
false detections' locations, preserving actual target observations, timestamps
and false-detection counts for each queried action. Near ghosts are displaced
by a smooth subject-specific physical-time signal rotated with view, with
approximately matched marginal scale. This introduces temporal and view
dependence without changing target visibility. All cases are included.
Train only on original simulator records; there is no fitting to the stress
results. Report actual modeled translation, accumulated rotation and paired
case bootstrap intervals. Source, split and model settings are recorded.
