**Independent verdict: this experiment does not support the proposed central T-RO claim.** Contingent B provides no observed improvement over the strong online MPC baseline. Online material updating changes some outcomes, but its comparison with frozen B fails the preregistered success and wrong-confirm gates. This is a completed negative result under the frozen protocol, not a reason to extend the cohort or select another case.

Online B and online MPC select the same first and final actions, acquire the same masks, make the same claims and incur the same costs in every paired case. Their observed success difference is zero. The empirical paired bootstrap interval is consequently [0, 0]; this describes the observed identical decisions and does not establish universal equivalence on untested cases.

Online B versus frozen B gives +4.6875 percentage points, with 8 wins and 2 losses. The subject-cluster paired bootstrap interval is [0, +9.375] points; the exact paired binary test within either reserve is p=.109375. I calculated this bootstrap distribution by exact convolution of the empirical subject differences, averaging the reserves before resampling. The estimate is below the preregistered +5-point requirement and its lower bound is not positive. Wrong confirmations increase by +1.5625 points, with a 97.5% upper bound of +4.6875 points, exceeding the allowed +2 points. Every wrong confirmation is clutter, not the trunk. Thus the map-update contrast supplies a modest, uncertain completion signal without the required risk result.

The reserve intervention changes the off-policy action graph, but never changes a realized decision. All three arms choose center view 3 first in every case. That exit preserves all three tails under both the 10-mm and 26-mm cumulative lateral-stroke limits. Therefore the observed reserve interaction is exactly zero and the claimed exit-reserve mechanism was not activated by the selected policies. Prospective B predictions contain multiple preferred tails in 72/128 online cases, but their mean option-value gap is only 0.000265 utility units. After a real new intermediate mask, B changes from its corresponding planned fixed tail in 16 cases; MPC makes exactly the same 16 changes. Thirteen of these cases succeed and one wrongly confirms clutter. These observations demonstrate shared replanning behavior, not an incremental benefit of valuing contingent tails before the observation; they do not estimate the causal benefit of switching against an unexecuted fixed tail.

The prespecified sensitivity subset contains the daughter in the common probe in 96/128 subjects; none of the common probes is empty. Within this subset, successes are 56/96, 56/96 and 51/96, respectively, with 13 wrong confirmations in each arm. Online B versus frozen B is +5.208 points, interval [0, +10.417], with 6 wins and 1 loss. B still ties MPC, and the lower bound is not positive. This conditional analysis cannot replace the all-case primary or establish that target presence made the probe informative.

Scope is the frozen moving-Y atlas, synthetic endpoint centroid masks, sampled fixed-rail kinematic execution, and an ideal synchronized low-level knee-pose signal. The confidence and outcome are for a newly generated terminal mask, not measured ultrasound image quality. The reserve limits are explicitly imposed cumulative simulated stroke resources, not measured native joint-history or tissue-contact reserves. The bounded fixed-15-degree native preflight found no state meeting the declared narrow eligibility rule; it therefore supplied no qualifying native pair for this holdout. There is no physical-robot, force-control or moving-patient validation here.

The supported attribution is limited: online map updating can change terminal choices or associations at comparable simulated cost; ordinary MPC replanning realizes the same actions as contingent B in this cohort. This study establishes neither a distinct B advantage nor the claimed combination of online map benefit and reserve-aware contingent continuation required for the central T-RO contribution.
