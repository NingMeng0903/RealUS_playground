# Moving-Y continuation study

## Paired design

The cohort comprises 128 prospectively generated independent case seeds under the existing combined `MapWorld(mode='stress')` suite. The fixed seed rule is `SeedSequence([2026111217, i])` for indices 0–127. Smoke seeds use base `2026111290` and are excluded. Run each seed once in each of three arms under both simulated reserve settings. The paired unit is the seed, with both reserve observations kept together when a paired statistic pools conditions. All cases enter the primary analysis. The independent evaluator also reports the sensitivity subset with a true daughter centroid present in the shared probe, plus counts of empty and other probe outcomes. Neither subset membership nor simulator labels enter a planner, filter, or choice of cases.

All six runs receive the same 28-frame inherited material-map rule sweep, followed by one byte-identical extra probe. The geometry-only common probe is site 3, center view 3, with both nominal lumens inside the 50 mm acoustic aperture and the 3–52 mm depth gate over the preflight pose grid. The candidate and probe addresses are fixed before cohort outcomes. The three arms are online material-map plus contingent B, online material-map plus stochastic open-loop MPC, and prefix-frozen material-map plus contingent B. The frozen arm snapshots the complete static marginal immediately after frame 28 and before the common probe. New masks continue updating conditional lag and identity in that arm. Each suffix uses one local correction/recheck at site 3 and one final daughter continuation at site 4. Each address admits the existing −8, 0, and +8 mm views before the common mechanics and stroke gates. No early stop or alternate branch is available.

## Actuation and reserve model

The first sweep inherits the source acquisition clock and contact sequence. The timed joint-reference experiment begins at its last contact and preserves the simulated exit q continuously across the common probe and both following actions. The registration, contacts, orientation knots, station arc coordinates, and checked edge masks come from the frozen moving-Y asset. A common rail coordinate comes from the baked 15° q at the final prefix contact. The reference uses a monotone quintic progress profile, a piecewise bilinear atlas contact strip for translation, and endpoint geodesic interpolation for orientation. A local fixed-rail IK uses the prior path's outgoing branch as the next reference seed. Preflight samples the pose and path grids for FK, configured self-collision, joint limits and modeled speed. Runtime samples at about 20 ms, integrates joint actuator steps subject to URDF speed bounds, and checks sample-level tracking, configured self-collision and limits. The low-level tracker has an ideal synchronized knee-angle signal from the simulated plant. The planners receive only the inherited noisy pose packet and public masks at capture time. Acoustics render from the registered nominal contact at the realized capture clock. The evaluator logs positional and angular FK residuals to that contact and applies the shared 0.25 mm and 0.15 degree tracking tolerances. The final 0.21 s of each slot tracks that contact before the sample. Joint-rate limits are the same at preflight and runtime. The shared probe runs for 2.50 s and is reported separately. Both arms and reserves share 0.65 s and 0.85 s post-probe slots and shared total caps of 60 mm and 60 degrees. Report summed FK sample distances, geodesic rotation and elapsed duration for all attempted post-probe paths, including attempted parts before any failures.

The two reserves specify cumulative lateral travel in the baked contact chart after the common probe. Narrow allows 10 mm, wide allows 26 mm. This is a simulated stroke intervention based on the baked 8 mm contact spacing. The same unsigned offset change is accumulated at each transition, and the monotone reference realizes exactly that lateral travel on a completed path. Starting at the center, both signs remain available as first actions under both reserves. A signed first action uses 8 mm, leaving only 2 mm in narrow, so it can continue in its own column, whereas wide admits recentering and cross-column continuations. Starting from a center first action leaves all three continuation views in both reserves. Log the actual support per exit. The same leg and endpoint identities remain present in every condition. The fixed stroke limits and ideal simulation are separate from fixed-pose native preflights, physical contact, physical calibration, and synchronized moving-leg experiments.

## Decisions and outcomes

Each planner uses the same full belief likelihood, stochastic masks with calibrated nominal noise, clutter and dropout, kinematic path bank, cost, terminal criterion, and deterministic scrambled Sobol nodes. Real masks retain the existing combined stress generator, including its physical-time dropouts and persistent clutter. Use 32 first-mask nodes and 128 terminal-mask nodes, shared as common random numbers. Terminal observation probabilities use self-normalized predictive importance quadrature with a common proposal per terminal view. The terminal utility is zero below fresh daughter confidence 0.87, otherwise model probability of a correct claim minus model probability of an incorrect claim. Cost is 0.055 per scheduled second plus 0.00045 per nominal preflight path mm. At the first decision B maximizes over admissible tails separately inside each predicted first-mask outcome, then averages. MPC maximizes over fixed admissible tails after averaging over the same predicted first-mask outcomes. Both assimilate the actual first mask and replan at the last real decision with the same one-step algorithm. Score ties use stable candidate order. No prior witness contributes directly to the final utility or claim. The planned score matrix summaries, first choices, matched open-loop tails, real tail scores, posterior changes and realized tail switches are logged.

The primary binary outcome is one correctly associated daughter centroid from the final site-4 image, at confidence at least 0.87, and completed legal execution of both post-probe paths. A separately stored evaluator identity label scores the declared centroid. Earlier witnesses and the independent map RMSE diagnostic do not count toward primary success. Report wrong confirmations overall and by trunk versus clutter. Mechanical failure counts as failure in the primary denominator. A matched timing budget does not imply equivalent realized paths.

Two paired primary comparisons are online B minus online MPC and online B minus frozen B. Preserve both reserves in subject resampling and report each reserve and the paired reserve interaction as secondary. To support both contributions, each pooled paired success estimate must reach +5 percentage points and each one-sided 97.5% paired bootstrap lower bound must exceed zero. Also require each paired wrong-confirm one-sided 97.5% upper bound to be at most +2 percentage points. Claim equivalent realized cost only if the simultaneous 95% paired intervals across two comparisons and three metrics fall inside ±2 mm, ±2 degrees and ±0.10 s, respectively. Report matched caps or tradeoffs otherwise. Compare observable tail switches with the corresponding planned open-loop tail and fresh terminal outcome as mechanism diagnostics. The visible-probe sensitivity subset and the reserve-specific diagnostics cannot replace either primary comparison.
