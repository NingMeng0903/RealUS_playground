Independent streaming-A audit, 2026-11-10

Verdict
The frozen results are reproducible and show a learned conditional response acting causally on action scores in the declared synthetic family. They do not establish an effective first-long-axis acquisition skill, a trained production response model, a calibrated belief, or an advantage of B. B is absent. The main cohort's improvement in independently audited ever-quality (+5/256) is uncertain under the declared paired descriptive interval, while correct public stops decrease (46 vs 51). Preserve that negative result. Shift-cohort ever-quality improves (+12/128), but correct-stop difference remains uncertain and one false public stop occurs. No change of objective or test tuning is justified by this audit.

What was independently reproduced
All sealed manifest entries matched at audit start. Each of the two source groups was regenerated for all 512 worlds x 16 attempts. Selection used only the two public visible images and identity scores; private renderer returns were discarded. Accepted pair indices (473 positive / 613 exact) and feature/response byte hashes match MODEL.json exactly. An independent augmented-least-squares ridge solve reproduces the saved conditional W within 1.01e-15 and constant W within 8.33e-17. Both model coefficient arrays are bit-identical across the timing correction. Source/test/shift/negative-control world seeds and noise keys are distinct; the test source only loads the frozen model, with matching protocol/source hashes and prior parent approval. This checks saved provenance and implementation; a file timestamp is not a cryptographic proof that a human never viewed data.

All 2,048 method-world episodes and 54,420 public frames were replayed read-only from public histories. Every recorded DMP actual endpoint and duration was reproduced with the actual DMP library. Every saved public feature frame and audit source was regenerated from its frozen seed; action argmax, maximum score, particle posterior ESS, mismatch count, unique evidence ID, no-repeat rule, five-frame public stop, independent true-quality check, and final outcome agree. All paired descriptive bootstrap intervals and response diagnostics were recomputed. Entire trajectories of actions/audit outcomes/public stops match the retained pre-accounting-fix run. All six production experiment contract tests passed, including the complete-episode hidden-world/audit noninterference counterfactual.

Prior, information boundary and integration
The allowed generic template lane/tangent prior, public external body context, known command and current actual endpoint remain legitimate inputs; probability does not require deleting template/path priors. No sampled test curve, response E, hidden center, source identity, future frame or independent audit outcome enters the policy or training target. Hidden patient truth is used by the renderer and the separate evaluator. The actual DMP endpoint causes the generated feature observation. This remains a one-station L0 feature experiment with a linear designed response family, not B-mode, a continuous 15 mm 3D vessel, the production SMPL-X atlas, dIRD, joint QP, full servo, or physical ultrasound. The linear conditional generator is exactly representable in the chosen feature family; prediction gains here cannot establish necessity of that architecture or e3nn.

Likelihood / stopping qualifications
The likelihood uses continuous Gaussian image features and Beta identity, explicit missing outcomes, and fixed particles retaining E and geometry across frames. Its valid/invalid observation branches have their declared probability masses; nuisance E is not independently resampled per frame. Missing numeric features are null. Public stopping uses five distinct observed frames over 0.5 seconds, with visible length/center/angle/contact/identity thresholds. Audit truth cannot trigger free retries. The low final ESS (commonly 1-2) and one reported model mismatch preclude a calibrated-posterior or population-coverage claim.

Three protocol/report qualifications require explicit language:
1. rank_actions p_la is an expected heuristic geometric/detection score. Its exponential center/angle terms and ndtr length term are not the exact probability that five noisy frames pass the public verifier; it omits that conjunction and identity/contact factors. Calling it calibrated public-LA probability would overstate the implementation.
2. response_diagnostics measures normalized error against the hidden generator J on independently sampled candidate displacement pairs in test worlds. It does not restrict to observable two-image transitions, contrary to that metric wording in the original protocol. This is a valid synthetic diagnostic but not empirical held-out observable response error. It is not a training leak: the diagnostic is downstream of fitting and not fed into policy.
3. Broyden updates J from already obtained public blocks without extra observations. The retained particle weights are not recomputed under the changed J for earlier frames. This is an approximate online filter, not exact joint Bayesian inference over a fixed response model. All methods share candidates, action/frame limits, and cost accounting, but that approximation limits what a comparison can establish about optimal adaptive geometry baselines.

Additional identity limitation: false-source length is generated only on [0,10 mm], whereas public LA requires >=15 mm. Thus a false-source detection cannot pass this public gate by construction. The sole reported false public stop contains target-source frames and reflects noisy public features passing while true geometry fails. Near-zero false-stop counts do not validate confusing-vessel identity discrimination or a calibrated real-image verifier.

Budget / accounting
Same 37 candidates, six actions, thirty new frames, and 60-second limit are enforced. Every audited saved episode stayed within the deadline. Total elapsed equals saved actual movement + 0.5 seconds per action + charged compute within 3.6e-15 seconds. Planning, on-demand actual-endpoint DMP fitting, likelihood updates, and Broyden updates are charged; the shared 1369-path generic cache setup is separately reported, with isolated method caches. The source correction is consequential for cost honesty, though it left all 2048 action/outcome sequences unchanged. These wall-clock observations are not a real-time guarantee; image rendering models sensing, and small verifier/bookkeeping overhead is not comprehensively timed. Candidate ranking uses nominal motion cost, while execution accounting uses the actual DMP duration.

The RMT statistic censors failed ever-quality acquisition at 60 seconds, even though six-action exhaustion usually occurs earlier. It is an audit ever-quality time, not time to a correct public acceptance, nor average actual operating time. The -1.662-second main learned-vs-analytic RMT difference must be labelled accordingly. Full empirical counts are in streaming_independent.json, along with exact reproduction scope, hashes, and all cohort summaries. No test outcome retuning or production changes were performed by this audit.
