INDEPENDENT FINAL AUDIT — A/B OFFLINE VALIDATION
Date: 2026-11-10. Reviewer: independent Astra checker.
Scope: source/math/data audit plus fresh offline numerical/module checks.
No hardware, contact experiment, ultrasound acquisition or network action was
performed by this auditor. Only audit/ files were written by this auditor.

VERDICT

There is enough evidence to pursue B as a bounded OFFLINE research direction
on top of A: observation-conditioned recourse is correctly implemented in the
finite model, improves the declared synthetic task objective against two
full-belief baselines, and the learned-response extension closes a real A->B
dataflow through actual DMP endpoints and actual robot-kinematics queries.

This is NOT evidence that B is a validated end-to-end anatomical ultrasound
system, that it has acquired a first qualified image, or that every proposed
module is necessary. A remains a supported simpler response-model foundation.
The learned dIRD proxy provides ZERO measured decision benefit in these core
comparisons; the capability benefit is due to sampled exact admissibility.
The additional B benefit over full-belief greedy is modest in absolute loss
units, with many ties. Production servo failures remain unresolved. Selecting
B as an offline direction is a scoped research judgment, not a deployment or
publication-readiness endorsement and not a universal superiority claim.

3. THEORY: WHAT WAS ACTUALLY ESTABLISHED

For ell(a,X)=(X-a)^2, r=X,B=-1,W=1, both P(±1)=1/2 and
Q(0)=2/3,Q(±sqrt(3))=1/6 give Gamma=diag(1,1), hence current risk1+a^2.
Binary Y=1{X>0} yields expected posterior MSE0 under P and2/5 under Q.
At observation cost4/5, P observes and Q skips. With ternary sign including a
separate zero outcome both posterior MSEs are0; that version is invalid.
Therefore current quadratic moments need not determine future observation
value. This does not prove A must discard belief or that the full posterior is
the only possible sufficient representation. It is not a novel general VOI
theorem or physical result.

The full-risk/cross-term, nominal-nullspace, conditional-second-moment and
nonrigid normal examples are algebraically sound in their stated models.
Numerical equivariance is verified for a declared representation and canonical
frame, not across real tissues or arbitrary deformations. No fitted uniform
model-error/remainder bounds or post-selection physical chance certificate are
provided. e3nn is actually exercised but untrained; its necessity or advantage
over an ordinary local-coordinate model is not demonstrated.

V9.3 already contains a short-lookahead equation. Thus the evidence supports
an operational planning comparison, not a claim that B first invents lookahead
relative to every algorithm described in V9.3.

8. FIVE GATES AND NEXT DECISION

Gate1 — MODULE CORRECTNESS: PASS for bounded tested DMP/KMP and corrected dIRD
operator/bridge contracts; NOT a blanket production-stack pass. Stock servo,
Module C and atlas runtime defects remain. Source/asset provenance is traceable.

Gate2 — A MECHANISM: PASS in the declared held-out synthetic affine response
family with negative control and measured-probe comparator. PARTIAL relative to
the requested complete anatomical first-acquisition task; no valid full-servo,
ultrasound or cross-physics validation.

Gate3 — B SYNTHETIC MECHANISM: PASS. Finite reference agreement, public-history
updates, no-information/high-cost controls, matched full-belief baselines and
failure accounting are correct. Superiority is local to the declared objectives,
model assumptions, finite action graph and offline compute convention.

Gate4 — B PRODUCTION INTEGRATION: PASS NARROWLY for the finite learned-response
->actual primitive->real mechanical graph dataflow; PARTIAL/NOT PASSED for the
proposed end-to-end continuous/contact/ultrasound controller. Exact capability
information matters; learned dIRD-proxy decision benefit is absent. This must
not be compressed into an unqualified claim that all B modules were validated.

Gate5 — PHYSICAL/ULTRASOUND EVIDENCE: NOT PASSED; none was collected.

Recommended next decision: keep A as the common response/control foundation and
continue B as the specific offline recourse research branch. Carry forward the
no-dIRD-proxy result; do not add unnecessary EM/e3nn/primitive expansion to make
the architecture appear essential. Before making an end-to-end claim, repair
the missing-data and production execution contracts, define observed image
qualification/identity and failure/deadline semantics, integrate that actual
observation/termination loop with the same measured motion and capability state,
and then compare against a corrected strong A/servo baseline under common
physical and compute budgets on independent physical objects. These are missing
evidence requirements, not experiments that have already passed.
