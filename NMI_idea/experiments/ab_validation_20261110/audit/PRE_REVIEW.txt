1. OVERALL ASSESSMENT AND SCOPE

PLAN correctly separates hidden truth, public evidence, patient belief, response,
motion and capability. Its intended controls and failure reporting are sound.
It is a preregistered conceptual protocol, not yet a confirmatory statistical
protocol: primary endpoint, evaluation populations, world counts, fixed seeds,
training budgets, execution/compute budgets, practical superiority margins,
confidence construction and stop/acceptance thresholds are not fixed here.
Freeze these before inspecting comparative performance, with an amendment log.
If choices follow observed results, call that portion exploratory/development
and retain untouched test worlds for the final comparison.

Three evidence labels are mandatory and cannot be promoted by combining them:
SYNTHETIC MECHANISM: abstract/simulated observations, losses and first-acquisition
outcomes, including a synthetic acoustic proxy.
PRODUCTION KINEMATICS: actual imported primitive/dIRD code and real robot assets,
coordinate contracts, numerical behavior and available exact mechanical checks.
TRUE PHYSICAL ULTRASOUND: actual probe motion/contact and acquired ultrasound
on independent objects with a defensible first-qualified-image endpoint.
The third category is absent in the stated offline scope. Actual dIRD weights
plus synthetic observations remain mixed first/second-category evidence.

2. EXACT GAMMA / OBSERVATION VALUE EXAMPLE

Define loss ell(a,X)=(X-a)^2, actions a in the real line, r=X, B=-1 and W=1.
P(X=-1)=P(X=1)=1/2. Q(X=0)=2/3 and Q(X=±sqrt(3))=1/6 each.
Both have E[X]=0, E[X^2]=1 and Gamma=E[[r,B]^T[r,B]]=diag(1,1).
Thus every current quadratic action risk is R(a)=1+a^2 and min_a R(a)=1.
Define the observation explicitly as binary Y=1{X>0}; do not write only sign(X).
Under P, each outcome identifies X, so expected posterior optimal MSE is 0.
Under Q, Y=1 occurs with probability 1/6 and has zero conditional variance.
Y=0 occurs with probability 5/6. Conditional mean is -sqrt(3)/5, second moment
3/5, and variance 3/5-3/25=12/25. Weighted residual MSE is (5/6)(12/25)=2/5.
For observation cost 4/5, observe costs 4/5 under P and 6/5 under Q; skipping
costs 1. The optimal decisions therefore differ. A binary >=0 convention also
works by symmetry. A ternary sign with a distinct zero outcome reveals X in
both cases and destroys this example.

Valid conclusion: this current quadratic-risk matrix is not, in general, a
sufficient recursive state for observation-conditioned decision value.
Invalid conclusions: all moment representations fail, the full posterior is
the only possible sufficient statistic, A cannot retain belief, B is always
better, or a robot/ultrasound benefit is demonstrated. This is an existence
counterexample with fixed actions and costless physical relocation, not a
physical experiment or novelty claim for value of information.
An independent Fraction-based reproduction is check_moment_example.py.

3. A VERSUS B: DEFINE THE ACTUAL COMPARISON

V9.3 already contains a two-step lookahead equation and same-world rollouts.
Therefore do not equate A with all of V9.3 and B with the first introduction of
Bayesian short planning. Define A concretely as the implemented one-step or
open-loop-recourse baseline; define B by observation-conditioned recourse.
Both should receive the same full belief, response, candidate action permissions,
public observations, low-level controller, stopping logic and exact validator.
Only the designated planning feature should differ in the main comparison.
Belief-compressed or no-capability methods are secondary ablations, not the sole
main baseline. If A is intentionally simpler, state the induced limitation.

B can validly establish a causal advantage of choosing an observation whose
public result changes a later feasible decision, under a declared model and
budget. It can show capability changes alter the useful observation/action and
that preserving unresolved modes matters in specified cases. It cannot by that
alone establish new general control theory, necessity of every architecture
module, physical ultrasound transfer, or superiority over all strong A variants.

The exact full-belief enumerator must have the same root actions, observation
alphabet, horizon, terminal objective, admissible history policies and exit
states as B. If it is a larger-horizon oracle, label the bound rather than demand
equality. Include a horizon-matched open-loop plan (same two-step costs, second
action not observation-conditioned) to separate horizon benefit from information
recourse. Include a forced-observation replay with common observations as a
diagnostic of recourse; do not give its unseen data to online baselines for free.

4. HIDDEN-TRUTH AND COUNTERFACTUAL LEAKAGE TESTS

Controllers must have no hidden-world or evaluator reference through constructor,
closure, candidate generation, training feature, cost label, termination flag,
random seed, object ID, or renderer method. Posterior sample hypotheses are
legitimate; the realized hidden sample index is not.
Run a behavioral noninterference test: identical public histories and robot
states with different compatible hidden truths give identical policy outputs
using the same planner randomness. This includes VERIFY/STOP decisions.
In exact trees, group hidden worlds by the same public observation history
before minimizing continuation cost. E_Y min_a E[loss|Y] is legitimate;
E_hidden min_a loss is clairvoyant. Policy branch keys cannot contain truth IDs.
Draw one persistent world (geometry, bias, occlusion/response state) per rollout;
do not independently redraw the patient after each action.
No-detection is an outcome with its own likelihood, not missing evidence erased
from the tree and not a fake zero center measurement. Likelihoods must sum to
one per world/action; impossible outcomes are skipped only when predictive
probability is exactly zero under the declared model.
The simulation evaluator may use truth to score a false stop, but policy stopping
must depend on public data. Reset states and priors cannot use true targets.

5. BUDGETS, COSTS AND BASELINES

Report two resources independently: executed acquisition time/actions and
planning compute/model/primitive/dIRD/exact-validator queries. Equal candidate
counts do not imply equal work when B expands many observation branches.
Use a fixed shared physical deadline and charge every acquired observation,
movement, recovery, return transition, verification and false stop. Charge actual
planning wall time to acquisition time if the deployment blocks motion while
planning; otherwise state the asynchronous timing model. Cache setup and reuse
rules must be shared. Offline table generation is not a free production query.
Report an equal-compute comparison or an explicit performance/compute frontier;
do not claim equal budget solely from identical physical action sets.
Normalize heterogeneous units once before test. Do not tune capability weights,
sensor costs or observation likelihoods on test cases to produce B's preference.
When capability changes, recompute genuine robot-relative inputs and outputs.
An obstacle penalty must remain distinct from fixed dIRD reachability; changing
an unrelated hand-coded cost is not evidence of dIRD/observation coupling.

Meaningful negative controls: exact analytic response with zero residual need;
uninformative observation; perfect prior; observation cost too high; one available
continuation action; capability identical for all candidates; strong common
feedback. B may tie or lose when extra work has no decision value. Every module
is optional unless independently demonstrated to solve a failure in the scope.

6. SUPPORT, CALIBRATION AND MODULE NECESSITY

Split by independent world/patient geometry, including shared registration and
response biases. More frames from one world are not independent test samples.
Include train/validation/test hashes and generation parameters. The same
analytic generator as the fitted response can check implementation but does not
establish generalization; independently perturbed held-out mechanisms and
declared out-of-support cases are needed to examine response robustness.
Broad prior support must cover allowed test targets and registration variation.
Report misspecification/failure when truth lies outside support; do not silently
inject the true mode, jitter around true target, or reset to truth after failure.
Check posterior normalization, repeated-data IDs, persistent correlated bias,
and no-detection updates. Approximation may be acceptable but must be labeled.
Measure detection log loss/Brier/calibration and conditional feature errors on
held-out executed or paired actions, with reliability counts. Also measure
calibration on selected/accepted actions, where optimization can exploit model
error. Average prediction error does not establish a uniform risk certificate.
Finite differences versus autodiff check the same mathematical implementation,
not correctness of acoustic physics or safety. For local risk claims retain
cross terms and assess the actual composed command after all corrections.

Compare harmonic/other body representations with the same correspondence and
registration information. Common SE(3) changes test coordinate consistency;
moving only the body changes physical reachability and is not an invariance
violation. Nonrigid generalization is a different claim. Local coordinates can
provide invariance without e3nn; no necessity claim from omitted strong baselines.
Importing DMP/KMP/dIRD is insufficient integration evidence: show outputs are
used in policy candidates, action timing/exit state or feasibility, and trace a
changed output through a changed decision. A straight-line wrapper is not a full
production primitive. A constant queried dIRD scalar is not meaningful coupling.

7. STATISTICS AND AUDITABLE DECISION GATES

Primary outcome should be mean deadline-truncated first-qualified-image time,
with failed/false-stopped runs charged the deadline; separately report success,
false identity and false stop. Do not report only successful-run mean time.
Use paired world-level effects and uncertainty intervals. Pairing simulation
randomness must preserve each method's own data-generating law; counters keyed
by world/action/repetition are safer than assuming one sequential RNG draw has
the same physical meaning after policies diverge. Predefine handling of ties,
timeouts, infeasible actions and computational failures.
Report all prespecified regimes and ablations, including non-improvements.
Retuning after observing outcomes must be logged as development, not erased.

Gate 1 — module correctness: real module/weight/asset provenance plus appropriate
independent numerical/mathematical checks and coordinate contracts.
Gate 2 — A mechanism: held-out action selection benefits where model mismatch
exists; analytic negative control does not invent an advantage; strong feedback
and canonical geometric baselines are retained.
Gate 3 — B synthetic mechanism: noninterference and observation grouping pass;
horizon-matched exact solution agrees; high-cost/no-information controls behave;
same-budget gain versus A is interpretable without withheld actions/data.
Gate 4 — B production integration: real primitive and dIRD quantities enter both
the planner and actual executed synthetic trajectories; state-dependent calls,
costs and exact checking limitations are traced, with useful ablations.
Gate 5 — physical claim: independent physical ultrasound acquisition evidence.

If Gate 3 alone passes, verdict is B prototype/mechanism support only. If A is
not usable or Gate 4 fails, B cannot be selected as a demonstrated integrated
system. If Gates 1–4 pass with stable practically meaningful benefits, B may be
the better offline research direction, with Gate 5 still explicitly missing.
An absent/inapplicable module need not be fabricated to pass a narrative gate;
reduce the claimed scope. An inconclusive or adverse result must remain visible.
