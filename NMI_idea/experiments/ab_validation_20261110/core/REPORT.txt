world distribution; strong measured local calibration remains a useful baseline.

stated synthetic model and finite mechanical action graph. Learned field score
marginal benefit is null in both tests; exact validation is essential in these
placements. Artificial priors/likelihoods, graph restriction, sampled mechanical
validation and substantial false acceptance prevent claiming a working physical
ultrasound acquisition system or a general necessity/novelty theorem.
