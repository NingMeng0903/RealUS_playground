# Independent design and verification

## ASTRA MAX

ASTRA specified the nested all-return and nearest-packet experiment. Its likelihood assigns equal target mass to indistinguishable slots and selects one candidate after marginalizing the joint hypothesis belief. It recommended separating association gains from gains due to the information used by a continuation selector. It also specified a case-conditioned opportunity diagnostic using independent tape halves and proposed a future active acquisition rule conditional on positive pilot evidence.

The inexpensive test evaluated one existing common packet and one checked paid probe. ASTRA verified the full freeze digest and each frozen source hash. Its outcome review found no relation-specific completion benefit and supported stopping before the acquisition trigger. It distinguished the 14 fixed-path daughter frame acceptances recovered from legacy selection from case-level completion. The primary closed-loop gain over legacy crosses zero and the separate resolution-sensitivity cohort has the opposite point direction. The two cohorts do not isolate a resolution effect. The split-repeat diagnostic is an achieved label-assisted performance estimate with noisy ranking. An exhaustive population bound is outside its scope.

## SOL MAX

SOL reviewed baseline parity, physical-clock pairing, tie multiplicity, cases without legal alternatives, feature separation and risk/cost reporting. It identified two pre-training fixes. The fixed policy now averages with incumbent fallback over every assigned case. The learned rankers include evidence-by-action interactions so evidence can change the ordering of the two alternatives. SOL also required the paid intervention to be compared against no-probe MPC, probe-only MPC, and free relational selection on matched tapes.

The independent kernel audit enumerated distinct assignments from the inherited fine-bin CDFs across both grids. It covered 1,140 clock/address/atom slices, 2,362,080 packet/slot masses and 48 explicit duplicate-bin draws. The midpoint smoke replay independently verified 24 complete paths, 24 anchor packets, 116 capture packets and MPC commands, and 24 stops. The primary and sensitivity trace replay checked a further 768 complete paths, 768 anchors, 3,661 capture packets, identity claims and resource totals. Numerical differences between indistinguishable slots occurred only below the claim threshold and left traces and claims intact.

SOL independently checked all four weighted ridge normal equations, train-only scaling and full-denominator fixed choices. The route audit rebuilt 512 public evidence packets and action libraries, verified 241 joint witnesses and 8,192 suffix keys, and checked 772 scores and 1,024 selections. It verified 256 fresh tape hashes, reconstructed 1,408 fresh policy paths with 3,747 packets and independently recomputed 305 MPC actions and 512 repeat paths. It reproduced all 70 paired contrasts, including case-clustered intervals and exact harmful-discordance bounds. The final review supported stopping on the frozen screen.
