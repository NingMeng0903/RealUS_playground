# All-return evidence and continuation choice on the shared MPC

This inexpensive development study holds the motion bank, joint latent case model, 256 belief atoms, 0.87 claim rule, two-capture MPC optimizer, cost/risk coefficients and endpoint from `ab_full_b_rollout_20261113`. The two required fresh claims and a completed scan determine success. Any wrong identity is recorded. The shared caps are six acquisitions, 65 mm TCP, 90 degrees rotation and 250 mm rail. Both the daughter and single-trunk tasks use the same code. Case and noise namespaces are disjoint from previous studies.

## Shared evidence control

Each of the three simulator returns is absent or drawn from its inherited centroid distribution. The observed packet is the sorted multiset of all visible bins. Bins preserve multiplicity and hide return-kind order. Tables sum the joint labeled assignments. For candidate slot \(j\), \(C_{hj}\) is the joint mass of that packet and kind 0 occupying slot \(j\), divided among indistinguishable duplicates. Choose \(j^*=\arg\max_j\sum_h b_hC_{hj}\) after mixing hypotheses, then apply the common threshold. The all-return update conditions the joint prior-correct mass on the chosen identity only when there is a claim.

Use symmetric nested cuts from the inherited quantization. Primary 3×3 uses lateral ±1.5 and depth ±0.7 mm relative to the nominal centroid. The 5×3 resolution sensitivity also uses lateral ±4 mm. The matched nearest control projects the exact same multiset to its nearest bin and a multiple-return flag. Both use the same original fine-pixel draws, physical-clock/channel tape, and model assumptions. The nested control resolves symmetric distances by public bin ID and randomly orders indistinguishable returns privately. The legacy 7×7 nearest arm retains the archived sensor/controller. Use a four-case smoke test, 96 new cases for the primary sensor comparison and 32 separate cases for resolution sensitivity. Report sampled cost and CPU separately. The motion clock advances on sampled checked trajectories. CPU does not move the object in this screening simulation.

## Cheap B and paid-acquisition diagnostic

Use a common checkpoint after two actual all-return MPC commands. A case already terminated stays in the denominator. At that checkpoint compare immediate MPC with at most two checked one-command continuations followed by the same all-return MPC. The alternatives are the center and a tilted window at the next required site, selected by a deterministic geometric rule and joint feasible witness. MPC duplicates are removed. Every case has the incumbent action. The tilted alternative prefers the opposite sign when starting tilted. None of these rules uses evaluator identity.

Test the value of the second common packet first. Both public route scorers get the same pre-packet belief summary, measured motion state, task progress and options. They differ in the evidence from this exact packet. One receives the nearest-plus-multiple projection, and the other also receives the full multiset, relative geometry and posterior summaries conditioned on it. Their downstream MPC uses the identical all-return state. A fixed comparator chooses the best action type from training, with MPC fallback if unavailable.

In a separate diagnostic, force one checked change to a same-station view before selecting a continuation. Select the mechanically cheapest view change with a joint finish witness after a non-claim. Hold if no changed view qualifies and an in-place observation has a witness. If the actual probe advances progress, immediately use shared MPC. If no probe is available, both paths execute direct MPC. Preserve the realized probe prefix and its acquisition clock. Compare each ranker against probe-only MPC at the identical exit and compare the whole forced-probe procedure against direct no-probe MPC with matched clock/channel innovations. This estimates the benefit of one extra acquisition before developing an active acquisition trigger.

Use 64 training and 64 independent held-out latent cases, both tasks, and 16 independent suffix tapes per public checkpoint. A single realized probe packet per case is frozen before the post-probe repeats. The paid comparison's direct-MPC replay uses the same frozen uniforms through the probe clock, followed by those independent suffix uniforms. Case-conditioned opportunity estimates choose on eight repeats and evaluate on the other eight, then swap. Report rank agreement, route availability, all-case completion, wrong identity and actual costs. Bootstraps group all tapes by latent case. Fit one fixed-penalty linear predictor for each context and representation using paired complete-scan differences, with public evidence by action-type interactions. Freeze it before held-out execution. The scorer prefers MPC on exact score ties. Confirm its choice on a fresh tape independent of training and the repeat-ranking halves. Record chosen and executed actions. Main acquisition planning uses no online rollout search.

Use a ≥3 percentage-point fresh-tape improvement over direct MPC as a practical screen, accompanied by positive paired repeat estimates over direct MPC, same-packet nearest and train-fixed selection. A paid acquisition also needs a positive repeat estimate over probe-only MPC and a positive fresh contrast over no-extra-acquisition relation B on its matched tape. Check no net increase in wrong-confirmation cases and simultaneous upper sampled costs within +2 mm, +2 degrees and +0.10 s. Report harmful and favorable wrong discordances separately. These are screening criteria, with insufficient sample size to establish the previous 2% harmful-discordance bound. A positive pilot calls for a separate freeze, a continuous or finer acoustic clock that charges planning delay, and a new confirmatory cohort.
