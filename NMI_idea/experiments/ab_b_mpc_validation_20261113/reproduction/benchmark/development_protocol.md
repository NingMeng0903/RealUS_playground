# Development protocol for the B alternatives

Reference commit cdb1705a02f60382ffbe9e42f2ed420e27a28ddb. The archived moving-Y cohort is historical development and diagnostic evidence.

**Route scheduling.** Two required fresh, correctly associated site images and completed motion, both sites counted. Twenty-four paired independent cases at seed base 6173061. Evaluate separately on daughter and trunk, on both physical stroke limits, and with nominal and suffix view-block deletion estimators. Freeze shared sampled fixed-rail execution, five acquisition slots, identical public Bayesian updates, identical admissible edges and stopping rules. Compare open-loop-plan receding MPC and one-verifier-status recourse MPC against full verifier-status feedback. B_bound must return the same solution as unpruned B_status. It assesses search effort.

**Reference challenge.** Development seed base 6173091, 32 paired cases. Test choice of reference station or near-target scan and existing center and tilted windows. Daughter and single-lumen trunk require a new correctly identified final image after two checked motions. Same actual case and acoustic rendering for all arms, including injected ghost centroid jitter. Cross the nominal, fitted marginal clutter, and fitted plus reference-deletion beliefs with open-loop-plan receding MPC, generic raw-mask feedback, paired-advantage B, and fixed center reference/near roots. Fixed roots share the real tail replan. No selected output is a success unless the evaluated final ordinal has the requested label. All failures stay in the denominator. Use the all-root evaluator replay only to describe optimistic action support.

The first mechanical preflight for the daughter task used stations 1, 2 and 3. It could not register both center controls within the shared slot and no cases ran. The completed task uses the reverse direction through stations 5, 4 and 3. The trunk task uses stations 5, 6 and 7. This adjustment came from the mechanical gate before reading any reference-challenge outcomes. The final shared graph has six first moves and 28 tails for daughter, and six first moves and 30 tails for trunk.

The final repeatability diagnostic uses 16 new development cases under seed base 6173093 and eight suffix acoustic replicates per root. Every forced root uses the same real-mask tail replanner and sampled motion checks. A per-case oracle chooses a root from even replicates by correct completion minus wrong confirmation, then is scored on odd replicates. It can use the evaluated outcomes of other replicates and serves only as a headroom diagnostic. The public MPC and B are also evaluated on the odd replicates. No policy or forecast weight is selected from this diagnostic.

**Development decision.** Use the task and estimator cells rather than selecting favorable cases. An efficacy candidate needs at least a five-percentage-point gain on both tasks over its model-matched MPC and positive net gain against the corresponding strong feedback comparator, with distinct realized action evidence and no observed increase over two percentage points in wrong claims. Treat a systematic absolute cost shift beyond 2 mm, 2 degrees or .10 s as a tradeoff. A fixed-window control matching the gain removes the need for adaptive B. Freeze the candidate, comparator, physical settings, analysis and new seed split before one prospective confirmation if the development gate is met. Report the gate outcome even if no candidate advances. The developmental strata share the same case draw within a seed and are not separate independent subjects.

**Confirmation target if promoted.** At least +5 pp paired mean correct completion over model-matched MPC, one-sided 97.5% lower bound above zero, paired wrong-confirm harm upper bound at most +2 pp and simultaneous intervals within 2 mm, 2 degrees and .10 s on realized execution. Compare against generic feedback and fixed reference at shared compute/cost. Sample size selected from development discordance, with power disclosed. An eligible improvement should replicate on independent geometry or a moving B-mode phantom before claiming transfer beyond the simulated atlas.
