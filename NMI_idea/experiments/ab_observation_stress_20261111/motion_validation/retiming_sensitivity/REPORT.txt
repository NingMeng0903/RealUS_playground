Fixed-time ProDMP sensitivity diagnostic
=========================================

Purpose
-------
This is a compact causal check on the previous paired replay.  The spatial
curve, endpoint, q seed, body anchor, native binary, scan profile, secondary
preferences, and QP settings are unchanged.  Only the ProDMP duration is
multiplied by 2 or 4.  The original 1x trace is reused from
``motion_validation/prodmp_segment_*_trace.npz``; the 2x and 4x runs are new
deterministic offline native replays.  No planner timing, gate, or controller
source was changed.

Time and frame checks
---------------------

The normalized spatial curve is the same for all three factors.  The
reference pose reconstructed from each trace agrees with the independently
sampled reference to numerical precision.  The native model period remains
0.005 s.  The ProDMP analytic twist is still the world/base geometric twist
from the fixed anchor; scaling tau changes its magnitude and not its frame.

-------

Position and transverse numbers are same-time ``FK(q_meas_before[t])``
against the reference.  The transverse column removes the component parallel
to the fixed endpoint chord; the full position column remains the official
position diagnostic.  Rotation is the geodesic SO(3) error.

Interpretation
--------------

The first three mixed-segment failures are strongly time-sensitive.  A fixed
2x slowdown changes them from 8.630/4.959/4.495 mm p95 position error to
1.651/0.111/0.029 mm, and a 4x slowdown reduces all three to about 0.005 mm.
The corresponding QP received-to-feasible twist projection falls sharply,
and the effective actual speed becomes close to the reference speed.  This is
consistent with a transient interaction between the ProDMP spatial shape and
the existing Cartesian task QP's per-tick allocation/box history.  It is not
evidence of a missing endpoint constraint, wrong pose/twist frame, solver
fault, or the extra J1 hard wall.

The test does not decide that 2x/4x retiming belongs in deployment.  It only
establishes that the prior failures are not intrinsic impossibility of the
spatial ProDMP curve.  The formal A/B result remains the original 1x timing;
the slower runs are a diagnostic and sensitivity result, not a revised planner
or acceptance criterion.  Actual effective speed can exceed the reference
effective speed at 1x because q_meas oscillates during the rejected transient;
this is why both error and speed ratio are reported.

Residual boundary
-----------------

This remains an ideal measured-joint native replay with no physical latency,
contact/force, ultrasound, or dynamic material-frame transport.  Strict jerk
margin is retained in ``RESULTS.json`` and is not used to alter the requested
2 mm / 2 deg gate.
