# Task-preserving inverse views: bounded opportunity screen

This protocol is fixed before reading the new screen outcomes. It follows the failed full-rollout, continuation-regression and relation-packet B studies. It does not tune another advantage admission threshold.

## Question and scope

Can a new *executable acquisition*, replacing a needed task capture, expose reproducible continuation opportunities beyond the complete receding MPC? A separate question is whether generating that acquisition improves the shared MPC itself.

The user suggested generating informative actions backward from a learned/current model and enforcing dIRD constraints. This first implementation uses the existing probabilistic geometric model and numerical finite search of its continuous tilt parameter, not RL and not a new learning theorem. Existing ±12 degree acoustic orientations bound interpolation. New intermediate angles are ±3, ±6 and ±9 degrees. They retain the skin contact position. This study does not establish acoustic accuracy outside that support or validate real B-mode/contact execution.

## Shared process and controls

The pinned full-MPC controller, observation confidence, per-observation posterior/claim-history updates, finite horizon, remaining-value approximation, termination and resource caps are reused. MPC replans after every real packet until completion or termination. It is model-based receding optimization with fixed hyperparameters, not a hand-coded route rule. Task types are daughter 4→3 and genuinely single-lumen trunk 6→7. Both require two fresh correctly identified target images and completed sampled trajectories. Any incorrect accepted identity fails correct completion and is recorded independently.

All pairs share the latent case, anchor image and physical-clock/return-channel disturbance tape. Cases have persistent geometry, calibration offsets, dynamic lag and false echoes. The new kernel preserves original-angle probabilities exactly. Intermediate geometry is reprojected. Endpoint false-echo regimes are smoothly interpolated, including their effective clipped positions, without seeding fresh random regimes from new view IDs. The latter is a declared synthetic interpolation, not a measured acoustic response or necessarily a single rigid ghost when endpoint clipping differs. An apparent gain requires a fixed-ghost-regime sensitivity before a physical interpretation.

The graph uses the same two dIRD-ranked rail realizations and original evaluator. New endpoint configurations and complete sampled rest-to-rest edges undergo FK, joint-limit, rate and configured self-collision checks. Old nodes, edge metrics and traces must remain identical. Same-site and adjacent-site diagonal advance+tilt connections are available to both coarse and new angles. There is no body-collision/contact-force or real-controller certificate. Each acquisition edge lasts 1.5 s; hard caps remain 65 mm TCP travel, 90 degrees, 250 mm rail and six acquisitions.

Controls are original coarse graph MPC, coarse diagonal-graph MPC, inverse-menu diagonal-graph MPC, claim-only matched-cardinality menu MPC, fixed-angle matched-cardinality menu MPC and a fixed immediate progress command followed by inverse-menu MPC. Equal physical actions are available to the direct inverse-menu MPC and each intervention. The two menu controls separate an information objective from immediate identity acquisition and action-library density. They are diagnostic comparisons and do not change the primary comparator.

## Generator and opportunity census

The common checkpoint follows a single anchor image and no MPC command. The menu is frozen from that public packet for the whole episode, so this is an initial action-generation screen, not a recursively optimized continuous policy. At each target station the menu adds at most two distinct off-grid angles: greatest expected correct-minus-wrong claim mass, and greatest expected value of information for a small set of reachable legacy-view identity decisions. Every proposed view must have a joint-cap complete-task witness under ideal future acceptance. Reference decisions have an executable route from at least one proposed realization; actual stochastic continuability is assessed separately.

For model hypothesis h, utilities U_c(h) use a claim rule fixed under the public belief, never a clairvoyant per-hypothesis identity classifier. The proposal surrogate is

`J(a) = sum_o max_c sum_h b(h) L_a(o|h) U_c(h) - max_c sum_h b(h) U_c(h)`.

It is an identity-decision proposal score, not a final correct-completion probability. Known decision-directed active sensing and rollout theory are prior foundations, not novel theorems here.

The census evaluates every eligible first command reaching the next required site with a full-route witness, followed immediately by the identical inverse-menu MPC. Direct MPC is always retained. No mandatory paid probe is introduced. Evaluator-only ranking uses half of the suffix tapes, and the selected continuation is executed on the other half; then halves swap. Ranking chooses greatest mean paired correct completion among candidates with ranking-half mean excess TCP/turn/time no greater than +2 mm/+2 degrees/+0.10 s. Ties retain direct MPC. The uncensored hard-cap selector is reported separately. This diagnostic sees labels from other repeats; it is not a deployable B and is not a population oracle upper bound.

The primary screen uses 32 new latent cases (namespace 719901), each with 24 suffix tapes, both tasks. Original and continuation-study cases are not reused as new evidence. Smoke namespace 719900 is for functional checks only. All assigned cases stay in every denominator. Repeated tapes are averaged within case; intervals resample cases, not frames or tapes.

## Frozen decisions

Proceed to a public predictor only if daughter split-repeat correct completion improves by at least 3 percentage points, its case-paired 95% lower bound exceeds zero, its point gain exceeds the fixed progress control, wrong-identity probability has no net increase, and simultaneous 95% upper excess cost limits remain ≤+2 mm/+2 degrees/+0.10 s. A practical cost/risk or completion regression in nonbranch trunk blocks a generality claim. With 32 cases, a failed screen means insufficient demonstrated opportunity in this tested library, not proof that no population improvement exists.

If this screen fails, stop this continuation predictor. A separately labeled generator-only result may be studied if shared MPC benefits over coarse diagonal MPC; it does not establish a B selection advantage. A further pivot must change the measurable mechanism, for example aperture-selective projected skin positions, and undergo a new geometry/headroom screen rather than tuning a gate on these outcomes.

If the screen passes, freeze a public-packet conditional action-value predictor trained on disjoint cases. The complete initial public state is a function of (task, anchor packet), so a table/shrinkage estimator is an appropriate first learned model. Train namespace 719902, development 719903 and a fixed 256-case confirmation namespace 719904 are reserved but unopened at protocol creation. There is no reason to introduce model-free RL before measurable learnable opportunity is established.

## Costs, risk and disclosure

Record all executed captures, TCP/turn/rail totals and sampled elapsed time, including failures and recoveries. Simultaneous 95% paired cost intervals must lie entirely within ±2 mm/±2 degrees/±0.10 s for *strict equivalence*. The upper-only test is separately reported as *no-worse within margins*. Faster arms may pass no-worse and fail strict equivalence. Neither equal hard caps nor post-hoc sample matching proves cost equivalence.

CPU for menus and the full controller is logged and charged to each arm even when cached in evaluation. Computation does not advance the acoustic clock. Combined sampled-motion+CPU is a ledger, not a real-time moving-object test; no real-time improvement can be claimed from this screen. Headroom enumeration cost is not treated as online B cost.

Record any-wrong rates, B-only and MPC-only discordance for fresh one-tape validation, and the exact one-sided 97.5% upper bound on harmful discordance. Repeated tapes do not increase the independent sample count or certify the historical 2% harm bound. No absolute high-success claim follows from a small positive difference.

Independent ASTRA theory review and SOL statistical/implementation audits are archived with code, traces, inputs and negative results. A final report must state which planned stages actually ran.
