# Independent review of the inverse-view experiment

## Decision entering development

Stop the short-prefix and paid-probe studies at their published conclusions. The expanded continuation library gave daughter anchor gains of −0.04 points under per-case matching and +2.82 points with cohort cost allocation that failed measured motion equivalence. The strongest fixed daughter rule gained 11/512 cases by advancing to the final site when MPC stayed at an accepted site. Its time interval extended below the lower equivalence margin. The all-return study recovered discarded target frames but the sensor-level daughter contrast changed sign in the resolution sensitivity. The held-out relational scorer lost to the same-packet nearest scorer in daughter. Its paid acquisition increased wrong identities and sampled motion. These results leave the earlier libraries without a reproducible contextual effect at measured cost. They do not bound the attainable performance of genuinely new views.

Permit one bounded inverse-view development experiment. The intervention replaces a needed acquisition with a physically different view. The generator uses public geometry, robot configuration, available task progress and shared predictive belief. Preserve one public observation interface and one model across the comparison. Generate each synthetic episode's target and clutter from common latent material and matched clock/channel disturbances. An off-grid view should change the physical projection or a justified visibility term. Its numerical ID should never introduce an independent clutter regime. Use the sampled FK pose to construct the acoustic projection when it differs materially from the proposal. Record kernel differences over matched public states so off-grid novelty can be distinguished from negligible changes in the observation law.

Use dIRD to propose or rank poses. Verify sampled paths for configured joint limits, speed and robot/probe self-collision, preserve a common endpoint convention, and audit the actual checked motion spent. For each alternative first command, require one concrete geometric witness for the remaining ordered task within the shared joint resource caps. Record witness assumptions and path availability before evaluation labels enter. Preserve unresolved, aborted and terminal cases in the analysis. An ideal-acceptance witness is a mechanical check.

## Shared baseline and controls

The primary baseline uses the full replanning MPC with the expanded action support. It updates the common belief and prior-correctness mass on every actual packet, applies the same association rule, and replans to termination with the observed task progress and remaining resources. The tested prefix returns to this exact controller with its real exit state. The baseline can select the same action as the candidate at every tested checkpoint. Keep the archived controller coefficients, stop rule and planning horizon fixed. Compare equal available online effort, or separately measure a budget-matched stronger baseline if the B decision consumes additional model search. Log planning work for both arms.

Include a fixed public geometry/progress-only comparator with incumbent fallback. Report the daughter results where the first difference is simply advance versus remain at a station that already accepted an image. Separately compare expanded action support and restricted action support with the same controller and observation law on common views. The restricted arm is comparable to the archived baseline only after overlap and replay parity are checked. Report any improvement from shared action generation independently of forecast-driven B.

## Opportunity before fitting

Use 32 new independently seeded latent cases per task, paired over daughter and NONBRANCH. Fix the common checkpoint and the deterministic fixed comparator before viewing the outcomes. Keep the actual prefix, posterior, acquisition clock, robot node, task progress and unused caps fixed within each case. Independently sample 24 suffix tapes with common uniforms by physical acquisition clock and return channel. Include direct augmented MPC in every case. Re-evaluate every considered legal candidate through complete task termination with the shared MPC suffix. A candidate can only replace the prescribed first command. If a case cannot intervene, record all ties under its original assignment.

Within each case, rank on twelve tapes and evaluate on the other twelve, then swap. The primary selector chooses the largest mean paired completion improvement among candidates with ranking-tape mean cost differences within the one-sided margins of +2 mm TCP path, +2° cumulative probe turn and +0.10 s sampled elapsed execution. The incumbent wins ties. Freeze all ranking rules first. Report the hard-cap-only version and same-tape hindsight separately when feasible. Rank/evaluation tapes remain disjoint, including in costs. Report the two evaluation directions separately, selection agreement, route availability, executed new views and progress at the first route difference. A split-repeat estimate measures the attained performance of this finite, label-assisted procedure. A same-tape maximum measures the optimism from observing future outcomes within the tested library. Neither estimates the attainable population optimum without further assumptions.

Use the latent case as the independent unit. Pool held-out repeat differences within a case and bootstrap cases, preserving both split directions. For daughter, require at least +3 percentage points mean correct-completion improvement over full augmented MPC with a positive paired 95% lower limit, positive point improvement over the pre-fixed geometry/progress comparator, no net wrong-case increase, and measured simultaneous upper cost limits within the one-sided margins. The opportunity screen governs further experimental work. It is not a controller admission rule. Report the same completion, risk, cost and mechanism measures for NONBRANCH. A material NONBRANCH regression precludes a general positive claim. At this sample size, a failed screen means the tested design did not establish opportunity. A passed screen still needs independent prospective execution.

## Prospective evaluation if the screen passes

Freeze exact source hashes, action support, simulator and model seeds, controller, forecast, case count and new namespaces before the final run. A fixed 256-case paired cohort per task is compatible with an exact 2% harmful-discordance bound if there are zero harmful pairs. At 128 cases the exact one-sided 97.5% upper bound exceeds 2% even with zero harms. The candidate selector only sees public history and frozen model outputs, with one fresh independent tape per latent case. Rank forecasts by their relevance to paired terminal completion on independent cases, including a fixed route or action-mean reference. Execute the frozen selection and full augmented MPC on the same latent cases and tapes.

The primary endpoint requires fresh correct confirmations in order at both required sites and completed checked motion. Count any wrong confirmation, including wrong confirmations during later failed episodes. Report paired totals, wins/losses, B-only and MPC-only wrong cases, and return-kind subtypes. Report exact one-sided 97.5% binomial upper limits for B-only harm on the fresh cohort. With repeats, report case-clustered estimates and keep repeats out of the exact binomial denominator.

## Cost interpretation

Hard caps define joint feasibility. Actual whole-episode costs include every acquisition and checked path, regardless of success, and the common prefix if measuring full cost means. Report paired mean differences and simultaneous 95% intervals for TCP distance, accumulated turn and sampled motion/acquisition time. Strict equivalence means the full intervals lie inside ±2 mm, ±2° and ±0.10 s. No-worse cost means every upper interval end is at most +2 mm, +2° and +0.10 s. The strict classification is independent of faster results outside the lower equivalence margin. Include capture count, rail distance, stops, and any separate compute caps. Use Bonferroni over the three two-sided intervals and bootstrap all independent cases together.

Record both measured process CPU and online decision wall time, identifying warm-up, offline shared preprocessing and parallel resources. Report the sum of sampled execution and serialized measured decision delay as a separate sensitivity ledger where overlap is absent. A hardware-time conclusion requires measured online delay to advance the acoustic clock, or an independently justified timing model. If planning is instantaneous in the acoustic simulation, report that explicitly when interpreting its outcomes.
