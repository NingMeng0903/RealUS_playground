# Independent timing preflight review

The tested hold choices do not warrant a fresh timed B benchmark. They add up to one second to the same checked 1.5 s needed-site command. The only positive quantities below are differences in the public model's nominal continuation value. Complete-episode efficacy, actual wrong-identity outcomes and performance against full time-aware MPC remain unmeasured.

The same 128 prior-model draws enter both tasks and checkpoints. The model atom supplies the synthetic packets for each actual shared MPC prefix. The hold calculation uses the posterior over all 256 atoms, joint previous-claim mass, current progress, public robot state and remaining resources. Each hold pays 0, 1 or 2 half-second ticks under the nine-second and six-capture caps. Completion witnesses use joint costs under ideal future acceptance. Cases without a needed-site command at the checkpoint remain in their assigned denominator with zero contrast. The first checkpoint can encounter the additional temporary loss. The second starts any tested acquisition at 4.5 s or later, after that modeled loss window.

Independent replay regenerated all 512 public states, 768 causal packet/MPC commands, 1,083 eligible hold values and their witnesses, and the four summary cells. All likelihood and correct-joint arrays at original capture times matched exactly. The separate fine-tick tape seed prevents an old-grid/fine-grid innovation overlap in future execution. The correction preserved every archived public record and summary value because this prior-only preflight sampled packets on the old grid and evaluated holds by expectation.

The gain over the best fixed hold was below 0.005 nominal reward units in every cell. Added time is observed for the proposed next action, while subsequent real acquisition and completion costs are outside the preflight. The model schedules captures without planning latency. This evidence supports stopping the tested timing mechanism here and leaves other clocks, public sensing and executable continuation classes unresolved.
