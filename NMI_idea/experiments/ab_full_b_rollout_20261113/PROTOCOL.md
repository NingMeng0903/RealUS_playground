## Controllers

`MPC` is the common two-capture receding optimizer. It predicts first-packet updates using ten quantiles, evaluates a fixed second action, and estimates remaining task value using a geometric opportunity value and resource lower bounds. It reconditions and replans after every actual packet. The public belief also tracks per-hypothesis mass in which all prior claims are correct.

`B` searches short options with a cap of two or three fresh observations. It may hand off early on a task claim or a concentrated displacement-sign belief, seek a complementary acoustic view, or return to the reference after a miss. Each candidate is scored by a complete rollout, with the same live MPC policy taking over from the actual state reached. After handoff the public belief, prior-claim joint mass, robot realization, exact acquisition clock, task progress and remaining resources are retained. Direct MPC is always available. All virtual candidate programs share posterior draws and acquisition innovations. Screening and independent validation use 24 and 48 draws. The relative completion/risk/cost guard is model based and the sign test is a screening heuristic.

`one` uses the same rollout but permits one intervention. `fixed` uses the same short complementary library and full tail without planned observation-dependent switching. `status` removes geometric evidence from the option rules, while its MPC tail and acceptance use all real observations. `single_realization` restricts the option to a representative realization and retains all MPC actions after handoff. `unguarded` executes the top ranked structured option without the admission checks. It is an ablation of the guard.
