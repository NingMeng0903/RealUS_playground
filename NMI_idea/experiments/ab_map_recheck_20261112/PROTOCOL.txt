Frozen versus online material map during recheck
2026-11-12

Primary question

Does continued online updating of the material-map marginal after the same
first sweep change useful follow-up decisions and strict completion?
Primary comparison is online versus frozen in the combined stress condition.
All independent cases are included. The nominal condition is a check on
ordinary operation. The common case seed is the paired statistical unit.

Both estimators receive one complete 28-frame rule sweep. Each comparison
starts from a clone of this same posterior and executes three captures.
In the frozen arm the entire static map marginal from this sweep remains
fixed. Conditional transient and identity states still assimilate each new
mask. The online arm performs the same measurement update and also reweights
the static hypotheses. Both retain the local material correction basis,
global lateral correction and daughter-branch correction.

Budgets and policies

Shared recheck action slots are the frozen knee simulator's repeat, fork,
alternate window, junction, branch and wait candidates. Use the same baked
edge checks with cumulative limits 95 mm, 180 degrees and 3.30 seconds.
Duration includes translation/capture and a rotation allowance at 120 deg/s.
Reserve a capture interval for each remaining mandatory observation. Each
policy may use a different amount of the allowed budget. First-sweep data,
candidate function, belief likelihood, terminal cost and physical budgets
are shared. The two-step planner draws four first-action outcomes and two
at the tail, then replans after the actual observation. All terminal
positions are scored at the same cutoff with a common simulated pose packet.
No segmentation after the last action is allowed. This is endpoint-based
acoustic simulation with modeled travel and turn, without controller runs.

Offline and information ablations

Online+Fisher adds 0.10 times a bounded task-position covariance reduction
proxy with visibility weighting. This is a local continuous information
proxy and does not count discrete identity discrimination. CQL seeds 7 and
29 and FQI seed 7 train on the same fixed transitions, features, cost and
legal masks. Log every legal first action per case, then uniformly sample
legal continuations. The 256 training cases alternate nominal and stress.
Use the same budget and three captures for each fitted policy. Compare
each to the online two-step planner. These are secondary comparisons.

Conditions and outcomes

Stress combines a local material displacement, intermittent physically
timed loss and a persistent near-branch false structure. The synthetic
world is defined separately from the observation model. The random field
depends on case and physical capture coordinates, not the selected policy
or action count. One label-specific loss lasts 0.45–0.90 s. The burst is
anchored around the nominal fork arrival for 65% of cases, and at nominal
first-sweep end plus 1.1 s for 35%, with independent 0.32 s SD jitter.
A persistent material-frame displacement near a branch is projected
through the actual selected view; appearance can depend on the window.
Train and held-out seeds are disjoint. There are 128
nominal and 128 stress replays of the held-out subjects. The stress
parameters are fixed before these outcomes are examined.

Strict completion requires both branch identities confirmed correctly
at the common 0.87 threshold and a maximum world position error of 3 mm
on the frozen query addresses. Separately count wrong confirms, opposite
branch vs false structure, and unresolved cases. Historic witnesses can
contribute to this outcome. Each observed recheck is compared with a
shadow that has the same pose and time but no new mask. An immediately
non-improving recheck neither improves correct/wrong identity counts nor
reduces the query RMSE by at least 0.10 mm. Report identity-only
non-improvement too. This diagnoses immediate evidence, while an
observation can still enable later decisions. All methods take exactly
three frames, so the comparison is wasted-frame count within this quota.

Run both cross-replays with the exact real capture frames from the other
planner. This separates map interpretation from action selection. Report
individual routes, actual modeled travel/time/rotation, paired discordances,
case bootstrap intervals and the two-sided exact paired test for binary
outcomes. The 3D plot shows acquisition positions, not continuous QP or
skin-contact trajectories.
