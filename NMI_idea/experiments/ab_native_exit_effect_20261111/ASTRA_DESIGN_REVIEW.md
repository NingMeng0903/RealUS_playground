# Native exit effect: prospective design and independent review

Prepared 2026-11-11 before the new continuation matrix is inspected. Design and final independent interpretation: Astra. Root/workers own implementation and execution. This is a local native/QP simulation study, not a physical-robot or ultrasound-patient experiment.

## Why this is worth testing

A diagnostic probe movement changes not only the observation but also the complete robot state from which the intended next scan must start. The proposed test asks whether choosing that movement with its executable continuation in view avoids a later rejected continuation or unnecessary physical retreat. It does not assume every successful diagnostic exit is interchangeable, and it does not assume that a finite library's missing witness establishes globally impossible motion.

The existing controller already supplies the appropriate physical restrictions: joint/controller state, native QP tracking, 2 mm / 2 degree whole-segment tracking gates, a measured contact frame, a 3 mm synthetic contact-gap check, and the existing workspace envelope. Keep all of these unchanged. The existing constrained fixture is a synthetic public exclusion region; it is not a measured rib or patient obstacle. A rib-induced acoustic shadow alone would not justify inventing a mechanical wall. Open-mode controls test whether an apparent effect requires that synthetic exclusion region.

## Stage 3: fair decision comparison, only if mechanics activates

The matrix by itself can establish that actual diagnostic exits have different native continuation outcomes. It cannot establish that a B policy is better than A.

For a policy test, freeze public states from the first existing development subject IDs and public histories in canonical order before viewing their policy outcomes; preserve their fitted belief model, full posterior, candidate library, immediate native feasibility, acceptance, terminal opportunities and recovery. No selection by private target, favorable sampled noise, B wins, or failed A continuation. Use existing A_info (task-class information gain) and A_task (immediate valid completion with its existing tie rule) as distinct strong comparators. Log both even if one wins. Direct scanning remains available whenever their common public gate permits it.

The B-specific change is scoring a diagnostic action together with the same permitted task continuation conditional on its actual complete predicted/verified exit. The mechanical lookup is common public model information; it must not provide B private true target or true acoustic mode. Both methods can precheck and recover. The policy distinction is whether a candidate's subsequent task accessibility influences diagnostic selection, not whether A is forced to execute a known unsafe motion.

Use the existing acoustic likelihood and fitted full-belief implementation without manufacturing informative packets or deleting memory. Hold paired fresh-capture random streams fixed across policies; index them by actual capture count. Update from missing and clutter observations and accept any qualifying fresh frame under the same public rule. If two arms choose different informative probes, their actual differing packets must update their own beliefs; forcing equal packets would be a separate mechanical controlled comparison, not a faithful closed-loop comparison.

Before outcomes, save all candidate A/B values, chosen actions, full belief hash and the expected next-task law. A B nominal-exit ablation changes only the forecast exit and executes legally from the real exit, with the same common recovery. If a cheap native feasibility filter achieves the same benefit as full B, report that result; do not attribute its gain to soft belief integration or learned foresight.

For speed, stage 3 may initially be an exact fixed-public-state decision witness plus fresh execution of both chosen probe-and-continuation sequences. Such a witness is a conditional mechanism demonstration and has no population success-rate claim. A broader closed-loop result requires the entire predefined cohort, not a favorable example.

## Claim gates

1. Mechanical activation: at least two root-feasible probe exits differ for the SAME intended target in native continuation success or additional lawful recovery burden, and final exhibited chains pass fresh complete-state replay. If none differ, this fixture did not activate the proposed mechanism.
2. Decision activation: the frozen existing A and B definitions choose different actions at the SAME full public state because of the measured exit effect; show candidate tables. If actions agree, no policy advantage is demonstrated.
3. Practical benefit: B completes the same intended verified scan with fewer actual retreat segments/path or lower measured motion/acquisition burden, and A is credited with the same lawful best recovery. An avoided native planning rejection is separately counted; it is not a contact break or physical execution failure.
4. Safety and correctness: unchanged gates must pass; wrong public stops, root failures, unresolved scans and open-control losses remain in denominators. Do not trade unreported completion/errors for apparently fewer motion steps.
5. Generality: this is deterministic controller/library evidence in pre-existing synthetic fixtures. Claims about patient prevalence, ultrasound accuracy, real contact force, learned-model performance or clinical benefit remain untested. No new hard wall, deadline, memory reset or irrevocable failure may be introduced to generate a win.

## Primary literature supporting the scenario, not its measured prevalence

The practical concern is independently motivated. Yan et al., [Multi-Modal Interaction Control of Ultrasound Scanning Robots with Safe Human Guidance and Contact Recovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05685) (2023), explicitly implement a recovery mode that re-establishes probe/body contact and validate a collaborative carotid scanning task. Zhang et al., [Tactile-Guided Robotic Ultrasound: Mapping Preplanned Scan Paths for Intercostal Imaging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20282) (IROS 2025), construct scanning trajectories within intercostal acoustic windows and adjust probe tilt, with experiments on four phantoms. Marahrens et al., [Towards Autonomous Robotic Minimally Invasive Ultrasound Scanning and Vessel Reconstruction on Non-Planar Surfaces](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9594548/) (2022), combine contact maintenance and orientation estimation during anatomical navigation and demonstrate a convex vessel phantom scan.

Inference: a diagnostic pose change can leave a controller in a state with different continuation/recovery burden, so the proposed local mechanical test is relevant to robotic ultrasound. None of these sources establishes the present fixture's wall geometry, the frequency of its failed native routes, or superiority of the present B policy. The current simulation must independently supply that evidence and must not borrow a real contact-recovery claim for its synthetic in-contact retreat.

## Algebraic design correction: one-attempt admission cannot activate

Before the new acoustic decision outcomes, root identified and Astra independently verified a structural limitation of the proposed one-attempt fast witness. The fitted model has target detection d=0.8067415730, present-to-lost a=0.0291933851, lost-to-present b=0.7201834862, and applies a visibility transition before each acquisition. Even with perfect geometry, quality and identity, the largest one-attempt correct mass is d*(1-a)=0.7831900556. Because admission uses min(raw, calibrated)>=0.90, no posterior can admit a one-attempt terminal. Thus primary admitted continuation score is identically zero; only its raw-value tie-break could change a diagnostic choice. A one-attempt scored witness cannot demonstrate the intended admitted continuation mechanism.

The unchanged original two-attempt option is not ruled out. Omitting all geometry/quality losses and absorbing false stops, its best upper bound is d*p1+d*(p1*(1-d)*(1-a)+(1-p1)*b)=0.9470908930 at initially present probability one, p1=1-a. The calibrated value there is 0.9331548301. These are ceilings, not achievable probabilities for the actual posterior. Root may restore the original two-attempt option and certify every retry needed by any scored candidate branch, keeping the original gate. This restores shared task semantics; it must not lower the gate or claim unverified retry access. Record earlier one-attempt computations as the structural-null diagnostic, not a discarded B loss. ## Retrospective full-state mechanism and matched-dwell regression

The following block was triggered by an archived difficult history, so it is an exploratory mechanism regression, not an independently sampled B-win cohort. Both initial archive states are valid complete exits. After four versus five consecutive right-side .60 acquisitions, TCP translation differs by only 0.0000123691 mm and orientation by 0.0000002341 degrees; rail changes by 0.177778 mm and the largest arm-joint change is 0.0543694 degrees, with additional controller-history differences. Fresh native replay from each exact state requests the SAME side_left .60 action and tries the same two existing route alternatives. After four, round_tip and retreat_fork fail the existing 2 mm whole-segment tracking gate at 3.993224 and 2.210762 mm. After five, round_tip still fails at 4.054151 mm, but retreat_fork passes at 1.935659 mm. Independent recomputation of all recorded route maxima agrees exactly. This demonstrates that practically identical Cartesian endpoints are insufficient to determine the controller's checked continuation set. It does not isolate whether q, velocity, acceleration, warm start, or their joint history is causal, and it does not show global unreachability or a physical contact break.

Before the matched-dwell add-on outcomes, four alternatives were frozen at the SAME earlier three-right-.60 full exit: direct side_left .60, or one right-side scout of .15/.30/.60 followed by that same left action. The direct control passes in 4.150 s. The .15 and .30 scouts themselves and their left continuations pass, totaling 4.975 and 5.125 s. The .60 scout passes but leaves the exact after-four state above, from which the checked left continuation has no witness. All four outcomes are retained. These results are a useful engineering regression: even legal extra acquisition dwell can change subsequent controller tracking at essentially unchanged TCP. They do not imply that .15/.30 provides the same information as .60. When the next target is already known, the direct control is strongest and must be credited. The need for a further measurement, and B's willingness to trade sensing quality against continuation, must be established by a subsequent complete-belief task comparison. No adequate final ultrasound image was measured in this mechanical block.
