implementation of that design and the chosen simulation scope.

Fixture. Two mirrored variants offset the wall center by ±1.1 mm and swap
the longitudinal window offset (±0.8 mm). The fixed-q fixture comes from the
previous public RM75 q2_+1 layout. The atlas patch frame is taken from the
actual zero-depth harmonic outer skin at a fixed 30-degree pose and placed
rigidly onto that public seed; a private synthetic local surface perturbation
is sensed by noisy 81-point measurements and fitted by the existing estimator.
The sensed frame, not the private surface, generates the robot reference.
Physical acceptance uses the private simulated surface and actual FK pose.
Obstruction checks use a probe-envelope planar forbidden region and checks
on segments between native ticks. Native self-collision is checked per tick.
This ideal joint-feedback simulator does not model external full-arm contact,
tissue force, or raster ultrasound.

Actuation. A full SE(3) reference is driven by the existing integral-basis
ProDMP phase, with analytic pose and six-dimensional velocity input at each
native tick. Tilt is rotation about the contact chart's local x axis.
Complete controller histories are restored from prefix replay, including
q, qdot, acceleration and native configuration. The native 2 mm / 2 degree
whole-segment tracking and zero-pause gate is retained, with a synthetic
3 mm geometry-only contact gap. No extra jerk cut is used. Public pilot with
stationary or ±4 mm fast rotation failed tracking, documented in the public
engineering log. The fork moved to ±6 mm x, -3 mm y, ±.075 rad tilt;
base motion at least .80 s, tilt reversal at least 1.10 s. Other transfers
use a shared .55 s minimum from the geometry-only engineering pilot.
Side probes and terminal positions are both offered. Central single and
either paired order are offered. Crossovers via both public tip/retreat
routes are tried in the public catalog, with dIRD batched ranking.
All planned arms use the same checked public catalog. The physical layer
independently reruns chosen routes at each distinct exact prefix before
reusing identical deterministic transitions; physical motion cannot rewind
on a failure.

Observation model. Uses a continuous local vessel curve with bounded offsets
and a common material patch response, w SD (.065,.23). Actual tilt s is
normalized by .10 rad. The beam intercept y - depth*tan(tilt) gives the
declared simple projected geometry. Quality score baseline .64 is chosen
near the fixed .58 threshold for this constructed mechanism stress.
The two terminal feature gates are center <=4.5 mm and view error <=.22 rad;
no claim is made about visible length or a raster-image gate. Probe physical
support is 22 mm. A true associated packet is drawn with probability .8
when supported; otherwise a 0.1 spurious probability after a miss. Candidate
confidence uses the same quality mapping for target and clutter. Missing
packets carry no target scalar. The same actual terminal frame passes only
when it contains the correct target feature and passes the geometry and
quality gates. Correct public stopping and false stopping are separate.
Models approximate unknown true geometry with nominal g_geo and a bounded
prior; probability forecasts use mixture association and fixed predictive
quadrature. Gaussian natural parameters are stored per association component.
No learned-prior or network-generalization claim is tested. One known
stationary patch spans all poses in this fixture. The reset control draws
new private w at the start of the terminal stage with the planner unaware.

construction is reported separately. The online library has at most two
probe acquisitions in a macro and at most two fresh terminal acquisitions.

complete schedule cannot fit. Each legal first-probe program is also forced
for same-prefix packet masking. Time-insufficient forced branches are kept
