FROZEN LOCAL CLOSED-LOOP A/B PROTOCOL
Design owner: Astra; implementation and execution are separate.
Date: 2026-11-11. This file defines a simulation experiment, not a result.

1. CLAIM, SCOPE, AND FREEZE

The testable claim is that, for a known acquisition task and a common bounded
anatomical prior, new acoustic observations can change which checked continuation
is useful, and anticipating the actual state and remaining time after a probe can
change which probe is worth executing. The claim is conditional on the sensing
model, the available program library, and the declared controller. It does not
claim a new Bellman equation, global terminal controllability, clinical ultrasound
performance, or universal superiority of a more elaborate policy.

The primary outcome is acquisition of the first NEW, privately accepted task
image/observation before a fixed deadline. Planning success probabilities, old
probe packets, endpoint IK witnesses, and safe withdrawal are not completion.

Freeze this protocol, final numeric public geometry/program manifest, model
manifest, source hashes, and random seeds before generating held-out acoustic
outcomes. Public geometry-only engineering may repair an invalid robot placement
or unsupported reference before that freeze. Record every attempted placement and
the public engineering rejection reason. Do not retain a placement because B
wins, because an acoustic outcome looks favorable, or because a comparator fails.
Any changed model, deadline rule, candidate, or threshold after test outcomes is
an explicitly dated amendment and a new experiment, not a silent replacement.

No patient prescan is available. Initial public inputs are the known requested
target/view, atlas/material anchor and bounded prior, calibrated robot state,
known fixture geometry, and current synthetic surface sensor data. Hidden anatomy
and acoustic latents enter only the simulator/evaluator. All arms receive the
same initial public information and use the same motion/checking machinery.

2. THREE RELATIONS AND THEIR LIMITED INTERPRETATION

Response, for an actual acquisition pose A and material address xi:

  Y = g_geo(S, Fhat^{-1} A) + H(Fhat^{-1} A) w_xi + epsilon,
  H = [1, s],   s = signed measured local tilt / 0.10 rad.

S is a public anatomical hypothesis when predicting, and private anatomy only
when generating/evaluating data. Fhat is fitted from current sensor points; it
is not the private true frame. g_geo is evaluated from geometry at the actual
probe pose. A diagnostic channel may use a declared non-target tissue response,
but its physical support and relation to target quality must be specified. Do
not assign visibility or a diagnostic label merely because an action is named
"central" or "informative".

For conditionally independent, correctly associated Gaussian observations:

  Lambda_xi = Lambda_xi^- + sum H_i^T R_i^{-1} H_i,
  eta_xi    = eta_xi^-    + sum H_i^T R_i^{-1}(Y_i-g_i).

Keep event IDs, acquisition addresses, actual poses, contact and calibration
versions. A frame is counted once. This natural state is exact only for its
conditional Gaussian component. Missing detections and unlabelled false
positives require their mixture likelihood; do not feed all positive detections
through an unconditional Gaussian update and call it exact. An exact small
Gaussian mixture or a fixed quadrature/particle posterior is acceptable. If an
approximation is used, freeze its support and report normalization/prediction
checks against a denser calculation on development data.

A two-dimensional shared w is permitted only as an explicit locally stationary
PATCH assumption spanning the two surface windows. Record the distinct sampled
addresses within that patch. Otherwise keep separate address states, or a frozen
joint prior with declared cross-address covariance. Observing one independent
window does not update another. Response-reset controls test this assumption.

Task value:

  V(a | b,x,r) = - E[c(a,O)]
    + E_{O=(Y,x',tau,flags) ~ K(.|b,x,a)}
      [ max_{pi in Pi_checked(x',r-tau)} J_fresh(pi | b^+(O),x',r-tau) ].

The maximization is by PUBLIC history, not independently by private world.
Equal public histories induce equal actions. Already successful/terminated
branches get their actual value. Unknown paths have no executable witness and
cannot be released. Missing a witness is not proof of global infeasibility.
The present deterministic mechanical simulator may make the exit/time component
of K degenerate. Such a result tests action-specific continuation accounting;
it does not alone establish a benefit of a nonfactorized stochastic kernel.

3. PUBLIC INTERFACES AND STATE CONTINUITY

physics.run(program, entry_state, fixture, measured_frame) returns:
  trace; complete exit_state; elapsed model time; mechanical/contact flags;
  newly generated public acoustic packet(s); checked/rejected/unknown status.

entry_state and exit_state include actual q, qdot, acceleration/history required
by the controller, model-clock phase, actual contact mode, and any controller
internal state needed to reproduce continuation. The target pose ID is not the
state. Never reset qdot, acceleration, elapsed time, or controller memory at a
program boundary unless a real checked settling segment has produced that state.

The planner API receives public observation packets, current calibrated state,
fixture geometry, measured frame, prior/posterior, candidate manifests, and
remaining time. It receives no true/false association flag, private centerline,
hidden acoustic coefficient, correct terminal label, or evaluator success bit.

The private evaluator receives private anatomy, actual pose/trace, and a fresh
acquisition payload. Its function does not receive policy/arm identity. The
planner can stop on a public detector criterion; false public stops remain
failures and are counted. Do not silently correct them with private truth.

Identical deterministic mechanical prefixes may be cached. Cache identity must
include the entire measured entry state, controller/binary/config hashes,
reference, fixture, contact/frame version, clock and all relevant history.
Static endpoint q witnesses are not native exit states. Cache hit rates and
native executions are reported separately. Cached outcome means replay of the
same deterministic transition, not a physically new acoustic acquisition.

4. CURRENT CONTACT FRAME AND PUBLIC FIXTURE

Use the production atlas skin/material anchor already available in
ab_live_surface_20261111/run_geometry.py. Save the anchor ID, side, pose and asset
hash. Fit current contact with that file's estimate_contact: observed plane fit,
public approach-line intersection, and a public task tangent projected onto the
estimated tangent plane. Reject degenerate fit/support/incidence/tangent cases.
All policies use this same accepted measured frame. The true displaced surface
is private to simulation/evaluation.

The reference sensor recipe is a 9 by 9 point patch over +/-10 mm, independent
0.25 mm coordinate noise, and a declared private surface displacement/tilt.
Use fixed public development seeds to obtain the final accepted fixture(s);
record offsets, surface equation, sensor data, fitted frame and fit residuals.
Do not fit the frame directly to private true normals or private contact points.

The implementation's proposed local fixture, in the fitted tangent chart, is:
  common fork/start:      (0, 0) mm;
  terminal windows:       (-16,18), (+16,18) mm;
  side diagnostic sites:  (-16,13), (+16,13) mm;
  baffle footprint:       x in [-4,4], y in [10,34] mm.
The nominal direct crossover through the baffle is forbidden by swept geometry,
not an action-ID blacklist. Routes around its lower end and every other supported
retreat/return/crossover route remain available to EVERY arm. A mirrored fixture
interchanges left/right constraint roles using the same published rule. Freeze
the probe envelope, inflated clearance, waypoints, fixture transform and all
pose/tilt values in the final manifest. The final manifest must show that an
actual acoustic channel has support at each diagnostic site; with a narrow
yaw-only image plane this is not guaranteed by the coordinates above.

It is legitimate for a central diagnostic to take longer than a side diagnostic
if it is a distinct acquisition program with its own physical location, signed
tilt, dwell and observation law. It is NOT legitimate to manufacture an advantage
by defining a longer probe as the identical side probe plus a mandatory return
while withholding that return from the shorter probe's continuations. With the
same evidence and all recovery routes, the shorter program can choose that
return after observing and weakly dominates the mandatory-return version.

These are known synthetic workspace/contact exclusions, not measured patient
ribs. State precisely what collision model is implemented. Probe-envelope versus
fixture clearance does not certify the rest of the robot against that fixture.
If full external-link geometry is unavailable, qualify the result as native
whole-segment robot/self-collision plus swept probe-fixture checking; do not call
it full external-environment collision certification.

5. MOTION AND PHYSICAL ACCEPTANCE

Use original integral-basis ProDMP references with actual measured entry pose
and entry velocity, transformed through Fhat, and preserve their analytic timing
and feed-forward twists. The current native controller can be used offline;
no hardware session is required. Baked dIRD ranks candidate searches, and every
arm uses the same database, ordering, query budget and fallback. dIRD score or
endpoint reachability never substitutes for a whole-segment check.

Before execution, a candidate must have a checked route from the ACTUAL entry.
Check motion, dwell, transition/settling and recovery, not only moving endpoints:
  - same-tick FK(q_meas_before[t]) against desired pose[t];
  - configured physical joint position/velocity/acceleration/jerk limits;
  - actual derivative continuity across adjoining segments;
  - configured self-collision and available external collision geometry;
  - swept probe-envelope/fixture clearance, including between-sample clearance;
  - synthetic contact geometry/support and declared contact mode;
  - elapsed model time, endpoint tolerance and controller-state validity.

Retain the established motion gate of maximum 2 mm translation and maximum
2 degrees orientation error where the native reference is the intended target.
The stricter true-contact/image gate remains separate. Use the controller's
actual physical limits, not new posture preferences chosen for this experiment.
For between-tick clearance, use a conservative swept/interpolated envelope with
a recorded maximum spacing/error bound; discrete checking alone is labelled
sampled, not continuous certification. A tracking or constraint failure cannot
be relabelled successful because a later endpoint happens to be good.

If a requested segment cannot be checked, record rejected or unknown and execute
a common checked stop/withdrawal when available. Safe withdrawal beyond deadline
remains a task timeout. The contact model is geometric: without a force/contact
plant, this study does not establish force regulation or stable physical contact.

6. CANDIDATES, BUDGET, AND THE 2 BY 2

All methods share direct acquisition at both windows, the supported side
diagnostic(s), central diagnostic/pair, fixed paired tilts, all supported routing
and recovery primitives, and the same terminal retry rule. A program may contain
multiple primitive segments; its acquisition events and end state are explicit.
Freeze the complete finite library and canonical lexicographic tie break.

Acquisition timing and noise:
  side single diagnostic dwell:       0.15 s;
  central pair:                       0.30 s at EACH signed tilt;
  fixed pair:                         same per-tilt dwell as central pair;
  terminal fresh acquisition dwell:    1.00 s;
  diagnostic tilt reference:           -0.025 and +0.025 rad;
  terminal tilt reference:             -0.10 and +0.10 rad, when supported;
  sensor SD per independent acquisition:
    0.035 * sqrt(0.15 s / actual acquisition dwell).

Include every actual travel, tilt, native settle and acquisition duration. A
central pair is not assigned an arbitrary extra penalty or free return. If the
native dwell/reference requires a different executable timing, freeze the
physical timing and record an engineering amendment before test outcomes; keep
the above declared acquisition time distinct from servo settling overhead.

Primary horizon: at most one diagnostic program, with at most two acoustic probe
acquisitions, followed by direct terminal acquisitions. At most two terminal
attempts may be made if remaining time admits both. A terminal failure is followed
by the same actual-state greedy legal recovery rule in every arm. No method loses
an already supported retreat/crossover edge. Longer H3 search, if run, is a
separate labelled sensitivity using identical physical and compute budgets.

I=1: after the selected diagnostic program, use all its new acoustic packets.
I=0: replace that program's entire acoustic packet(s), including acoustic
missing/detection/quality flags, with a constant mask. Retain actual q, qdot,
controller state, elapsed time, and public mechanical/contact status. If those
retained variables themselves reveal information, condition on their likelihood;
do not pretend that I=0 means absolutely no new information. Terminal acquisition
packets remain readable in all arms for their common recovery rule. Existing
legal prior history is identical across arms.

F=1: PRE-probe scoring uses the candidate-specific predicted executed exit state
and checked continuation times from that state, with the actual program timing
model and remaining budget. In deterministic mechanics these may be public
native calibration replays, explicitly labelled calibrated-model predictions.
F=0: PRE-probe scoring uses one common nominal post-probe state at the fork and
its common continuation-time model. It still pays each candidate's known actual
reference/prefix time. This factor deliberately removes action-specific future
exit/continuation accounting; it does not hide obviously known command duration.

After real execution, BOTH F arms discard any nominal assumed entry and check/
replan from actual q, qdot, controller memory and remaining time. F=0 must never
execute a fictitious edge. The factors only alter information use and first-stage
forecasting. Share observation laws, priors, candidates, transition checks,
search limits and numerical integration accuracy.

The four labels are I0F0, I1F0, I0F1, I1F1. Do not call I1F0 an independent strong
baseline without naming its deliberate nominal-exit ablation.

Additional baselines:
  A_greedy: full public posterior/history and actual q/time; choose the best
    checked immediate terminal acquisition by the same success-minus-time
    objective, then use identical public-feedback legal retries. No lookahead
    through future diagnostic observations. This is the strong immediate A.
  A_geometry: same executable immediate routes and public image feedback, but
    analytical geometric prior/servo without online acoustic residual learning.
  Fixed_pair: fixed minus/plus pair at the frozen supported diagnostic site;
    order chosen once using public motion cost, before outcomes. Then use the
    same posterior and actual-state terminal/recovery selector as I1F1.
    Report canonical minus-then-plus as a secondary order if order is material.

Fixed_pair matching I1F1 is an informative result: it limits any claimed need for
adaptive probe selection. A_greedy outperforming B is retained, not repaired away.

7. DEADLINES AND UTILITY

Let D_L,D_R be the fastest checked actual time from the shared start to the end
of the first fresh terminal acquisition, including route, settle and dwell. Let
P_min be the minimum time to complete a legal diagnostic program from that start.
The public geometry-only rule is:

  T0 = max(D_L,D_R) + P_min.

If one terminal has no witness, record that mechanical restriction before
private draws; do not replace infinity by an arbitrary successful time. Retain
the case with a declared finite operational deadline or mark it as a pretest
engineering rejection, consistently across arms and with the reason recorded.
The intended main fixtures have both direct terminal routes witnessed.
All-arm sensitivities are 1.5*T0 and 2*T0. Never choose the most favorable deadline
as the main result. No acoustic outcome participates in this deadline rule.

Planning utility is:

  J = P(first new accepted acquisition by T)
      - 0.04 s^{-1} * E[min(elapsed time to terminal task outcome, T)].

Failures/timeouts are charged T for restricted-time reporting; successful first
acquisition time is retained even if a public detector does not immediately
recognize it. Also report correctness of the submitted/stopped-on acquisition.
Avoid double-counting prefix time in the recursion. Primary inference uses the
binary timely-acquisition outcome, not a post hoc tuned utility coefficient.

A separate, explicitly CONSTRUCTIVE PUBLIC-GEOMETRY WITNESS is allowed. For a
central program set C to the maximum, over L/R, of its prefix time plus fastest
checked continuation to that terminal plus fresh acquisition. For a specified
side program set S to its prefix plus fastest checked cross-window completion,
minimizing over ALL supported returns/detours/recoveries. If S-C exceeds two
controller ticks plus declared timing uncertainty, freeze Tw=(C+S)/2 before
private outcomes. Otherwise report that no timing witness exists. Perform the
same rule in both mirrored fixtures and retain both. Tw does not replace T0.

This construction witnesses timing separation in the frozen checked library.
It becomes an actual fresh-acquisition fail-to-succeed example only when a
positive-probability public observation branch selects a physically executed,
privately accepted new frame. Timing alone does not establish image success.
Report the entire constructed scenario and all arms, with no inferential claim
from selecting an existence example. It is not proof that every continuous
controller is incapable of a faster completion.

8. HIDDEN ANATOMY, ACOUSTICS, MISSES, AND FALSE POSITIVES

Use a bounded public prior tied to the production material patch and known task,
not the private offset anatomy. The previous local residual scene's parameter
order is (y0, y_slope, y_curvature, depth, z_slope, z_curvature), with baseline
(0.00002,0.008,0.22,0.034,0.006,0.40) in metres and compatible derivatives.
Any translation/rotation needed to place this vessel through the present task
ROI belongs to the public anatomy manifest and is applied equally to the prior
and private draws. Do not silently use the original y approximately zero curve
with displaced windows that cannot see it.

Freeze bounded offsets before test. The recommended coarse-patch support is
y0 +/-3 mm, depth +/-4 mm, y_slope +/-0.08, z_slope +/-0.04, and the previous
curvature halfwidths 0.35 and 0.22 per metre. This is a declared synthetic local
prior, not an empirical population prior. If the implementation instead retains
the much narrower old residual_scene prior, label that restricted scope and
include a wider-offset sensitivity; do not describe it as broad patient anatomy.

The frozen primary residual prior is zero-mean Gaussian with SDs (0.065,0.23)
for intercept and normalized tilt slope, with zero prior cross-covariance. Use
this known simulator prior, shared by every arm; this is a model-based test and
does not claim learned-prior generalization. A separately labelled sensitivity
may reuse the existing independently fitted public prior. No new neural model
is justified here. If fitting new moments in a future experiment, use separate
training subjects. Do not train on held-out private geometry or select latent
coefficients to produce a desired arm.

Reference detector mixture for each fresh packet:
  genuine association probability p_real = 0.80 * physical_support;
  if genuine association is absent, spurious-packet probability = 0.10;
  otherwise return missing (no scalar value).
physical_support is computed from true acoustic/image geometry in simulation
and integrated over public anatomical hypotheses by the planner. A supported
true signal has quality from g_geo + H w + fresh Gaussian sensor noise. A
spurious candidate has quality N(0.12,0.08^2), independent of true identity and
residual unless a different frozen clutter law is explicitly stated. Its packet
does not reveal that it is spurious. Thus, for supported observations, real,
spurious and missing probabilities are .80,.02,.18; unsupported observations
have spurious/missing probabilities .10,.90. Use the complete likelihood in the
policy model. The private association flag is evaluator-only.

Freeze geometric acceptance at the previous local gates: correct intended
target, absolute image offset <=2 mm, true visible geometric length >=15 mm,
view-angle error <=10 degrees, and realized fresh true-target quality >=0.
Acceptance must use the actual rendered view/FK pose and actual contact support,
not the target command or nominal geometry. A positive spurious detector packet
cannot satisfy target identity. A missed detector may still have captured a
qualifying true image; distinguish first qualified acquisition from public stop.

Every terminal acquisition has a new event ID and independently keyed image/noise
stream. Probe packets are never reused as terminal pixels or quality. Share
common random numbers across arms by subject, physical acquisition parameters,
visit index, and image-noise family, not by wall-clock call order. Same-action
same-visit potential outcomes may be paired; distinct or repeated acquisitions
still receive distinct event keys. Log keys without exposing private labels.

If only geometric/image features are generated, describe the endpoint as
"blinded synthetic feature-observation acceptance". Do not imply human image
grading or validated raw ultrasound quality. If pixels are rendered, archive
representative fresh images and the method-blind evaluator rule, including
false positives and misses rather than only successful examples.

9. COHORTS AND NEGATIVE CONTROLS

Public engineering seed: 721411; at most 16 public-only fixture/pose attempts.
Held-out anatomy seed: 721419; observation seed family: 721420.
Use 128 independently drawn hidden anatomies, paired across the two mirrored
constrained layouts and all methods. A shared anatomy across layouts remains
one statistical cluster, not two independent subjects. Mechanical/contact
fixtures may be reused to make exact native replay tractable, but their number
must be reported separately from the 128 acoustic/anatomical draws. This is
conditional evidence for those fixtures, not 128 independently placed robots.

Required controls, using the same latent draws where pairing is meaningful:
  Open: remove the public baffle/slot constraint; recompute all checked routes
    and retain the same primary absolute deadline for the causal opening check.
    Also report the open-layout T0 rule separately if desired.
  Longer deadline: 1.5*T0 and 2*T0, all arms, same mechanics/acoustics.
  Uninformative: diagnostic packet distribution independent of task-relevant
    latent anatomy/quality, while retaining mechanical exit/time information.
    A rational model aware of this manipulation should not assign information
    value to those packets; information-factor increments should vanish in
    expectation, though finite sampled outcomes may differ.
  All missing: mask or generate every diagnostic acoustic packet as missing in
    both informed and masked arms; with identical retained state/time and the
    correct model, their deterministic decisions should agree.
  Response reset: redraw slope/residual at the terminal or other window using
    the declared prior, after the probe. A reset-aware model marginalizes the
    new response; blind reuse is a separate failure diagnostic.

Do not demand exact equality of F arms in an open layout: actual distances and
times can still differ. Opening is a valid null for F only when all relevant
continuation costs/feasible actions truly become the common nominal model.
Do not demand equality after a long deadline if the utility retains a time cost.

10. SAME-FIRST REAL CONTINUATIONS

For EACH supported side and central probe program, execute the first program
once from the common entry and save the full actual simulator/controller state
and realized public packets. Clone that exact post-probe state into I=1 and I=0
branches, changing only packet visibility. Execute both selected continuations
through the native simulator/checker and acquire new terminal observations.

The branches share actual prefix q, qdot, controller memory, contact, elapsed
time, acoustic realization and fixture. The I=0 branch still sees public state
and time. A counterfactual probability computed without executing a continuation
is not a real same-first continuation. Deterministic replay from an exact cached
state is acceptable and must carry its trace and fresh acquisition event.
Predeclare all tested first programs; do not report only the one with a positive
information effect. Same-first information effects can be zero at some exits.

11. ANALYSIS AND REQUIRED RECORDS

Primary paired effect: success(I1F1)-success(I1F0) at T0, averaged first over the
two fixed layouts within each independent anatomy. This isolates anticipating
future execution when new observations are used. Also report success(I1F1)-
success(I0F1), all four cell means, and the interaction:
  [I1F1-I0F1] - [I1F0-I0F0].
These are prespecified companion estimates, not a search over significance.
Report separate contrasts versus A_greedy, A_geometry and Fixed_pair without
silently changing the primary comparison. Report every layout/control and
deadline, including losses and null results.

Use paired, subject-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals with 10,000 resamples
and seed 721421, resampling the 128 anatomy IDs with all their layouts, arms and
paired noises together. Give raw paired discordance counts. For one-layout
binary comparisons an exact paired sign/McNemar diagnostic is permissible, but
do not treat repeated layouts/noise replicates as independent trials. Prefer
effect sizes and 95% intervals. If multiple confirmatory p-values are claimed,
prespecify a correction; otherwise label companion contrasts descriptive. No
power or general significance claim follows from the constructive witness.

Record at least: first fresh accepted acquisition by deadline; correct public
stop/submission; false stop; timeout; failure charged deadline; actual first
acquisition time; number of new probes and terminal frames; missing and spurious
packet rates (private audit only); chosen first program and terminal; predicted
versus realized success/time; all mechanical rejects and unknowns; tracking,
joint/derivative/collision/contact margins; recovery attempts; checked witness
coverage; native and cached execution counts; planning wall time and query count.

The primary clock is native/model execution time plus acquisition/settle time.
Report online planning wall time separately and include a sensitivity charging
it to task time when measurable comparably. Offline graph construction/calibration
is a disclosed setup cost. A budget of expensive checks cannot be granted only
to B. Do not imply a real-time implementation if a policy used offline exhaustive
branch replays that a comparator did not receive.

12. INTERPRETATION RULES

A clean positive mechanism result requires: actual observation-dependent legal
branching; real fresh acceptance; a matched same-first information effect at an
appropriate exit; and an F effect explained by executed state/time differences,
not missing comparator edges or hidden truth. Calibration errors and missed/
false detections must remain in the outcomes. A strong fixed pair or greedy A
matching B limits the practical contribution even if the finite mechanism works.

A null/negative overall result is a valid result of this frozen experiment.
Separate insufficient information, physical delay, unobserved target geometry,
model mismatch, native tracking failure, and search-without-witness. Do not turn
an implementation failure into evidence against all possible policies, or a
hand-constructed timing witness into general empirical superiority.

The previous exact finite-graph results remain algebraic counterexamples. The
new native simulation adds only the claims actually checked here. The Kobayashi
example motivates separating identification from fixed-controller completion;
its reported 22/50 at 50 steps, 3/50 at 200 steps, and early-risk 44% to 86%
must be cited/verified against that paper by the reporting author, and are not
new results of this protocol.

PRE-PRIVATE ENGINEERING NOTE

The implementation owner reports that a pure stationary central rotation failed
the existing native position-tracking gate, while translated/rotated paths passed
its public pilot. Before private acoustic outcomes, replacing that unsupported
reference by small central-corridor sweeps (approximately +/-3 to 5 mm translation
with signed rotations up to +/-0.075 rad) is allowed as an explicit engineering
amendment. Retain the failed pure-rotation attempt and all traces. Freeze exact
new poses and durations in the final program manifest; use their ACTUAL signed
tilt in H, preserve exact SE(3) timing, and keep the same 2 mm/2 degree gate.
The final manifest, not an idealized rotation-only reference, defines the tested
action. This note records design authorization, not independent validation of
the reported pilot.

If the resulting central program exceeds T0, it remains too slow at T0. Do not
increase T0 to include it. The frozen 1.5*T0 and 2*T0 sensitivities can reveal
deadline dependence. If the public constructive-witness gap is nonpositive,
report that no such timing witness was obtained rather than alter the fixture
or suppress a supported side-probe recovery after observing private outcomes.
