Held-knee w/z separation diagnostic — final 2026-11-12

Scope
-----
This is a paired estimator diagnostic only.  It reuses the frozen atlas and
rule sweep from ../ab_knee_latent_rl_20261112 and trains no RL model.  The
final split is the exact frozen-test indices 384–511 (128 cases), disjoint
from the earlier 0–255 and 256–383 reports.  The complete index-to-seed map is
in summary/SEEDS.json.

Data convention and schedule
----------------------------
Each scalar-w case has one shared 28-frame rule sweep (the full 28-site route),
one common legal preposition capture at trunk site i=8, and one common measured
15-degree stationary-stop frame.  The preposition and stop are generated with
the frozen world travel/capture clock.  The knee follows its frozen sinusoidal
trajectory until the first q=15 crossing after preposition, then is held at
q=15, qdot=0.  This is an explicit abrupt-stop diagnostic; it does not claim
continuous deceleration between the last moving frame and the hold.

Four paired arms then acquire exactly three frames, with the same first and
third physical times and a one-second difference in the middle slot:

  early/burst, one response:   (8, 8, 8)
  early/burst, two response:   (8, 11, 8)
  late/separated, one response: (8, 8, 8)
  late/separated, two response: (8, 11, 8)

The (8,11,8) travel is legal under the frozen eligible geometry (about
0.721 s minimum per leg including capture); waits fill the exact common slot.
Atlas response profiles are 0.806581 at site 8 and 0.540771 at site 11.
The public frames include the frozen pose noise.  All four arms use the same
prior and the same observations in their paired Filter instances.

Estimands and controls
----------------------
The primary truth is a scalar global w within the frozen W-axis support.  The
held response has case-specific tau (0.496–1.338 s over this split) and a
generated stationary process (truth-only).  `latent` is Filter(latent=True)
with all frames.  `no_lag_plus_US` is Filter(latent=False) with the identical
frames and w,b prior.  `latent_no_post_stop_US` keeps the latent filter but
propagates the shared prefix/stop posterior to the common final clock without
the three post-stop ultrasound frames.  z at the stop is a fixed-lag smoother
using the complete prefix, stop frame, and post-stop frames; it is explicitly
labelled smoothed rather than online.

At common final T, w and z are reported separately.  World 3D prediction is
scored at T+0.5 and T+1.0 s on the frozen query locations.  CIs in
summary/SUMMARY.json are deterministic 2,000-resample case bootstraps; paired
contrasts resample the within-case differences.

The truth RMS at common T is only 0.402 mm for z, so z_T error is a late-state
metric and should not be read as recovery of the start state.  The paired

the two-response versus one-site burst z_start difference is -0.009 mm (95%
CI -0.023, 0.006).  These modest intervals do not establish a large spatial
identifiability gain.

Known limitations
-----------------
This is a synthetic centroid/dropout model, not raw ultrasound or clinical
data.  The abrupt stop is a controlled intervention, and truth-only process
noise is not learned.  Sites 8/11 are trunk sites, so this diagnostic avoids
identity confusion and cannot stand in for the Y-branch association task.
The scalar-w primary removes site mismatch by construction; per-site mismatch
is not a primary estimand.  A fixed finite parameter grid and the frozen
latent prior remain in force.  Late z has small physical amplitude, and the
3D query score averages atlas points rather than validating a continuous
surface or a physical probe.
