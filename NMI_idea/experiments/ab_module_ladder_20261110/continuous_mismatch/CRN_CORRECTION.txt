Pre-release CRN correction, 2026-11-10

The initial unexecuted runner key included the method label:
``('continuous_mismatch', condition, object_index, method)``.  That would
give paired methods different renderer noise even when they selected the
same action, violating the intended common-random-number comparison.

Correction: the runner now uses exactly
``('continuous_mismatch', condition, object_index, 'a0_identity')`` for both
methods.  ``run_episode`` still appends action ordinal and rounded actual
pose internally, so same-object/condition/ordinal/same-pose observations
share noise while different actions may differ.  Persistent GT world and
nuisance state are unchanged.  No GT was opened and no strategy episode was
executed before this correction.
