Scope and exclusion
-------------------
This is an explicitly invalid-policy diagnostic upper bound.  The uppercase
marker ``ORACLE_LEAKED_TRUTH`` is mandatory in every result.  It is not a
legal A/B experiment, not a prescan, not a controller change, and must never
be merged into the formal finite-prior A/B summary.  Its only purpose is to
test whether giving the planner the exact private GT world removes the
geometry/candidate/six-action-budget failure observed under the legal finite
32-point prior.  No result here establishes a causal explanation by itself.

episode.  The GT object remains persistent through the renderer.

Oracle leakage
--------------

exactly ``[that case's private GT World]``.  This is deliberate truth leakage
and is recorded as ``ORACLE_LEAKED_TRUTH=true``; no public prior particle is
used.  The private GT is still passed to ``run_episode`` only as its private
render/audit world, but the same object is also exposed as the sole planning
particle for this invalid upper-bound diagnostic.
