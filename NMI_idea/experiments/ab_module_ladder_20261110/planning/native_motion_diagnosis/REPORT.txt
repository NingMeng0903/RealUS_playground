Native motion diagnosis (read-only)
===================================

Scope
-----
This is an offline analysis of the already frozen native traces.  It did not
run the controller, change a QP parameter, change the reference, or modify a
production file.  The 20 trace files contain 6,476 ticks; all QP1 statuses are
``solved``, with zero task-paused ticks and zero failure ticks.

The source fingerprints are:

* native replay results: 102acc248945aeeca6c45cb8c21c62c007fef30e84902263374c4c3344493caf
* replay runner: 861ef2cc4a421154148e587ee06e33818a31c9dc9f20a3e2a69f5c5e2c9b9d9f
* registered native subset protocol: 01b39ab0b557ae850aecd9edcd0993ea3eb4d06c04acd86a6cafd2da5020ce1f

Evidence
--------

Eight segments pass the same-tick 2 mm / 2 degree gate and twelve fail.  The
worst same-tick error is 30.04 mm and 5.00 degrees.  The severe nominal first
segments have zero entry pose error, so their error is not inherited from a
bad starting FK state.  Repeated hold segments begin with the preceding
segment's residual: in the stress context the entry position error increases
from about 0.03 mm to 2.74--3.60 mm across the repeated holds.

The raw command arrays have a frame distinction.  The outer Cartesian tracker
command is in the tool frame, while native ``v_cmd_received`` is stored in the
base frame.  Rotating each saved command by the measured TCP rotation reproduces
the native received command to at most 0.502 mm/s-equivalent across these
traces.  The opposite signs seen when comparing the raw arrays directly are
therefore a frame mismatch, not evidence of a clock race or a sign bug.

The dominant failure is local dynamic feasibility.  In the failed rows:

* the median norm of ``v_cmd_feasible`` divided by the received command falls
  as low as 0.067 (0.075 in the worst nominal continuation);
* the QP equality residual reaches 0.338 m/s and is above 0.01 in the failed
  rows;
* the local qdot boxes are active on roughly 45--100% of ticks, including
  84--94% of ticks on the nominal residual holds; and
* ``task_progress`` falls to zero while the solver still reports ``solved``.

These boxes are local qdot rate/history bounds, not the static URDF velocity
limit.  For example, the saved arm box widths are about 0.015 rad/s, matching
the configured acceleration envelope at a 5 ms tick.  The static IK witness
can therefore exist while the current reference twist is infeasible under the
current qdot history.  The controller returns the closest constrained result,
but does not pause or mark the segment as failed.

The saved robot Jacobian check gives a smaller secondary discrepancy:
``max |J(q) qdot - v_cmd_feasible| = 6.09e-3`` in the 20 traces.  Native's
``v_cmd_feasible`` and ``v_tcp_estimated`` are internally consistent; this
smaller residual may reflect the rail observer/frame convention and should be
resolved before claiming sub-millimetre execution.  It does not account for
the centimetre-scale failures.

Why the hold drifts
-------------------

The next hold starts before the preceding residual has been removed and while
the previous qdot is still carried in the dynamic box.  The hold's correction
twist is then largely infeasible under that local box; its realized feasible
twist can be small or opposite to the requested correction.  The position
error consequently grows during the one-second hold even though QP1 remains
``solved``.  This is a transition/feasibility problem, not a hidden anatomical
target, native clock, or tiny jerk residual.

What is and is not proven
-------------------------

The traces establish that the primary failure is active local dynamic
feasibility plus an execution gate that treats a solved QP as success.  They do
not identify whether the unique implementation defect is the qdot-box
construction, the secondary objective, or the reference retiming.  Static IK
existence does not contradict this finding: it does not include the current
qdot history and rate envelope.

Minimum next diagnostic
----------------------

Use one frozen failing tick and log the box construction terms, task residual,
and the unconstrained/box-constrained task solution.  Then apply the smallest
execution-layer rule: treat low task progress or a residual above the declared
tolerance as infeasible, retime the Cartesian primitive against the active
qdot box, and insert a settle/zero-qdot transition before a hold.  Recheck the
same-tick metric from measured ``q_meas_before`` against the desired pose.  No
AB policy result should be interpreted as native execution success until that
gate is in place.
