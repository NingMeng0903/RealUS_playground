# Probe-generation pilot

This synthetic study tests whether a small offline action library improves a streaming ultrasound scan on fresh simulation seeds. It reuses the atlas, finite posterior, image retention, 10 Hz packet updates, in-block feedback, fitted ProDMP renderer, and complete MPC continuation from the startup and reinspection experiments. There is no new motion fit or policy training in this stage.

## Questions

1. With four nominations from the same feasible probe grid and four paired full continuations per nomination, which proposal rule supplies more usable examples?
2. Once frozen, does any method's library improve deployment with a common public-information rollout selector, including relative to the same selector on the stock library?
3. Does the decision-information term change nominations or held-out outcomes when it is the only changed term?

The direct teacher uses the simulated geometry of the current world, including its shadow and calibration perturbation, without access to future noise. It ranks requests by peak detection propensity in the unresolved query interval on the nominal reference. The decision-information teacher models several explanations consistent with the observed history. For a given first view, it builds a payoff table over feasible nominal follow-up paths and the retained but not yet confirmed query witnesses. A path payoff combines first- and second-block peak visibility as a ranking approximation. A witness payoff is its stored conditional identity probability. Short nominal predictive observation sequences update the posterior while sharing a latent world. The selector compares choosing the follow-up in advance with choosing after a packet. The **precommit** ablation uses the same payoff table with that information increment set to zero. The random control samples uniformly without replacement from the same feasible, deduplicated grid. The four arms have the same allowance of full closed-loop evaluations per candidate. Cheap geometric and packet-scoring costs are logged separately. Historical CEM is outside this matched comparison.

An example is eligible for the frozen library when four paired discovery tapes give it higher average correct completion than the existing MPC and no higher final wrong-identity rate. This is a pilot screening condition. Qualification on independent cases and the held-out comparison are the evidence for reuse. All nominations, including failures, and the nominal and executed first blocks are archived. The library retains at most four distinct relative requests from each source. On held-out cases the public selector ranks the stock menu plus its library with common simulated posterior worlds and one actual first block. The exact held-out scene and future tape are used only in execution and evaluation. The stock online rollout, the current frozen learned B on its stock menu, the stock MPC, and MPC on the union of all frozen libraries are controls. The best fixed first request is selected per task family using only qualification data, with the same adaptive suffix and the union MPC as its feasibility fallback.

## Splits and endpoints

- 48 discovery, 40 qualification and 64 held-out scenes. Each split balances connection and continuous parallel identity, with naturally occurring interference or a prescribed short dropout burst spanning handoff. Independent seed ranges start at 71,000,000, 72,000,000 and 73,000,000. Every policy in a scene starts at the same one- or two-block MPC checkpoint, and the eight test noise tapes are paired by scene and replica.
- Correct completion requires arrival at the scan endpoint and correctly identified retained observations at every required interval. Record new correctly confirmed imagery after handoff, new query confirmations, first-block target-derived detections, new and terminal wrong identities, executed distance, turn, time, frames and feedback corrections. Report the older centering criterion separately. Simulated time excludes planning CPU.
- Use scene-level paired bootstrap intervals. Separate task families and corruption strata. Report feasibility, nominal path aliases, selection overlap, the analysis of the information ablation, and any net cost/travel tradeoff. All tested policies share 67 mm and the original 8 s simulation horizon. The study has no extra 0.10 s or 2 mm rejection threshold.
- Treat this as a generator pilot. A practical advance needs more eligible candidates at an equal verification budget and reuse on held-out scenarios relative to random and the stock selector. A paper-level claim additionally requires frozen, on-policy B-mode comparison against full MPC and the best fixed recheck, agreed error/cost limits, additional layout and motion conditions, and a prospectively checked no-benefit prediction.

The transfer unit is an admissible view and a task payoff conditioned on observations. The candidate evaluator has no dependence on whether the target has a fork. The ultrasound adapter in this experiment instantiates it on a fixed 15-degree knee scan. Held-out seeds use the same two finite scene families and do not demonstrate a new patient, topology, motion condition, or modality.

The decision-region perspective is related to [Javdani et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v33/javdani14.html). Learning a public-information selector from privileged information-gathering rollouts is related to [Choudhury et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07834). Reusing the verified approach or recovery segment on new scenes is related to [SkillMimicGen](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v270/garrett25a.html). Their guarantees do not transfer automatically to this nominal screening score.
