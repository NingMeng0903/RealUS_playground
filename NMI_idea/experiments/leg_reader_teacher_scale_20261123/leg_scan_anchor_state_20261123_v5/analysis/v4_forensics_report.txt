Additional continuation and group audit:
  "Terminal old-pool persistence" means at least one public pre-review
  candidate [frame_index, slot] remains in the terminal old-pool candidate set;
  it is not a truth-correctness claim.
  Each case/arm/tape now stores the exact post-rejoin suffix sequence as
  (station, sector, tick), its full pose+time SHA-256, and a pose-only SHA-256.
  Exact pose+time and pose-only echoes are compared pairwise.  Hold and oracle
  use identical suffix schedules in all selected cases; their physical pose
  schedules therefore echo exactly.  Against MPC, actual pose-only schedules
  echo in 20/22 rescue cases and 23/25 MPC-loss cases; full pose+time echoes
  are zero because review/hold consume a different elapsed tick boundary.

Prescribed-suffix acquisition timing diagnostic, actual tape

The native equal-time hold and active review acquired identical raw centroid
and quality sequences after rejoin in both selected groups. The controlled
replay then used the exact original MPC pose sequence and only changed its
absolute sampling time by the review duration. No extra image was given to
the frozen reader on this delay-only arm. It reproduced all 28 post-prefix
region losses on the same case and region IDs in the selected 25 cases.
Adding the hold history or review history to the same delayed suffix added
zero subsequent region losses or rescues. The selected 22 cases had eight
suffix losses and eight rescues under delay alone relative to MPC, with no
suffix changes from extra hold or review history at the delayed boundary.
This counterfactual covers the archived actual tape on the selected layouts.
