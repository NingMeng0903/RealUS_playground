# Fixed-angle recovery and a public-history teacher

The unit of evaluation is a synthetic leg scene derived from the existing registered artery and vein sections. The shared scanner runs two one-second MPC blocks before the comparison. All arms begin with that same public history, tool state, retained images and remaining resources. Checkpoint selection is scheduled for every scene, including cases where the legacy recovery trigger stays inactive. The evaluator classifies the checkpoint by an active mistaken certificate, target absent from the current packet, target present without a confident association, or normal tracking.

`connection` has two local connection hypotheses. `parallel` uses two continuous unbranched tracks whose relative depth in an accessible interval can help distinguish them. The two tasks use identical controller code. The observation model includes fixed lateral offset, unknown spatial shadow lane and layout, persistent clutter, centroid noise and independent whole-packet outage. The filter uses the same finite family as the generator. A matched suffix uses the same hidden geometry and disturbance tape across all arms. `wrong_prior` deliberately oversamples a rare connection against an atlas prior of 0.975 for its rival. `alias` swaps indistinguishable identities with equal priors. These diagnostic cohorts are separate from the primary two cohorts.

The shared evidence record keeps the legacy first acceptance and the revised active certificate for each interval. For a fixed hidden hypothesis, the candidate association probability stored at an exposure stays conditional on its own packet. As later observations change the joint hypothesis distribution, the controller recomputes that past candidate's marginal identity probability. It revokes a certificate below 0.95 and can replace it only with a fresh accepted image. The evaluator counts historical mistakes, time for which mistaken certificates remained active, new mistaken frames after intervention, repaired bins and final mistakes. Final correct completion requires the endpoint and six retained genuinely correct, centered images. The old strict completion is reported in parallel. The legacy ledger bitmask is kept solely for that comparison.

Each candidate has checked sampled speeds, accelerations, chart bounds and remaining reference TCP path budget. The registered chart plant transmits feedback every 100 ms. New candidates use a quintic path with a bounded interior lateral waypoint. The same causal suffix correction preserves position and velocity for these waypoints and their ProDMP replacements. Legacy candidate types retain their streaming ProDMP reconditioning. The primary budget is eight simulated seconds and 67 mm of reference chart TCP motion; the study measures realized TCP path, cumulative rotation, acquisition count and time rather than adding artificial padding. The setup uses synthetic centroid packets and reference chart kinematics. It does not implement full robot IK, contact or wall-clock plant evolution during planning.

## Offline motion discovery

Use independent training scene seeds. Search one one-second waypoint segment with CEM over relative longitudinal goal, lateral goal, and midpoint displacement. Rank using three search disturbance replicas of a fixed hidden world and the entire common MPC suffix. Reinstate rejected/clipped actions in the archive with the rejection or effective endpoint, and deduplicate nominally equivalent sampled trajectories within 0.05 mm in both axes. Verify the finalists with six fresh paired tapes. The archives record the MPC incumbent, matched-duration wait, selected waveform and a nearby poorer waveform when available. The first verification tape contains nominal paths, feedback revisions, executed paths, public packets and separately marked evaluator evidence.

Fit a small ProDMP model only to decision-time **nominal** lateral motions that obtain more complete scans than the incumbent, complete at least four of six verification trials, and incur no excess new centered mistakes in the comparison. Save separate results for first-block target appearance and fresh accepted evidence. This fit preserves endpoint position and velocity and tests whether the behavior of the nominated motion survives changing the trajectory representation. It does not use realized future feedback as a start-of-segment training label. The CEM result is a finite privileged reference.

## Fresh independent cases

- `mpc_original_menu` runs the original option types on the upgraded shared model and ledger.
- `mpc_shared_menu` also receives the waypoint options. It has the same two open decisions as the teacher, can keep using expanded options at subsequent recovery decisions, and always updates and replans from the actual observations.
- `public_adaptive` scores each shared candidate with six stratified posterior world draws, actual feedback in the simulated segment, and the full shared MPC tail. It executes one selected segment, then recomputes all scores from the new public observations after that segment. A forecast evaluates one explicit intervention and shared MPC continuation at each decision. It is not an exact joint two-step Bayes solution.
- `public_first_only` shares the first choice and all its observations and hands back to MPC immediately. `prodmp_same_first` uses the same request and same one-segment design with the fitted ProDMP reference when feasible, and records refusals.

The scores use predicted final completion minus 1.25 times the probability of a final wrong certificate and small executed time and travel penalties. Every candidate at a decision is evaluated on the same posterior bank. Past certificates are evaluated in virtual trials by marginalizing their archived conditional probabilities. Their evaluator truth labels are unavailable to selection. The next decision sees only the actual new packets, posterior and robot state. Different noise replicas of the same scene do not constitute independent anatomy samples. The primary confidence interval resamples scenes, keeping all arms and repeats of each scene paired.

The primary comparison pools `connection` and `parallel`, equal numbers each. It requires an improvement of at least 5 percentage points with a two-sided 95% paired interval above zero, upper 95% interval endpoints at most +2 points for both final and new mistakes, and complete paired execution cost intervals within ±2 mm, ±2°, ±0.10 s, and ±1 acquisition. Intervals for each family and the failure strata are reported separately. Neither the synthetic timing nor these sample sizes support an online compute or generalization claim across real anatomy. Measured teacher calculation time is logged separately.

The study uses 12 discovery scenes per family for `connection`, `parallel` and `wrong_prior` with six verification tapes, and 28 fresh scenes per family for all four families with three verification tapes. A distinct offset-7000 namespace is for integration checks only. Further policy training is a separate step from the motion fit.

## Follow-up comparison with execution constraints

Use one public intervention, then the common MPC, since the primary experiment identified the first action as the main source of gain. Compare two ways of picking that action from the same twelve posterior rollouts per action. One uses the original score. The other considers only actions forecast to stay within +2 mm, +2 degrees and +0.10 s of the shared MPC incumbent and within +2 points of both kinds of mistake. The MPC always remains admissible. These are constraints on predicted execution, not promises about realized cost. Use a new namespace starting at seed 8,210,000, 28 scenes per primary family and three paired future tapes. Report both the original two-sided realized cost equivalence and the upper interval endpoints against the same margins. An actual saving can satisfy the upper-margin test while failing equivalence. Every family must separately sustain any claim of improvement without added execution cost. Include planning time as a separate recorded outcome.
