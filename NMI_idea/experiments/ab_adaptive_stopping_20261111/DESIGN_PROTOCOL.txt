ADAPTIVE STOPPING B — FROZEN IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT
Astra design, 2026-11-11. Status: protocol, not a result.
DESIGN_PROTOCOL.txt, MID_REVIEW.txt, ASTRA_FINAL.txt and the independent

Implementers own their assigned modules and must preserve other workers' edits.

0. Objective and honest scope

Implement the original B objective: choose each diagnostic action and dwell from
public history, stop probing when a complete executable acquisition option is
sufficiently reliable, otherwise continue probing or explicitly withdraw. No
hard task-time deadline and no primary time penalty. This remains a bounded
local simulation, not a claim about infinite search or clinical ultrasound.

The research target is NOT presumed achieved: on the untouched 128-subject test,
B must improve final correct completion over BOTH the strong A and an actually
completed fixed-pair strategy by at least 10 percentage points. Both paired
subject-bootstrap 97.5% two-sided intervals must have lower bound above zero
(Bonferroni for the two primary comparisons). The common declared task-risk
requirement must also be met in actual committed executions. Wrong completion,
withdrawal, failure and watchdog cases remain in the all-subject denominator.
An earlier good frame followed by a wrong final declaration is NOT primary
success. Report ever-qualified separately. A failure to meet this target is a
negative/incomplete result, not permission to tune on the same test subjects.
The additional goal of purposeful reacquisition is retained: report loss-to-
reacquisition capture count, native elapsed time, failed recovery moves and
simultaneous diagnostic/acquisition use. Speed is a meaningful secondary benefit;
it cannot replace the primary correct-completion requirement. Do not reduce A
to random search or assume that it must recover only by luck.

1. Frozen numerical settings

Train 512 subjects, seed 721501; development/calibration 128, seed 721502;
untouched test 128, seed 721503; observation seed family 721504. Subject identity
is the unit across both mirrored layouts and all policies. Main bootstrap uses
10000 subject-cluster draws, seed 721505. The two mirrors reuse the existing
robot placement and material-patch realization: they are not independent robot
placements. Training sensor samples may reuse fixed native poses; do not run or
claim 512 independent native mechanical experiments.

Four diagnostic locations: the existing side_left, side_right, fork_minus and
fork_plus. For each, dwell is .15, .30 or .60 s: 12 actions. Keep accepted native
SE(3) reference durations and actual measured-frame coordinates; dwell is added,
never substituted for travel/settling. Terminal dwell remains 1.0 s.

Common risk gate: empirical conservative task score >= .90. Sensitivities .80
and .95 are separate and cannot replace the main result. Main time weight is
zero. Existing .04/s is optional labelled secondary sensitivity only.

Operational watchdog: at most 8 diagnostic acquisitions before commitment;
a paired program consumes 2. Watchdog cases are unresolved and not successes.
This is a finite experiment/compute guard, not a physical deadline, resource
claim or learned stopping time. Prespecified sensitivity continues unresolved
cases from their exact state to 12 diagnostic acquisitions, equally for all arms.
If B wins only because competitors were censored at 8, the independent research
claim is not achieved. Report this dependence. All costs remain recorded.

2. SENSOR_V2 is a NEW frozen synthetic observation model

Keep the previous continuous vessel and bounded geometry support:
offset +/-3 mm, slope +/-0.08, curvature +/-0.35/m, depth 18--26 mm.
Keep target quality g_geo(S, actual pose)+[1,tilt/.10]w plus independent quality
noise SD .035*sqrt(.15/dwell); generator w has SD (.065,.23). These are generator
settings, not parameters the fitted model may read instead of estimating.

Every detected candidate, target OR clutter, has identical public field names:
quality, signed center_offset_m, signed axis_angle_rad. Target center is the
signed actual beam-minus-vessel intercept plus Gaussian SD
0.0005*sqrt(.15/dwell) m. Target axis is signed actual tilt-minus-vessel tangent
plus Gaussian SD .03*sqrt(.15/dwell) rad. Missing has no scalar feature; never
replace missing with a zero-centered image. candidate_type is only candidate or
missing. No private association, true geometry, response or target-success label
enters a test policy.

Clutter quality is N(.60,.12^2); center is uniform [-.022,.022] m; axis is uniform
[-.35,.35] rad, mutually independent in this declared stress model. It is a
high-quality spurious candidate channel, not a validated ultrasound renderer.
Keep the old low-quality clutter N(.12,.08^2) as a separate legacy control;
never combine the two generators' results under one old-law claim.

Transient visibility loss is a hidden two-state acquisition-indexed Markov mode.
Initial mode is present. Before each newly acquired packet, present->lost=.03,
lost->present=.70. When present and physically supported, target detection=.80;
otherwise no target packet. After absence of a true packet, clutter probability
is .10; otherwise missing. This models synthetic loss/reacquisition per capture,
not measured tissue motion. Preserve a common random stream per subject and
acquisition/view/visit coupling; retain unique physical acquisition event IDs.

Pure public feasibility check: with a just-confirmed present mode, perfect
geometry/quality and two terminal opportunities, this loss/detection law gives
maximum genuine-detection success .943344. The initially considered .10/.50
loss/recovery law would cap it at .8896 and make a .90 gate impossible even for
an oracle; it was rejected analytically before data. No threshold/layout/test
outcome was used to obtain this choice. It creates a feasible methodological
task, not evidence that these transition probabilities describe patients.

3. Learned response, joint belief and first two equations

Preserve Y=g_geo+Hw+noise and conditional Gaussian Lambda/eta evidence updates.
Learn shared response/noise/detection/loss/clutter parameters from independent
training action outcomes. Training may use annotated target/clutter and training
anatomical geometry; it may NOT use true w directly as the fitted answer.
Fit per-subject w from repeated genuine Y-g_geo and H, estimate noise from
repeated-cell variation, and estimate group mean/covariance with measurement
noise correction. Fit detector/loss transition frequencies and clutter/noisy
feature distributions from annotated training records. Use fixed small PSD
regularization if needed and disclose it. All policies share the fitted model.
At test, freeze it and update only patient/material state. This is statistical
response learning plus planning, not end-to-end policy learning.

Belief: 512 common Sobol particles over S=(offset,slope,curvature,depth), with
the broad prior above. Each S has conditional Gaussian w association components
and two loss-mode weights. Genuine-candidate branches receive Gaussian natural
parameter updates and noisy center/axis likelihoods; clutter branches retain w;
missing uses the correct visibility/association likelihood. Propagate the loss
mode once per NEW acquisition. For masked acoustics propagate mode without
pretending a missing packet was observed. Use log weights and event deduplication.

Bound memory to at most 4 Gaussian components per S and loss mode. If exceeded,
merge the pair with smallest symmetric KL, preserving their total mass and first
two moments; reconstruct natural parameters. Disclose this approximation.
Fixed geometry particles need ESS diagnostics. The primary filter retains their
weights without unregistered resampling/duplication. Development checks against
1024/2048 geometry particles must quantify task-probability/decision sensitivity.
Do not call this an exact full-world posterior or claim memory-performance gain
from merely matching the replay of its own admitted events.

4. Two distinct stopping decisions and the complete execution option

Stop probing/commit only through the shared .90 task-score gate described below.
This predicts future complete acquisition. It is different from declaring the
task completed after a NEW observed packet meets a public verification rule.

The bounded complete option is: choose left or right, follow its actual checked
route, acquire a fresh terminal packet, and if public verification rejects it,
retry once at the same window. At the first public acceptance, stop immediately;
after two rejections, end as failed/no verified completion. All methods have
exactly these same two options, retries and public verifier. No extra unseen
retries may be executed outside the option whose probability was predicted.

Public verification requires detected AND quality>=.58 AND absolute noisy
center<=.0045 m AND absolute noisy axis<=.22 rad. Private final correctness also
requires genuine target identity and TRUE center<=.0045 m and TRUE axis<=.22,
with the quality from that SAME fresh packet. A wrong earlier public acceptance
terminates as final failure; a later hypothetical good packet cannot repair it.
This is detected-feature acceptance, not raster-image or visible-length grading.

DUAL-PURPOSE AMENDMENT BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION/TEST: apply this SAME public and
private verification to EVERY newly acquired packet, including one labelled
diagnostic/probe. A genuine qualifying probe may immediately complete the task;
an incorrect public acceptance immediately fails it. No role label suppresses
the acquisition benefit. A privately qualifying but publicly rejected frame is
ever-qualified only until a correct final declaration actually occurs. The
diagnostic risk/stop decision and new-packet task-completion test are distinct.
After two rejected terminal packets this bounded experiment ends uncompleted;
do not silently return to probing while still predicting only two attempts.
This shared finite execution-option limit is disclosed and is not a claim that
unrestricted recovery could never succeed. Diagnostic-stage recovery from target
loss remains available until the common guard.

Compute raw_complete_p for the whole option, not only quality and not the
independent-marginal formula 1-product(1-p). Integrate the SAME S and w across
both attempts. Per S/w/mode let s be probability of truly correct public
acceptance, f probability of wrong public acceptance (including a geometrically
wrong target passing noisy gates and clutter), c=1-s-f. With mode transition P,
let p1=the current mode distribution times P. Complete success is
  p1 dot s + ((p1*c) P) dot s.
Integrate that expression over S, association components and the projected
Gaussian H w. Normal CDFs handle quality/feature gates, and the clutter law is
explicit. PRE-RESULT NUMERICAL CORRECTION: for identical pose/dwell the option
probabilities are quadratic in Q(w), the conditional noisy-quality pass
probability. Use the equivalent exact moments E Q=Phi(z) and
E Q^2=Phi(z)-2*T(z,sqrt(R/(2v+R))), where z=(g+H mean-threshold)/sqrt(v+R),
v=H covariance H^T, R is quality-noise variance, and T is Owen's T function.
The same first/second moments integrate both correct and early-false stops.
This replaces integration only; it does not reduce 512 particles, association
components or public-outcome nodes. GH33/GH65 remain comparison diagnostics,
not assumed-converged truth: audit_exact_option.py independently checks the
identity against adaptive integration and exhibits their possible tail error.
For unequal attempts use E Q1, E Q2 and E(Q1 Q2) under the correct joint Gaussian
projection/noise covariance, or independently converged integration. Do not
force correlation one or round differing poses into an unreported equality.
Propagate the HMM once before each fresh attempt; a one-attempt option has no
retry term. Native actual/predicted poses and validity remain separately logged.
Recompute calibration after the final corrected implementation is frozen.

Shared calibration is monotone Platt:
  p_cal=sigmoid(a*logit(clip(raw_p,1e-5,1-1e-5))+b), a>=0.
Fit log loss on independent calibration subjects' actual complete-option
correct/incorrect outcomes, weighting each subject equally across its records.
Use common fixed exploration prefixes of lengths 0,1,2,4,6 and both terminal
options for calibration coverage; record how their poses were generated.
The gate score is min(raw_p,p_cal). This is an empirical conservative score,
NOT a distribution-free confidence bound. Report held-out calibration and
uncertainty; do not evade poor calibration by making a stronger claim.
No low-sample statistical gate is to be invented that trivially rejects everyone.
Conversely, do not lower .90 after seeing a low acceptance rate.

5. Finite adaptive policy implementations

At every new diagnostic packet update belief, re-evaluate complete options and
actual state. If an option can be checked and its common score>=.90, select the
highest-scoring such option and execute it. This common rule prevents B from
winning merely by applying a different refusal standard. If none qualifies:

A_info: choose the next single diagnostic maximizing predicted information gain
about the four latent terminal-viability classes (neither/left/right/both).
The class uses true geometric and noiseless-quality viability under posterior
hypotheses, not hidden test truth. A sees full common belief and all 12 actions,
can repeat, change windows, and use the same physical recovery. It is not forced
to stop when initially uncertain. Ties use the common deterministic rule.

A_task: choose the next diagnostic maximizing the probability of immediate
correct public completion from THAT fresh packet; break ties by the same task-
relevant information gain, then the common deterministic rule. This is the local
task-response greedy baseline. Select the stronger A_task/A_info on development
under the common risk standard and freeze it as A_full for test. Retain both
development results. Do not call an entropy-only baseline that ignores the
current image's task value the only strong A.

B_H1: maximize immediate correct public acceptance probability PLUS expected
best releasable complete-option score on PUBLICLY REJECTED packets only. False
public acceptance has value zero and is absorbing. Never count immediate success
and continue that same branch. On rejected packets a branch without a releasable
option has zero immediate completion value. Break these truncated-horizon ties
by expected maximum raw complete-option probability (a labelled potential
heuristic), then by the same deterministic rule; do not mistake this heuristic
for achieved coverage. Replan after executing exactly one diagnostic.

B_H2: the same public-observation recursion with up to two future single
diagnostics before the complete option. First layer evaluates all 12 actions;
second layer retains the top 2 ranked by H1 value then raw-potential score.
Execute only the first action and replan. This is bounded belief lookahead,
not an exhaustive optimum or a precommitted pair.

Complete_pair: fixed +/-fork order selected from public native worst-case
completion cost. Dwell is selected once on development from the same three
choices. Execute both members of every pair and update after each packet;
after the complete pair apply the common commit gate. If needed, repeat complete
pairs up to the common guard. No task-time cutoff may prevent its completion.
If the first packet already passes the common public completion verifier, the
pair may immediately finish just like every other arm. Otherwise it completes
the pair before deciding to commit. Finishing the task early is distinct from
stopping diagnostic probing early.

All methods can explicitly withdraw if no lawful motion remains. Lack of a
finite-library witness is recorded as unknown, not global impossibility.
When progress scores are numerically tied, use higher complete-task score, then
fewer additional acquisitions, then shorter dwell ONLY if task predictions are
equivalent at the declared numerical tolerance, then canonical action name.
Do not add a time penalty to force varied durations. With the frozen noise law
longer dwell can dominate; selecting .60 everywhere is an admissible result.

Development selection: compare B_H1/B_H2 and separately A_task/A_info under the
same risk standard,
correct completion first, then fewer diagnostic actions. Tie chooses H1.
Select pair dwell the same way. Freeze the resulting B and pair configuration
before test; retain all development scores. If only H1 is completed, label that
limited scope and do not claim the full B search question has been resolved.

Predictive-observation budget: common Sobol/CRN 16 outcomes/action initially;
re-evaluate leading first-layer actions at 32, then 64 if ordering changes or
value changes by >.01; up to 256 if still unstable. H2 secondary layer starts
at 8 and refines selected candidates at 16/32. Report unresolved differences,
actual outcome counts, beam effects, wall cost and node sensitivity on development.
Do not revive the previous unvalidated 5/3 GH expected-max calculation. The
smooth complete-option integration check is separate from this nonsmooth max decision.
Predictive nodes provide immediate correct/incorrect acceptance MASS for value
integration, not hidden association labels to posterior updates or action choice.
Publicly identical rejected packets update the same belief. Adding dual-purpose
early acceptance only in the runner while predicting exclusively later terminal
images is invalid.

After selecting B on development, report I(use diagnostic packet)/F(predict
actual post-probe route state) 2x2 with identical risk gate/options. Mask only
acoustic payload for I0; actual state/time and capture occurrence remain public.
F0 substitutes the same nominal state for first-stage future feasibility only;
all actual continuations remain checked. No-time reversible layouts may give
zero F effect because every terminal remains reachable. Retain that result;
the old baffle's detour alone cannot justify non-time terminal impossibility.

Matched observation burden: report common diagnostic-cap curves 2/4/6/8 and
the predeclared guard-12 continuation. Also use the first 32 test subject IDs,
chosen before outcomes, for exact-count shadow comparisons at 2 and 4 diagnostic
acquisitions: continue each diagnostic selector to that count, then apply the
same complete-option gate/executor. These are separate controlled trajectories,
not unexecuted counterfactual probabilities. Report their native cost and do not
treat the subset as the primary 128-subject significance result. If execution
budget prevents these controls, attribution remains incomplete; do not declare
the independent core benefit solely from more observations in the main policy.

Required controls: uninformative diagnostic packets; all diagnostic packets
missing; response reset with reset-aware and unaware models; legacy low-quality
clutter; open reversible mechanics. Keep higher-quality clutter and lost-mode
episodes in primary outcomes. Include full learned-vs-generator model diagnostics
on development; do not train one arm with additional private data.

Calibration/selection uses development only. Test once with the frozen model,
verifier, geometry, paths, selected B and pair dwell. Record all-subject final
correct completion, wrong completion, no completion, withdrawal, watchdog,
commit coverage, committed failure rate, ever-qualified, diagnostic/terminal
counts, planning/motion/acquisition time, native routes/cache hits, predictive
calibration, misses/clutter/loss/reacquisition and physical failures. Audit wrong
declarations for ALL completed packets, including diagnostic ones. Define loss
episodes from the frozen private visibility-mode trace for evaluation, and also
report public detection-loss episodes. Measure acquisitions and native time to
the next genuine target packet and to final correct completion. Unrecovered
episodes remain censored/noncompleted; do not omit them. Private mode labels
are evaluator-only. The Markov law is acquisition-indexed: a recovery-time
result does not establish a physical time-dependent loss model.

If B meets the numerical primary effect target but violates the shared risk
requirement, wins only through more samples, uses an uncompleted fixed pair,
depends on unstable integration, or relies on an unsupported physical barrier,
the requested independent research goal is NOT achieved. Negative results may
motivate a NEW version with a newly held-out test, never outcome-driven changes
to this test. Finite refusal/guard effects are not evidence against all possible
B policies, nor proof of eventual superiority in a reversible zero-cost world.

Theoretical interpretation remains the existing three relations: local response,
material evidence, and public-history adaptive stopping with a checked complete
continuation. No claim that stopping/options, Bayes filtering, or generic belief
planning itself is new. ASTRA_FINAL.txt will be written only after independent
implementation and result review.

SCOPE NOTE — PURPOSEFUL REACQUISITION (interpretive only; no setting changed)

The frozen lost->present transition is acquisition-indexed and independent of
action. For action a, genuine detection probability is the posterior expectation
of .80 * P(next mode=present | current mode) * I(geometry has support at a).
If every candidate has support for every relevant geometry hypothesis, all
actions have the same per-acquisition probability of genuine redetection. A
shorter route or dwell may reduce elapsed time, and choosing a better view may
improve qualifying-task completion, but neither proves that the selected
direction caused the target to become visible again. The .70 mode recovery
itself cannot provide that evidence.

Where support varies with action, the existing geometric term can affect
redetection; report this separately from mode recovery. To test purposeful
directional reacquisition as a mechanism requires independently justified
action-dependent FoV, acoustic-window, occlusion or contact visibility, with
different actions compared from the same actual lost state against a strong A.
Such an extension is not part of this run and must not be introduced or tuned
to obtain a B win. This run can test post-loss inference, confidence, routing,
qualifying acquisition and measured recovery burden within its stated law.
