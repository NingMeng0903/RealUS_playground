Why RESULT has no fifth event
-----------------------------
MotionRollouts materialized both candidates, but neither produced a valid
Transfer.  Its documented aggregation then returns ``reason=no_checked_route``
with ``route_id=null``.  NativeAdapter records that as the infeasible fifth
check; checkpoint ``acbc475b5405d0156af7c8f0.json`` confirms this exact
``no_checked_route`` result.  This is the expected consequence of both native
route attempts failing the 2-mm tracking gate, not an adapter inability to
find the route.  The adapter does not currently expose the rejected candidate
rows in the final event, but that reporting limitation did not cause the
invalid result.

Classification
--------------
This is a physical/native controller tracking-gate failure on the final
waypoint of both available routes (position > 2 mm).  It is not a 2-deg
rotation failure, public geometry/window failure, wall/collision failure,
pause/exit failure, jerk policy failure, or state-continuation failure.  No
adapter/source fix is indicated by this audit; no code was changed.

Other-arm / same-route observation

The exact full route string above occurs only in this target planning manifest;
no other arm has the same complete prefix and route.  The same rig has the

round_tip fails at 3.147 mm or 3.432 mm while retreat_fork succeeds.  Other

in ``A_info`` fixture_01, round_tip fails at 2.835--3.344 mm while the paired
retreat_fork routes pass.  These are different complete states/fixtures, not
independent repeats of the exact target route, and are reported only as
context.

Conclusion for the pilot
------------------------
The first ``native_probe_invalid`` is explained by two real, deterministic
native tracking failures at the 2-mm position gate.  Do not reinterpret it as
geometry/collision or as an adapter cache bug; retain the failed route rows
and state history for downstream accounting.

Gate interpretation
--------------------
The frozen native ``motion_tracking_pass_2mm_2deg`` test is a full-trace max
test over actual-vs-desired position/rotation (plus its native limit checks),
not a 10--90% middle-only test.  The fixture's aligned before-tick check is
also computed as a full-trace max.  Therefore ``round_tip`` is rejected by a
large middle-segment position excursion in both views.  ``retreat_fork`` is
rejected by the full native actual peak at the early transition and also by
the before-tick aligned peak, which remains just over 2 mm in the 10--90%
window.  Neither route is rejected by the tail/dwell: both settle to
sub-millimetre (indeed micron-scale) position error and sub-degree rotation.
The retained evidence therefore does not support calling this an end-of-trace
artifact; only the native actual component of the retreat-fork failure is
predominantly an early transient, while the adapter before-tick gate still
shows a middle-window overrun.
