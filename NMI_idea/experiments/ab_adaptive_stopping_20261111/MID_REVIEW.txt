1. Exact acceleration, independently checked

For two identical-pose/dwell acquisitions, conditional on one geometry/response
component let Q be the noisy-quality tail probability, and let the shared
response projection have variance v and independent image noise variance R.
With z=(geometric quality + projected mean - threshold)/sqrt(v+R):
  mu=E Q=Phi(z)
  nu=E Q^2=Phi(z)-2*OwenT(z,sqrt(R/(2*v+R))).
Correct-stop, false-stop and reject probabilities at each mode have the forms
s=a*Q, f=b+(u-a)*Q, c=1-b-u*Q. Thus the shared-latent two-attempt HMM expression
p1.s+((p1*c)P).s is a quadratic polynomial in Q and is integrated exactly by
mu and nu. False-stop probability has the same property. Use one transition
before the first acquisition and a second only when a retry is actually allowed.
An efficient implementation can evaluate current mode-conditioned mixture
components directly with mode-path weights, avoiding a predictive merge.

The saved standalone audit_exact_option.py imports no experimental outcome or
production model. It checks 200 seed-721506 stress inputs against independent
adaptive Gaussian integration. Saved audit_exact_option.json contains every
input/error, environment versions, source SHA and maxima:
  E Q^2 maximum absolute error: 2.9683199463192256e-14
  full correct-stop probability: 7.51076595775882e-16
  full false-stop probability: 3.3792413312028202e-15
  GH33 comparison error: 0.05623825170236668
  GH65 comparison error: 0.05069447633658819.
The integration domain is [-12,12] standard deviations, with a split at the
quality transition and 1e-11 absolute/relative quadrature tolerances. These are
numerical-identity checks, not empirical ultrasound or policy performance.
GH33/65 are not converged reference truth over this stress range. Production
must agree with the analytic/adaptive reference, not preserve GH's errors.

For unequal actions or sensor variances, use the correct joint Gaussian mixed
moment E(Q1 Q2); a shared scalar node incorrectly forces correlation one. Do
not equate merely close actual poses without reporting the approximation.
This optimization preserves the model and all 512 geometry particles and QMC
outcome counts. The DESIGN_PROTOCOL numerical paragraph is amended before any
formal result. Final production code must be frozen before recalibration.
