# Streaming recovery training on a registered leg chart

This experiment uses the registered Among_US artery and vein sections and the existing endpoint-conditioned ProDMP. The primary plant is an ideal material-chart simulation. Anonymous synthetic centroids are generated at the realized sampled pose. Geometry, persistent clutter and visibility generate the packets. Probe-action names have no role in packet generation.

## Stages and splits

1. Collect complete one-second visual-feedback demonstrations at the reference angle. Apply bounded training-only lateral goal perturbations. During the scan, save the actual first eligible checkpoint. From it execute each feasible recovery segment. Fit the lateral integrated ProDMP's endpoint-nullspace weights on locally successful demonstrations, with separate shape fits for advance, hold, lateral recheck and revisit. Preserve the existing primitive implementation and compare old and new weights with the same planner and feedback.
2. Freeze those shape fits. In separate training scenes, branch each first eligible public checkpoint into the common set of available actions. Use the same initial state, angle and stochastic tapes for the alternatives. Each option streams observations along its actual trajectory. After one decision block, return to the common feedback and replanning controller. Use completed continuation, wrong acceptance and local recovery as outcome labels for each candidate. Fit a small action-value model to public features. Select a fixed-action comparator on independent development scenes.
3. Compare live MPC, the fixed first action and learned B on all held-out cases. The three use the same learned weights, shared prefix, constraints, candidate menu, observation/association model and live MPC continuation. B changes the action at the first eligible checkpoint. A scene with no checkpoint uses the common A outcome. The preset split bases are documented in the summary. Eligibility is determined before looking at branch outcomes.
4. Reuse the held-out anatomy and noise realizations in both signs of a measured-angle trajectory. The angle starts at the reference at episode entry. It develops through the common prefix and through the options at the same physical time, so checkpoints remain paired within a schedule and can differ across schedules. The learned motion policy and action scorer were trained at one angle. The belief updates online under measured angles, using the response hypothesis prior. Run a separate known alias condition with exchanged labels and invariant anonymous observation likelihood.

## Readout

Strict completion requires arrival and a verified fresh target centroid within the centering tolerance in every required leg interval. A wrong accepted identity invalidates completion. Report the all-case denominator and the subset eligible for B. Compare the position posterior, error and empirical 90% interval coverage at the unresolved interval's midpoint and the pre-action angle. Score which track carries the target separately at the downstream station 28 mm. Both addresses and the query angle are fixed between the pre- and post-action measurements. Interpret covariance reduction jointly with target error, empirical coverage and wrong acceptances.

Causal acquisition proceeds at 10 Hz and chart motion at 50 Hz. Time runs during holding and reverse motion. Angle, visibility and observation noise are indexed by physical time. All arms share the same duration and travel caps. Report actual incumbent/learned differences in executed reference-pose Cartesian path length, sampled turn, acquisition count and simulated episode duration, including probes and failures. Use paired case bootstrap intervals. Equivalence margins are 2 mm, 2 degrees, 0.10 s and one acquisition. A completion-rate gain with greater cost is a tradeoff. The held-out shape and moving schedules are simulation stress tests. Where included, sampled RM75 inverse kinematics is an offline witness of saved reference-pose paths.

A new path, tighter posterior, best label-informed branch, or more local evidence does not by itself demonstrate a gain in correct completion. The primary B comparison is correct completion against live MPC and the frozen fixed-action comparator at comparable cost. Document the direction and uncertainty of the separate flexion and extension comparisons. Report all observed effects.
