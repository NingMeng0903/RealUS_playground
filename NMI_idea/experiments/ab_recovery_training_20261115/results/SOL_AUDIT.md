# SOL independent recovery-study audit

The audit used the formal `recovery-training-result` archives. It reconstructed 3,080 arm-case records, 825,930 chart samples, 167,650 exposure records, and 1,341 before/after acquisition readouts. Shared traces count once per arm-case record. The raw aggregation matched 1,630 fields in `summary.json` within (3.38\times10^{-14}).

## Splits and execution

The raw seed namespaces contain 192 motion episodes from 700000, 320 branch-training episodes from 800000, 96 development episodes from 900000, 192 regular test seeds from 1600000, and 40 alias seeds from 1800000. The branch and development sets contain 235 and 66 eligible checkpoints, with six actions per checkpoint. All 192 regular test seeds reproduced identical anatomy, offset, calibration bias, knee-response coefficients, and clearance/noise tapes across the three schedules. Each schedule starts from the same chart state and packet. The three B-comparison arms share the exact recovery checkpoint within each schedule. Recovery checkpoints across schedules can differ. Fixed and flexion have one trigger disagreement and four trigger-frame differences among shared triggers. Fixed and extension have no trigger disagreement and three trigger-frame differences.

From the saved executed one-second blocks I recovered the fitted coefficients exactly. The retained segments were 602 advance, 60 wait, 58 left, 63 right, 74 short, and 87 revisit. The five recovery counts also count distinct motion seeds. The maximum endpoint-constraint error of the fitted example weights was (9.33\times10^{-16}). Development selected wait among all six fixed options. The development choice that knew the first tape's outcomes gained 7.58 percentage points on that tape and changed completion by −1.52 points on the resampled suffix, with a paired 95% interval of −9.09 to +6.06 points and +0.424 s mean duration.

The sampled chart reference uses 20 ms motion and 100 ms exposures. Peak longitudinal and lateral speeds were 14.893 and 17.995 mm/s. Peak sampled accelerations were 65.061 and 99.996 mm/s². The paired chart path costs reproduced within (1.42\times10^{-14}) mm and (8.35\times10^{-14}) degrees. The archived maximum handoff residual was (1.78\times10^{-15}). These costs use the reference-pose Cartesian chart map. A separate kinematic lift can evaluate the sampled robot path.

## Association and covariance

An independent calculation of 1,200 likelihood examples covered zero through three returns and rectangular FOV censoring. The maximum likelihood discrepancy was (1.31\times10^{-18}). A single source at the FOV edge with pre-noise detection probability .8 had missing probability .6. An 81-pose moving-angle alias check returned bitwise identical anonymous packets under swaps of the true target/distractor roles. Pooled structural probability stayed within (1.67\times10^{-16}) of .5 and target-slot probabilities stayed at or below .5 to rounding. All 40 formal alias cases showed the same lack of identity certification.

Full joint-posterior replay over the three comparison arms for seeds 1600007 (fixed), 1600013 (flexion and extension), and 1800000 (alias) reproduced the saved packets bitwise. Maximum discrepancies were (6.67\times10^{-16}) for association, (5.56\times10^{-17}) for checkpoint weights, and (2.14\times10^{-14}) mm² for covariance entries. The within/between structural decomposition matched total covariance to (5.33\times10^{-14}) mm². In the alias replay, MPC reduced the within-structure trace from .02292 to .000247 mm², with the between-structure trace at 56.25279 mm² before and after. Both identity hypotheses retained equal weight.

The nominal 90% coordinate intervals undercovered in the regular triggered groups. At fixed angle, both marginals had width at most (10^{-12}) mm after the first action in 99/136 MPC, 95/136 fixed, and 93/136 B cases. Average marginal coverage was 9.2%, 11.4%, and 11.8%. B coverage was 18.1% in flexion and 14.0% in extension. Coverage here averages the lateral and depth indicators at the fixed local query. The public model uses finite offset atoms and independent per-view emissions, while the generator includes continuous offsets and temporally correlated dropout.

The final causal rerun used seed 1600007. Permuting evaluator roles and translating evaluator target positions changed the score while leaving all decisions and 401 motion samples bitwise equal. Resampling the hidden suffix after exposure 21 left the available observations and path through that exposure unchanged.

## Reproduction from the archives

- Read `test_fixed.jsonl.gz`, `test_flexion.jsonl.gz`, `test_extension.jsonl.gz`, and `alias_fixed.jsonl.gz` with Python `gzip.open(path, "rt")` and `json.loads`. For a triggered arm concatenate the prefix packets with suffix packets at `packet_offset`. Concatenate prefix motion before `motion_offset` with suffix motion, whose first sample is the shared checkpoint. Suffix decisions, accepts, and block bounds retain global indices. For a non-triggered arm use the shared `a_new_trace`.
- The columns of `motion_t_s_x_vs_vx_q` are time, station, lateral position, station velocity, lateral velocity, and body angle. At sample (i), check (t=.02i), and match exposure (f) to sample (5f). With linearly interpolated section origins (o(s)) and Slerp section rotations (R(s)), use (p_i=o(s_i)+x_i R(s_i)[:,1]) for TCP travel. Sum successive Euclidean distances and the rotation angles of (R_i^{-1}R_{i+1}). Take first centered claims in bins with edges ([2,7,12,17,22,27,31.4]) and apply the role/centering test above. Recompute paired percentile intervals by resampling entire seed rows 4,000 times with NumPy seed 84122.
- Read `motion_demonstrations.jsonl.gz` by block, with the same global offsets. Keep full 51-sample blocks containing a clean fresh target return and no wrong confident slot. Fit each basis residual from actual lateral position and velocity using the endpoint nullspace. For regression, let (X=[1,(x_{end}-x_0)/5,v_0/6]). Compute ((X^\top X+.008n I)^{-1}X^\top Y) within each skill group and compare with `prodmp_weights.npz`.
- Reconstruct the packet weight by summing injective assignments to target, distractor, and clutter. For a source with detection probability (d), use (d f(y)) for an assigned centroid and (1-d\int_{\mathrm{FOV}} f(y)\,dy) for a missing source. Use normalized posterior weights (p_i) to compute (\mu=\sum p_i x_i) and (\Sigma=\sum p_i(x_i-\mu)(x_i-\mu)^\top). Query before/after at the same archived station midpoint and pre-action angle for position, and at 28 mm for identity. The intervals are the weighted .05 and .95 coordinate quantiles. `pre` and `post` retain the moments and intervals.
