                                                                                converges to the real system (the real robot with the auxiliary

                                                                                than a compensator that estimates the extended disturbance

                                                                                   Finally, the change in the control structure according to the
                                                                                α value indicates that the proposed method (α modulation)
                                                                                can be considered continuous switching between admittance
                                                                                control and impedance control. As mentioned in [21] and [22],
                                                                                impedance control and admittance control are complementary
                                                                                to each other. Combining the two control strategies can be an
                                                                                effective solution for physical interaction of the robot. Unlike
                                                                                existing methods that have defined independent impedance and
                                                                                admittance controllers and combined them [21], [22], [23], [24],
                                                                                the proposed method unifies the two control schemes within a
                                                                                single control structure and provides the parameter adaptation
                                                                                rule based on the passivity analysis of the control structure.
                                                                                Therefore, the proposed method can be considered an extension
                                                                                of the hybrid (unified) impedance and admittance control, which
                                                                                can ensure stable interactions with high accuracy.
