What the uploaded V8.5.2 evidence actually supports
----------------------------------------------------
- Cartesian-lag geometric simulation: 5,440 complete-chain interventions
  (two main runs plus matched-budget supplements), but only 96 main and 40
  supplement configuration cases—not 5,440 independent objects/patients.
  There is no real B-mode, probe-contact, force, or hardware result.
- A (curve prediction): for rho=+0.8, the combined KEY_METRICS values are
  max population-curve errors 2.22 percentage points (basic), 7.45 pp
  (variable), and 15.03 pp (stress). The fixed-60-degree balanced supplement
  can reach roughly 41 pp maximum error. Full-history and structural models
  are close; a stable data-efficiency advantage is not established.
- B (release timing): early versus 8-s-late old evidence has peak completion
  differences of about 7.19 pp (basic), 11.25 pp (variable), and 4.45 pp
  (stress), only at selected budgets and not a pre-registered/significance
  result. The stress effect is unstable.
- C (interaction cost): at the same minimum population target (>=0.8), the
  basic/variable early-vs-hidden costs are nearly equal; the stress condition
  has no saving. The attained completion rates overshoot and differ, so these
  are not equal-completion cost comparisons.
- Genesis joint execution is free-space position-PD validation: 26/32
  candidates completed, 6 were rejected before execution, and all eight
  public conditions had a candidate. It is not a contact/B-mode/real-robot
  validation. The full-leg v2 zero-completion result is limited to a finite
  virtual-registration/candidate grid and is not a global reachability result.
- Joint entry-conditioned v3 has only two independent test objects and six
  continuation chains. It predicted completion at T=20 for all cases but
  measured 0.5; both measured cases completed by T>=40. All conditions chose
  the same `fixed_plus` tree and the q-command traces were identical across
  information conditions, so it shows no observed B/C information benefit.
- The option-tree `1/2` versus `1` table and finite-limit parity results are
  mathematical toy-model checks, not robot experiments. They cannot be
  counted as physical evidence of a 0-to-1 capability transition.

Consequences for the strong claim
----------------------------------
The branch does not support claims that the method reliably predicts a real
completion window, causes a dIRD/harmonic-coordinate benefit, beats a strong
full-history baseline in data efficiency, or transfers to multi-organ graph
nodes. In particular, the package records the main m+/m- quantities as
geometric reference/path parameters rather than measured dIRD joint modes;
there is no controlled pair of equally image-qualified dIRD implementations
with crossed downstream outcomes. Thus the necessary “old evidence selects a
different legal continuation and changes the guaranteed task set” condition
has not been executed. The V8.5.3 claims in the pasted conversation (31-page
manuscript/bundle and proposed four-cell/graph protocol) are not auditable
from this remote ref; they should be treated as design/theory until the
corresponding commit and verification outputs are pushed.
